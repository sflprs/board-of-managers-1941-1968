Hospital - Mrs. Miller in the absence of the Chairman, Mrs, Bowes,
stated that there was nothing further to report.

Dining Room - Mrs. Landis stated that the Dining Room Committee has
decided to delay the purchase of mats for the dining room until we
are in the new building. °

Family - Mrs. Krick, Chairman, reported that she had drafted the
Tetter to the Family as authorized by the Board asking $71.00 for
room and board. The Family is fine and enthusiastic about the new
building. ‘

Visiting - Mrs. Griffith reported that everyone is glad to know
about the room assignments.

Occupational Activities - Mrs. Byrne, Chairman, reported an amazing
amount of articles have been made and have been stored away. The
Board decided that they should be displayed in cases at the opening
of the Home. The chairs chosen for the Hobby Room by Mrs. Byrne and
Mrs. Bruck have been ordered. The Finance Committee was asked to
consider the cost of using a sound deadening material in the ceiling.

Entertainment - Mrs. Krick stated that she had enjoyed seeing one of
the movies supplied by the San Francisco Department of Adult Education
shown each Friday at the Home. . .

Rooms - Mrs. Blackie reported on the plan of decoration for the par-
Tors on the second, third and fourth floors, The necessary expend-
itures for the decoration of the Garden Room will amount to $963.92.
Some of the furniture stored at Laguna Street found unusable for the
building was given to St. Vincent de Paul. The Board approved the
suggestion of the Rooms and Furnishings Committee that a budget be
worked out for additional confortable beds and other pieces needed to
furnish the rooms that will be available to monthly guests. Miss
Bridge moved that the Rooms Committee be authorized to look over the
available furniture for the 1) new rooms and submit a report to the
Finance Committee, with the understanding that the Finance Committee
be permitted to act on it. This was seconded by Mrs.-Stephensen and
voted by the Board.

Room Assignment - Mrs. Porter stated that rentals for monthly guests
in the new building go from $170 to $300 a month. At present 27
monthly guests have been placed, with rentals totaling $5,940. This
leaves 1) rooms available for monthly guests with rentals totaling
$3,700 a month.

Garden - Mrs. Hilbert, Chairman, reported that Mrs. Landis and she

had checked with Podesta on the cost and materials for the planters

in the dining room. The circle in the center court will be filled in.
The architect is making plans for the potting shed. The Board author-
ized the Garden Committee to remove the two dead Plane Trees on the
sidewalk on Bay Street and the two Plane Trees next to them and to fill
in this area with concrete. It was suggested that Mrs. Hilbert consult
with Mr. Hindes about the possibility of getting compensation for the
tree by the cottage, which has died because of the building activities.

Budget - No report

Old Business - Mrs. Perry asked everyone to send a list of names for the Home to
Hrs. Stephensen. These names will be discussed at the October meeting.

New Business
I. Library - Mrs. Perry asked the Committee to check the books at Pine Street

to see which ones should go to Laguna Street.
