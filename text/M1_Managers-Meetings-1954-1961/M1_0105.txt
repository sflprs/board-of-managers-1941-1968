token payment reducing the balance to $1,073.90.

Building - Mrs.’ Porter reported that the utility room on the third floor in
the old building would be remodeled in accordance with the revised plan of the

A letter was read from the architect, Mr. Perry, stating that some sort of
regular inspection is necessary for the heating and mechanical equipment’
at 300 Laguna Street. For daily maintenance a retired Navy man with a
partial knowledge of mechanics might serve as an engineer and live in the
