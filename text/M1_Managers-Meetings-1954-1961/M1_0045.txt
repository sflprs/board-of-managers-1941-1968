Home Furnishings: Mrs. Towne reported having a chair re-upholstered
for the Nurses' Home at a cost of $56.00. A cotton rug was also
bought for the Nurses! Home and three bath-room rugs. A chair for
Miss Betke's desk was also purchased.

It was moved by Mrs. Landis, seconded by Mrs. Hilbert and
carried that the Home Furnishings Committee be allowed to take
advantage of sales and auctions up to $2000.00 towards furnishing
the new home,

Books: Two donations were received during the month.

Entertainment Committee: Miss Zane spoke of the invitation ex-
tended the Family and Board by Mrs. Bruck for tea at her home in
Ross on Wednesday, September 7th at 3 o'clock.

House Activities: Mrs. Byrne announced the date of the Bazaar
as November 5th. :

Home Merger: Mr. Hindes sent word to the Board that the terms of
the merger have now been sent to Sacramento and that in about twenty
(20) days the final vote will be taken. Financial audits of both
Homes have been completed satisfactorily.

Mrs. Craig spoke of the untold hours of work done by
Mr. Perry on the plans. The new building plans are almost completed.
The Dohrmann Supply Company is working on plans for the kitchen.
University of California Hospital Suppliers are working on the plans
for the infirmary.

Superintendent's Report: Miss Ash gave her report. August was a

quiet month and the Family remained quite well. Miss Walter was away
for three weeks. Two visitors from the Marion Osborne Home in Rye, N.Y.
visited the Home during the month. Miss Moore returns on Sept. 11th.
and Miss ash leaves on her vacation on September ith.

Mrs. Perry read excerpts from a survey made for another home
in San Francisco.

There being no further business the meeting adjourned.
