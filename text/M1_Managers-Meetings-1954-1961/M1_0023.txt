April 5, 1955

The regular monthly meeting of the Ladies Protection and
Relief Society was held on April 5th, at the Home.

Presiding - Mrs. Bowes.
Present - iiesdames Bowes, Bruck, Byrne, Hilbert, Lyon, Krick,
Towne and the Misses Rolph and Zane.

The minutes of the previous regular meeting and the
mid-month meeting were read and approved.

Family Report: . Mrs. Krick reported that Mrs. Kingsbury and
Mrs. Power are away on visits. Miss Knowlton has had influenza
but is recovering very well. All other members of the family
are very well.

Convalescent Report: Mrs. Bowes reported that one convalescent
expressed the wish there were colored pictures of the Home for
sale. It was reported that there are such pictures. The Depart-
ment is full. Fourteen were admitted in March and there were
ten inquiries.

The Home is now almost meeting the 5 day week for the nurses.

Miss Moore is leaving on the ist of June, but will return in time
to relieve Miss Ash when she takes her vacation. Probably

Miss Moore will leave permanently in October but may retire during
vacations later.

Follow-up-Fund: Miss Rolph reporting. Miss Scott is still re-
ceiving help. Irene Mansfield is, to be given assistance.

Home Furnishing and Maintenance: Mrs. Towne reported that the
new hand rail on the steps cost #250.00.

Garden Committee: Mrs. Hilbert reported that the new tulips are
out and the cherry blossoms are about to bloom.

Books: Mrs. Krick reported several donations have been received.

House Activities: Mrs. Byrne reports a committee meeting is to be
held this afternoon. The Family has accomPlished a great deal for
the Bazaar already.

Entertainment: Miss Walter is planning to serve tea twice a week
from now on.
A musical evening has been planned for May.2nd.

Old Business: Question of a new name for the Home.
Additional suggestions were as follows - Bay Haven, Bayside and
Hawthorne House.

Complaints about the noise of the 3rd floor television and possible
remedies of the situation to be discussed with Miss Ash.

New Business: Mrs. Craig spoke of the merger - now waition for the

by-laws to be drawn up -

Miss Ash is leaving April 13th to take care of a friend, she will
return April 27th and everything is arranged for the running of the
Home in her absence.
