May 21st. 1957

The regular monthlymeeting of the Board of Managers of the San Francisco Ladies
Protection and Relief Society was held Tuesday, May 21st at 10:30 A.M. at
2507 Pine Street. ‘he President, Mrs. Perry presiding.

There were present the Mesdames Perry, Stephenson, Leavitt, Mortimer Smith,
Griffith, Miller, Byrne, Craig, Appleman, Stuart Smith, Charles Smith, Hilbert,
Nigh, Walkup, Porter, Krick, Pogson, and the Misses Rolph, Zane and Bridge.

In the absence of the Secretary and Assistant Secretary Mrs. Krick was appointed
Secretary pro tem.

The minutes of the previous meeting were read and approved.

The Preasurer's report showing a balance in the current account of $5,20h.19
was read and placed on file.

A resolution was voted authorizing the signatures of Mrs. Perry, Miss Zane and
Mrs. Nigh on the bank account for Laguna Street at the Bank of California.

After discussion it was moved by Mrs. Porter - seconded by Mrs. Craig and carried
that in the future the minutes of the meetings of the Board of Managers be sent
to the Board of Directors as requested by their President, The vote was 10 for
and 7 against.

The Superintendent, Mrs. Pogson gave q full and excellent report of her trip
to Southern California where she visited six Homes for elderly people. She
acquired much valuable information which will help in planning the running of
the new Home.
