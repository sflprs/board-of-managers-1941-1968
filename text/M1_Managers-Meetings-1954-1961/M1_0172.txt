Admissions - No report.

Policy - Mrs. Porter, Chairman, reported that the Committee met to
discuss the contract for Life Care submitted by Captain Hindes,
The sub committee of this committee met with the sub committee

of the Board of Directors, After a discussion of the contract,
it was suggested that a contract lawyer should be consulted.

The Policy Committee, at the request of the sub committee of the
Board of Directors, drew up and submitted to them a contract and
3 application forms, Mrs. Porter stressed the need for a joint

meeting of the Admission Committee and the Policy Committee on
April 2, 1959.

Dining Room - No report

Visiting - Mrs, Lyon, Chairman, told of many pleasant visits,
The birthday parties and other parties are bringing people together

and many friendships are being made. As a result, there is less
need for the Visiting Committee.

Occupational Activities - Mrs. Hilbert, read the report of the
Chairman, Mrs, Byrne, ‘The Birthday Party on March 10th was
attended by 53 guests and 5 Board members. The next Birthday
Party will be on April 28th, Board members were urged to come,

Entertainment Committee - This report was covered in Mrs. Pogson's
report, After a discussion about a tea in the Spring, it was
decided to defer action until we learn more about the budget.
There was.a general feeling that we should consider one if possible,

Personnel Practices - Mrs, Walkup, Chairman, read the Duties of
the Administrator, It was moved, seconded and carried that the
Duties of the Administrator, as read by Mrs, Walkup be accepted
with the deletion of the word "all" from duty number 4, which
states “attends all meetings", It was moved, seconded and carried
that the duties of the Office Secretary as read by Mrs. Walkup

be accepted with the deletion in duty number 16 of, “and sends
report to the Department of Social Welfare within 48 hours.”

Tt was moved, seconded and carried to give the Administrator the

by the Personnel Practices, as read by Mrs. Walkup.
