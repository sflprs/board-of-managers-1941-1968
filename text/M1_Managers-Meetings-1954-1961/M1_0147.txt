June 17, 1958

The regular meeting of the Board of Managers of the san

ladies Protection and Relief Society was held at 3400 SEAN
Street on Tuesday, June 17, 1958, at 10:30 AM, the President
Mrs, William H. Nigh, Jr., presiding. ;

Present were Mesdames: Nigh, Griffith, Lyon, Bruck, Miller,
Krick, Matignon, Mortimer Smith, Wilson, Craig, Porter, Hilbert,
Byrne, Bowes, Appleman, Walkup, Pogson, Landis; the Misses: Rolph,
Bridge, Zane.

Minutes - The minutes of the regular meeting of May 20, 1958,
were accepted with two minor changes.

The Secretary read the minutes of the meeting of the Board of
Directors of June, 1958. ;

Treasurer - Miss Bridge read the Treasurer's report.

‘Operating Balance - May 31, 1958 §12, 264,02

It was moved, seconded and carried that the Treasurer's report
be placed on file.

Administrator - Mrs. Pogson reported the arrival of three new
monthly guests. Three of the permanent care people have moved
into the Infirmary and are not expected to return to their rooms,
All employees will have chest X-rays this month. Mirrors have
been installed in the new wing. Mrs. von Lowenfeldt, the new
food consultant, has commenced her work and the dining room is
functioning adequately.

It was moved, seconded and carried that this report be accepted
and placed on file.

Nurse - Mrs. Miller, Chairman of the Infirmary Committee, read
the report of the Head Nurse, Mrs. Josephine Owen, R.N.. The
nursing care has developed into a health supervision service for
the whole Home and has been extended as the need has grown.

It was moved, seconded and carried that this report be cia iia |
and placed on file.

COMMITTEE REPORTS

Finance - Miss Bridge read and explained the revised budget.

The present budget is $18,536.71. The revised budget is $19,763.00.
Miss Bridge stated that any balance will be returned to the Board
of Directors at the end of the year.

It was moved, seconded and carried that the revised budget be
approved and that it be presented to the Board of Directors.

Infirmary - Mrs. Miller, Chairman, read a letter from the State

of California, Department of Health, stating the requirements from
the licensing regulations apply to sterilization in a Nursing

Home or Infirmary. Mrs. Miller stated that the Committee had
studied the advisability of using a large autoclave in the In-
firmary, or a sterilizer and a small autoclave. Mrs. Miller
noted that the Infirmary Committee recommends that an autoclave

be installed, The Committee feels that an autoclave is in keeping
with the modern planning throughout the building,

It was moved, seconded and carried that the two alternatives be
presented to the Board of Directors for their decision with the
pecommendatiann af the Tnfirmarvy Gommittee.
