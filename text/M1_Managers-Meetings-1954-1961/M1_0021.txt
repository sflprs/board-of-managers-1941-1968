Mrs. Krick also reported that Miss Knowlton is vastly im-
proved. Mrs. Power is using her walker, and the rest of the
Family are all doing very well.

Mrs. Charles Smith spoke about how the fire in the library
was being enjoyed every afternoon.

Convalescent Committee: In the absence of Mrs. Bowes, Mrs. Smith
reported a full end smooth running Department.

Home Furnishing: Mrs. Towne reported buying a cherry chest for
Mrs. Hoeft's room, and shopping for a combination desk and vanity,
as the room is small. It was noted that Sloane's is most co-
operative and allows the Home a 20% discount. Mrs. Towne reported
that a new hand rail on the steps is needed, as the old one has
corroded.

Garden Committee: Mrs. Hilbert reported the garden was beginning
to look very spring-like, and in another two weeks hopes to see
her plans in flower.

Books: Mrs. Krick reported donations of books.

Inasmuch: Mrs. Stephenson was asked to et a price on One Hundred
new books besides the fifty books owed the Society. These are
to be paper covered.

Entertainment: Miss Zane asked for suggestions for an Easter
Party.

Activities: Mrs. Byrne reported receiving $13.55 more from the
Bazaar. Mrs. Byrne also reported that two dozen aprons are being
made and three dozen tea towels. There was a request made for
an old blanket and mattress cover.

Mrs. Byrne requested permission to send each member of the
Family the usual $5.00 at Easter time; the money to be taken from
the Bazaar Fund.

Mrs. Nigh reported ordering a Sylvania table model television
to be sent out on trial for the third floor sitting-roon.

Old Business: Mrs. Craig reported visiting a Sanitarium in San
Leandro with some of the Board members. Featured were radiant heat
and an inter-communication system for each patient.

Mrs. Craig reported that the State Commission is studying the
value of physical therapy, as well as occupational therapy for
infirmaries.

The question of telephones was again discussed.

‘New Business: Mrs. Perry asked for volunteers to attend a National
conference and Eighty-Second Forum of Social Work, to be held from
May 29th to June 3rd. Mrs. Krick and Miss Ash will represent the
Home.

The question of filling vacancies on the Board was brought up.
Superintendent's Report: Miss Ash reported that the Home was quiet
during February, ana the Family well and active. Mrs. Hoeft was
welcomed to the Home. Mrs. Lennox is enjoying her new room. The
Santa Clara County Welfare Department is now handling Mrs. de Mari's
affairs and quite well. The Convalescent Department was full with
reservations ahead.

There being no further business, the meeting adjourned.
