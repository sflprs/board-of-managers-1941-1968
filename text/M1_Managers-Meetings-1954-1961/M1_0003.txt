OCTOBER 5, 195k.

The regular meeting of the San Francisco Ladies Protection snd
Relief Society was held at the "Home" on October 5th. Mrs. Perry
presiding. Present were Mesdames Hilbert, Stephenson, Cambron,
Craig, S.Smith, Lyon, Krusi, Krick, Bowes, Miller, C. Smith, Allen
Nigh and Miss Rolph.

A briéf devotional period was held.

Corresponding Secretary: Mrs. Stephenson read a letter from
Mrs. Byrne regretting her absence from Board meetings due to illness.

A letter of resignation from Mrs. Pierson was read. The Board
accepted her resignation with very sincere regret.

Recording Secretary: The minutes of the previous regular meet-
ing and mid-month meeting were read and approved. A discussion was
held concerning having the minutes typed and inserted in a special
loose-leaf binder, instead of copying them by long hand. It was
moved and seconded and so ordered.

The Treasurer: Mrs. Hilbert read the report for August.

Family Committee: Mrs. Krick reported that Mrs. deMari fell
and broke her hip while at Los Altos. She was immediately brought
up to San Francisco and is now in the Allyne bed.

Mrs. Power and Mme. Olivier are both much
better. Mrs. Munn is also better and is again working for the
Bazaar.

: Mrs. Lenox moved into the Home and has
already made a place for herself in the Family. She was most
enthuastic about her room.

Convalescent Committee: Mrs. Bowes reported that the Depart-

ment is working very smoothly.
The Bed Days report as follows:

August - 620 bed days
52h used
62 free beds
$1142.75 Income

In going over the figures for the past six months, it has been
found that about 500 bed days per month are used, with an average
income of #1000.00 per month. This is a better percentage than last
year.

Our two benefactors from Mill Valley have asked to be allowed
a week's rest in a two bed room whenever possible.

Miss Radford is better and plans to come back next month for
a four hour shift, sharing a full shift with Miss Moore.
