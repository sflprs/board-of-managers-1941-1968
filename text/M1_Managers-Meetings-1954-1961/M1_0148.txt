Policy - Following a discussion covering persons to be taken
into the Home at less than per capita cost and on a joint
tenancy basis, it was recommended by the Policy Committee that
any such person with an income in excess of $111 a month should
be allowed $40 a month for her own personal use with the residue
of her income being paid to the Home up to the yearly per capita
cost, the financial assets of such person to be put in joint
tenancy between the Home and the member. Permanent guests shall
be examined by the physician employed by the Home.
