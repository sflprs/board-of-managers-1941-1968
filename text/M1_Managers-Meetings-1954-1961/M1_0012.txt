The regular meeting of the Ladies Protection and Relief Soc-
{ety was held Tuesday, December 7th at the Home. Mrs. Warren Perry

Stephenson, Krick, Towne, Landis, Lyon, Byrne, Cc. Smith, Miller,

Joy Buttle, daughter of Mrs. Biggs, of great appreciation for the
loving care given her mother during her residence at the Home.
