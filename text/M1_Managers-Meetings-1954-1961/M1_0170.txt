the absence of Mrs, Byrne:

Bazaar: Total receipts $1,623.47
Expenses 500.00

The Handicraft Fund will assume the expense of the birthday parties,
Estimated cost - $20/party. Mrs. Byrne stated that a plan fora
new program for a fund raising project will be worked out and pre-
sented to the Board at the March meeting.

Entertainment - No report

Personnel Practices - Mrs, Walkup, Chairman, reported that the staff
Manual is working out very well. It is too detailed to bring to the
Board but is available in the office. Mrs. Walkup suggested that
at a later date the Board may wish to have experts review our per-
sonnel practices. It was moved, seconded and carried that the Re-

cording Secretary be empowered to have the minutes typed at the
expense of the Home,

Garden - Mrs, Hilbert, Chairman,reported that a blue hellebore and
a reticulata have been purchased with Captain Hindes' gift.

Furnishings - Mrs, Blackie, Chairman, stated that blue fabric has
been purchased for 3 wing chairs for the bedrooms.

library - Mrs, Stephenson, Chairman, reported that 4 books were pur-

chased during the month. Mrs. Nigh stated that there is a request
for books on travel.

New Members - Mrs, Craig, Chairman, stated that Mrs. Archibald
Gralnger has accepted the invitation for membership on the Board of

Managers, Mrs, Shaw, Mrs, Tully and Mrs. Ganyard have declined.
Mrs. Francis Lloyd will be invited.

Building and Maintenance - Mrs, Craig, Chairman, reported that the
cupboards in the Infirmary are completed and will be painted.

Chapel - No report,

House Committee - Mrs, Craig, Chairman, reported that the first meeting
of this committee was held on January 22, 1959, It will meet on the
second Tuesday of each month, Mrs. Craig read from the By-Laws

which designate that: “The House Committee shall have the super-
vision of the physical property, personnel and operation of the Home,
subject to 2 policy worked out and approved by the Board of Managerse
It shall act as an advisory committee to the Superintendent.“

Old Business - Mrs, Nigh reported that the court had awarded Ben
Wong's children to the Reverend and Mrs. Oss. .

Nominating Committee - Mrs. Nigh appointed Mrs. Wilson, Miss Rolph,
Mrs, Walkup, Mrs. Krick and Mrs. Bowes to serve as the nominating
committee for the officers and members of the Board of Managerse

New Business - It was moved, seconded and carried to delegate the
Administrator to employ and dismiss personnel in accordance with
Section 9, Article 5 of the By-Laws for six months.

There being no further business, the meeting was adjourned.
