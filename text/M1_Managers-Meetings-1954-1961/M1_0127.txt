April 15, 1958

The regular meeting of the San Francisco Ladies Protection and Relief
Society was held at 300 Laguna Street on April 15, 1958, the President,
Mrs, Warren Perry, presiding.

Present were Mesdames Perry, Bowes, Krick, Nigh, Craig, Wilson, Matignon,
Bruck, Lyons, Stuart Smith, Charles Smith, M. Smith, Leavitt, Walkup and
Pogson, The Misses Rolph, Bridge and Zane.

In the absence of Mrs. Landis, Mrs. Bruck read the Secretary's report. ‘The
report was accepted with one correction concerning the date of the Open House

to be held Friday, April 18th.
The Treasurer, Miss Bridge read the report vhich was accepted and placed on file.

The Superintendant, Mrs, Pogson gave her report. The house is running smoothly

and 1s well staffed. The old building has been safe guarded by a new system
of alarms that alert the front office and the Fire Department. The three new

Old Age members have settled in comfortably.

Nurses Report was read and accepted. A surprise visit was made by ir, Wall
of the Department of Public Health. The five day week was launched in April.
there were 1 people in the Infirmary, and 39 people were given care outside

the Infirmary.
Finance Committee - report was accepted.

Hospital Committee - Mrs. Bowes reported buying extra basins within the budget.

The five day week is now working with one graduate nurse on each shift.
According to the doctors a vote of gratitude is owed irs. Owen, the Head Nurse.
Miss bridge brought up the question of the autoclave. hr. Hindes had asked that
a second hand autoclave be bought, but Mrs. Bowes had already asked for one
from government surplus and had been refused. The Committee will study the

question further.

Dining Room —- no report
House Activities - No report
Rooms - No report

Garden - No report

Budget Committee - Miss Bridge moved and it was seconded and carried that We
Yeturn any surplus to the Trustees at the Annual neeting. And that a new
budget be ready for presentation at the June meeting.

lirs. Porter and Mrs. Craig were thanked for the travel posters now on display
in the little theater and hall.

Theater, linen room, and hall.
