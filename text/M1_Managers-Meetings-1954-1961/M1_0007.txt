The Trustees, Mr. Hindes and Mr. Brown arrived to dis-
cuss the negotiations now going on with the Crockers concer-
ning the absorption of the Old Peoples Home by the San
Francisco Ladies Protection and Kelief Society. It is felt
by our Trustees that the best plan is for our Society to build
the Annex after which the merger will take place. The Trustees
felt there were two reasons for doing this.

1. The Board would not have to run two Homes at
the same time. .

2. The Trustees do not wish to assume the moral
or legal risk of the Pine Street Home.

A special meeting of our Trustees was held last week at
which time the attached resolution was passed unanimously, and
a copy of which was then given to the Crocker Trustees who felt
it was acceptable. They in turn will present it to their Board
this next Thursday.
