Repair Committee: Mrs. Towne reported that new linoleum will
have to be installed in the flats.

Garden Committee: Mrs. Hilbert reported that Henry has been
moving plents preparatory to building the annex.

Entertainment Committee: Miss Zane reported a very successful
Bingo Party last evening, which was enjoyed by eighteen members
of the Family and Convalescents.

Bazaar Committee: Mrs. Byrne reported that some articles have
already been made in preparation for next Year's Bazaar. The
question was raised whether we would be able to hold one in the
Homie due to building. It was pointed out that we might use the
Pine Street Home instead.

Superintendent's Report: Miss Ash reported that January was an

uneventful month in the Home. That the Family kept quite well,

and that the Convalescent Department was full. Some books were

received for the library. Mrs. Perry reported that Miss Ash had
been very ill during part of January - the first time in twenty-
two years. Miss Ash will be away on vacation from February 9th
to February 16th.

Old Business: Mrs. Perry reported that Phillip S. Boone did
our publicity at the time of the merger and an article in the
Argonaut was passed around for the Board to read. Mrs. Carol Wilson
also wrote an article on the merger, at the request of the Board
which was not used. It was moved by Mrs. Krick, seconded by
Miss Zane and carried that a gift be sent Carol Wilson in ap-
preciation of work she had done for the Home, the gift not to
exceed One Hundred Dollars in cost. Mrs. Perry reported attend-
ing a meeting of the City Planning Commission with Mr. Hindes,
at which time this property was classified as "3rd. Zone with
stipulations."

By-Laws Committee: Mrs. Craig reported that the Committee was
meeting once a week and hoped to be ready for the February 21st
meeting.

Policy Committee: Mrs. Miller reported on a meeting held by the
committee at which time they outlined a general policy. The
Committee also sent the following recommendation to the Board:-
"It is recommended .by the Policy Committee that, until the com-
pletion of the new wing at 3400 Laguna Street the operation of
each separate Home shall be carried on by Committees of Management
which shall be composed of the present members of each Board.
These committees shall be under the Board of Managers of the
Ladies Protection and Relief Society. There shall be an Executive
Committee comvosed of Officers of the Committee of Management of
3400 Laguna Btreet and of 2507 Pine Street. This Committee shall
serve as a liason between the two Committees of Management."

it was so moved by Mrs. Miller, seconded by Mrs. Craig and
carried.

Mrs. Krick reported that Fifty-six letters had been sent
to the names on the waiting list for admission to the Home, and
that she is now receiving answers to her letter. She will report
on this matter at a later date.

Building Committee: Mrs. Craig reported that the architect,
Mr. Perry is working on the plans continuously.
