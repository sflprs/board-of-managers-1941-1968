June 5th, (cont'd)

CONVALESCENT COMMITTEE: Mrs. Bowes reported that the department
had been full for part of the month, but was quiet now. During
April there were available, unoccupied etc. as follows:-

Available bed days 570
Occupied bed days 347
Free bed days 0
Income 3760.75

It was reported that at present the Convalescent Department
could take care of Mrs. Taylor, but that she has not applied for
admittance.

Mrs. Miller told the Board how well Miss Mansfield is doing.

BOOKS: Mrs. Krick reported buying 8 books for the library, all
of which went into immediate circulation.

ENTERTAINMENT: It was again announced that on June 23rd at
7:30 o'clock, Dr. and Mrs. Kroll will show some pictures at the
Home.

HOUSE ACTIVITIES: Mrs. Byrne reported thet Mrs. Cadman can no
longer do work with her hands and is very sad about the situat-
ion. The Board were shown a display of dolls, in a wedding
procession, which were dressed by a convalescent.

SUPERINTENDENTS REPORT: Miss Ash stated that May had been a
quiet month, and that 211 the substitutes for vacations were
excellent.

OLD BUSINESS: Mrs. Perry called the attention of the Board to
Article 1V, Section 1, that in future the first Tuesday of the
month will be for meetings of the Ladies Protection and Relief
Society only, and that the third Tuesdays will be a joint business
meeting of t:.e Ladies Protection and Relief and 2507 Pine Street.

Mrs. Porter explained the "Committee of Management at
2507 Pine," what they are trying to do, and what Mrs. Porter's
report to the Ladies Protection and Relief will be.

It was decided to skip the July meeting.

BUILDING COMMITTEE: Mrs. Craig reported that the plans were in
the hands of the contractor.

Mrs. Perry reported that Dr. Stiegeler wanted to know if he
is to be retained when the merger is completed. It was felt that
he should have been consulted about the plans for the infirmary.
The feeling of the Board was to retain both Doctors, if possible.
