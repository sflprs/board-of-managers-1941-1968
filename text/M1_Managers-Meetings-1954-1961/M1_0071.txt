September 18, 1956

The mid-month meeting of the San Francisco Ladies Protection
and Relief Society was held September 18th, at the Home.
Mrs. Perry was in the chair and present were the Mesdames Porter,
Hilbert, Craig, Krick, Mategnon, Leavitt, Stephenson, Miller,
Towne, Stuart Smith, Bowes, Bruck, Charles Smith, Byrne, Nigh and
the Misses Rolph and Bridge. .

The Secretary read the minutes which were approved with
two corrections. The Secretary then read the minutes of the Spec-
ial Meeting of the Finance and Policies Committees.

The Administrator, Miss Ash reported that August had been a busy

The Policy Committee: Mrs. Miller read the recommendations of the
Committee, which are as follows:

This organization is to operate a Home for men and women
70 years of age or older. .

They must be residents of the San Francisco Metropolitan Area
and must have been residents for 5 years.

All applicants will be accepted on a non-sectarian basis.

They must be men and women with the same cultural background.

Preference shall be given to citizens.

Applicants will be accepted on a monthly basis and may be
composed of those who can pay the minimum cost and those who cannot.

Preference is to be given on the basis of need.

No life members are to pay a lump sum down on admittance for
life care.

The minimum charge shall go to those on pensions. We must be
sure to remain a charitable institution.

Pensioners may be either men or women with the understanding
that the Admission Committee shall decide what men and in what
proportion.

The Admission Committee shall have the right to say who are
eligible for admission to the Home.
