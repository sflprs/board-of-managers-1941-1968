September 20, 1960

The regular meeting of the Board of Managers of the San Francisco
ladies’ Protection and Relief Society was held on Tuesday, September
20, 1960 at 10:30 AM at 3400 Laguna Street, the First Vice-President,
Mrs, E, E, Blackie, presiding.

Present were Mesdames: Stephenson, Blackie, Bowes, Charles Smith,
Lloyd, Byrne, Lyon, Rettenmayer, Griffith, Noble, Skene, Kirkwood,
Gunther, Krick, Hilbert, Pogson, Landis; the Misses: Rolph, Bridge.

Excused were Mesdames: Porter, Granger, Craig, Perry.

The minutes of the regular meeting of the Board of “anagers on August
23, 1960 were accepted as corrected.

The Assistant. Secretary, Mrs. Charles Smith, read the minutes of a
special meeting of the Board of Directors on September 8, 1960.

It was moved, seconded and carried that these minutes be placed on
file. ‘

TREASURER - Miss Bridge, Treasurer, read the Financial Report:
Operating Balance August 31, 1960 $12,431.49

It was moved, seconded and carried that the Treasurer's Report be
placed on file,

ADMINISTRATOR - Mrs. Pogson reported that there had been no admissions
during the month. It has been necessary to place Mrs, Emma Davis in
Agnew due to her disoriented condition. A monthly guest (Mrs, Ethel
Thompson Taylor) died at Children's Hospital. The new piano has been
placed in the lobby. Professional help is necessary to rearrange the
furniture in this area, There are weekly discussion groups and lec-
tures. Mr. Pape has resumed the weekly movies. Miss Fox is in charge
of the record concerts. The September Birthday Party was most success
ful. Miss Skelton who has been ill since September 7, returns today
in the capacity of Director of Nursing. Miss Margaret Long is a

great help in the maintenance and housekeeping department. It was

moved, seconded and carried that the Administrator's report be
adopted,

COMMITTEE REPORTS

Infirmary - Mrs, Morrison, Chairman, reported that the load in the
Infirmary is relatively light. There were 11 Life Care Members and
1 Guest in the Infirmary and 3 Out-Patients. Twelve patients re-
ported to the Infirmary (8 Life Care Members and 4 Guests.) ‘The diet
kitehen is functioning satisfactorily. The housekeeping department
is handling the trays which go directly to the patients. Miss Skel-
ton became ill after taking over as Director of the Infirmary. Mrs.
Morrison expressed the hope- that the personnel of the Infirmary will
be arranged on the basis of Miss Hotchkiss' recommendations.
