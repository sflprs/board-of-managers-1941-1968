San Francisco, California
day of 1957

San Francisco Ladies Protection and Relief Society is a charitable
organization which has for its object the rendering of protection and
assistance to dependent men and women. In carrying out this object
it maintains a Home at 300 laguna Street, San Francisco, California,
in which it houses and boards dependent men and women for a small

charge, which may change from time to time, and which does not yield
a profit to the Society.

Since ny application to become a resident of that Home has been
accepted, and since the Society has received me into its Home, I

agree while a resident in said Home I shall and will pay to the Society
its regular rates and charges for my maintenance in said Home, which .
I understand will be less than the monthly old age assistance I receive
from the State of California, with the understanding that these rates
and charges may be changed from time to time, but in no case to exceed
the amount received from the State of California. I also agree that

if it becomes necessary for me to be a permanent patient in the
Infirmry, I shall pay a reasonable charge for this special care, but in
no case shall the total amount vaid to the Society exceed the amount of
old age assistance received from the State of California.

Th consideration of the Society having so received me into its Home,
and maintaining me at its regular rates to those receiving old age
State aid, I further agree that if at the time of my death I am

still residing in said Home, then and in that event all property be-
longing to me at the time of my death shall go and belong to said
Society, and I agree to make and leave a Will devising and bequeathing

ny entire estate (with the exception of my jewelry, furniture and
personal effects) to said Society.

It is also understood that should I at any time hereafter desire to
Sever my connections with and leave said Home I may do so, or should
the Society request me to remove from said Home I will do so at once.

In either of the above events I shall be released from any obligation
to leave my property to said Society.

I have read the admission requirements for residence in the Home, and
agree to abide by such requirements,
