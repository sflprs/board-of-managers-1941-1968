ADMINISTRATOR - Mrs. Pogson reported that all rooms are occupied.
There has been a contract made for the cleaning of the windows

of the Home twice a year at a cost of $213.00 per cleaning.

The carpets in the hall have been cleaned during the night by a
machine purchased for that purpose. The broken slate on the roof
has been repaired for $95.00, the cost being borne by the insurance
company, Miss Walter has resumed the organizing of weekly con-
certs and a class in music appreciation has been started. Be-
cause of the illness of Ben Wong, the chef, an extra helper has
been added to the kitchen staff.
