December 6, 1955

The Special Meeting of the Ladies Protection and Kelief
Society was held on December the. sixth at the Home.
Mrs. Warren Perry Presiding. Those present were as follows:
Mesdames Craig, Knight Smith, Nigh, Bowes, Stephenson,
Stuart Smith, Hilbert, Miller, Landis, Charles Smith, Lyon,
Krick, Byrne, Bruck and the Misses Allyne& Rolph. Also
present were Messers Cornwall, Hindes, Leicester, Brown,
Moore and Anderson.

Mrs. Perry turned the meeting over to Mr. Hindes, who
reported that the Crocker Board hed voted for the merger
on December 5th, and thet the Secretary of State of
California had agreed to the merger. Mr. Hindes urged the
Board to make the two Boards one unit, and pointed out the
need of new By-Laws, particularly:

1 - A good intake Policy.

2 - A definate policy for assigning quarters.

3 - The exact duties of the various committees and
members. .

Mrs. Bowes then offered a resolution here appended.
It was seconded by Mrs. Craig and carried unanimously.

Mrs. Perry read a letter from Mr. Stetson Hindes offering
to re-build the garden cottage in memory of his wife,
Saretta Hindes, as a home for the Manager.

A recess of 15 minutes was called, in which to organize
the Interim Society.

Mr. Hindes called the Interim meeting to order.

Mrs. Nigh read the resolution of the Interim Protection
and Relief Society, - here appended - Seconded by Mrs. Hilbert
and unanimously carried.

Mrs. Bruck moved the meeting adjourn. Seconded by
Mrs. Landis and carried.

Before retiring, the gentlemen all agreed that the merger
is a challenge which can be met successfully; thanks were
extended firs. Craig for her untiring work and devotion to

the merger.

The mid-month meeting was then called by Mrs. Perry. A
brief devotional period ensued.

The minutes of the previous meeting were read and approved,
with one correction.
