* Copy of Infirmary Committee's Report

A meeting of the Infirmary Committee was held at 3400 Laguna Street
on Tuesday, August 15, at 9:45 A.M. Both Mrs. Pogson and Mrs.
Rathman, Director of Nursing, were present.

Mrs. Rathman reported that the Infirmary is so crowded that the

room which had been set aside as a consultation room for the doctors
is now converted into a two-bed ward. According to the attending
physicians there are from five to eight of the Life Contract members
who will require infirmary care in the very near future. But the
problem of where to put these cases becomes increasingly difficult.
Both Dr. McKenzie and Dr. Steigler feel that the need for more space
is increasingly evident. Since we are taking more older people who
are less robust than those we admitted formerly, the situation will
inevitably become more acute. The average age in the Infirmary at
present is 89 years. There are 6 who must be fed and 7 who are
incontinent. Such patients require more time and a somewhat. larger
nursing staff.
