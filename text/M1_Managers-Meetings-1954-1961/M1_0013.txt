December 7th (continued)

Finance Committee: Miss Rolph requested that the Petty Cash
account be raised from $75.00 to $100.00. Moved by Mrs. Craig
seconded by Mrs. Krusi and so ordered.

Convalescent Committee: Mrs. Bowes reported that all the beds
are taken at present. She also called attention to the fact that
six members of the Family have tray service, besides those in the
Convalescent Department.

In discussing the possible annex Mrs. Bowes
felt that a 10 room infirmary would not be adequate, and that our
nursing problem will definately be enlarged.

Mrs. Hughes is filling in for Mrs.Loeffler
while the latter is on vacation.

Follow-up Fund: Miss Rolph reported buying a pair of shoes
for Blanche; also paying a $25.00 drug bill for Miss Scott.

Mrs. George Beaver sent a contribution to
the Christmas Fund.

Furnishings: Mrs. Towne stated that a vent is to be
installed to carry off steam created by the drier on the second
floor.

Garden Committee: Mrs. Hilbert reported that the flower beds
are all dug up and the bulbs planted. Also that Henry's wife is ill.

Inasmuch: Mrs. Stephenson reported ordering the new
copies.

House Activities: Mrs. Krusi reported for the Bazaar Commit-
tee. A gift from Mrs. Edwin Statmuller of $25.00 which is included
in the net profit of $840.70 realized from the Bazaar.

Mrs. Byrne reported a bank balance of
$1565.27.

New Business: Mrs. Wilson has written an outline for
publicity which will be taken up later.

A new committee is to be formed with
Mrs. Miller as Chairman on Home Policies.

Mrs. Craig suggested that we should perhaps

consider ourselves a non-profit institution with endowed beds in-
stead of a charitable organization.

Building Committee: Mrs. Craig is on the committee to revise
the State Code for the Aged.

: Mrs. Bowes and Mrs. Miller are visiting
various infirmaries.

A discussion was held regarding television
in the Home. Mrs. Nigh was asked to look into the subject.
