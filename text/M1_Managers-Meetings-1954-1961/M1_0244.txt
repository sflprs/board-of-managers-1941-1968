Finance and Budget: Miss Bridge, Chairman, reported her committee met
on September 17th to go over replacement items above and outside the
Regular Budget. Recommendations sent to the Board of Directors for
their approval were: Plant replacements, $2,762.88 (kitchen screen-
ing and garbage disposition). Equipment replacements, $1,745.56 (4
hospital beds and office furniture).

Admissions: Mrs. Gunther, Chairman, reported 3 new guests -- Miss
Marcia Fee and Mr. Henry Latrodec under Trust Agreement; Rev. Harlan
Bailey as a Low Income Resident.

A motion was made, seconded, and carried that the Study Committee
explore the following points:

(1) Applicants with limited amount of capital shall be
permitted to retain the same amount of money (1, 500.)
which is permitted to residents on OAA, and this be
retroactive.

(2) That the salient points in our Trust Agreement Contracts
be simply and briefly outlined to permit applicants to
readily understand the contents.

After discussion of Life Care Contracts and the crowded Infirmary,
it was moved, seconded, and carried that, "The Board of Managers
urgently recommends to the Board of Directors that an Infirmary

be built as soon as possible, -in order that we may adequately care
for our growing number of Life Care members."

Occupational Activities: Mrs. Rettenmeyer reported 68 attended the

Entertainment: Mrs. Skene, Chairman, reported on the successful
story-hour, and a talk by an officer of the Historical Society;

also an hour of song on the 25th of August. "Current Events" talks
