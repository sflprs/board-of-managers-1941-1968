The regular meeting of the Ladies Protection and Relief Society
was held August ist, at the Home with Mrs. Philip Landis presiding.
Present were Mesdames Hilbert, Krick, Craig, Byrne, Stuart Smith,
Bruck, Nigh, Lyon, Bowes, Towne, Miller and the Misses Rolph and Zane.

In the absence of the Corresponding Secretary Mrs. Stephenson,
letters were read by Mrs. Bruck - a letter from Mrs. Pooley and
Miss Searight and a letter from Mrs. Stephenson suggesting that paper
copies of Inasmuch be sold for $1.50 a copy.

The minutes of the previous meeting were read and approved
with two (2) corrections.

Mrs. Hilbert read the Treasurer's report for June which is
as follows:

Income 7364.62
Expenses 7072.55
Bank Balance June 1, 1955 9269.86

Bank Balance June 30, 1955 9602.99

Follow-Up Fund: Miss Rolph read two letters of appreciation -

one from Miss Estrem, and the other from Mrs. Wyckoff. Help is
still being given Miss Mansfield. $600.00 has been taken out of the
Follow-Up Fund savings for current expenses.

Family Committee: Mrs. Krick reported that the Family are quite
well. Mrs. Cadman is recovering from her fall and is back from her
stay at the Hospital. Miss Holliger was not too well during June

so was sent for a change to Casa Bella where she improved rapidly.

It was moved by Mrs. Krick and seconded by Miss Rolph that Mrs. Hoeft
be asked to become a permanent member of the Family. Carried.

Bazaar Committee: Mrs. Burne reported that the Family are doing
amazing work for the Bazaar.

- Convalescent Committee: Mrs. Bowes reported that the: Department
was quiet during May, with the lowest occupancy of the year.
June picked up.

89.6 Occupancy
10% Unoccupied
45 Free bed days given
$1223.00 Income
A letter of appreciation was received from Mrs. Blumferd.

House Activities Committee: Mrs. Byrne stated she had never exper-

enced such co-operation and enthusiasm as the Family, Convalescents,
and Nursing Staff are showing this year. Contributions of yarn,
dolls, buttons etc. have been received. Miss Zane asked for contrib-
utions to the White Elephant table, as well as the Treasure table.
The question of whether the Board should be allowed to buy before the
official opening of the Bazaar was discussed.

Garden Committee: Mrs. Hilbert reported spending $61.60 for Spring
ulbs.
