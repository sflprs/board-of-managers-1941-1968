Mr. Anderson sent word to the Board that on our Financial
statements the cost of employees living in was $40.00 per Month,
while at the Crocker Home the cost was $60.00 per Month. It was
moved by irs. Craig, seconded by Mrs. Miller and carried that the

Mrs. Cherles Smith, Mrs. Craig and Mrs. Nigh reported
attending meetings on "Standards for Homes for the Aged."

Mrs. Perry reported receiving a questionaire from the
Chest for a "National Survey for Welfare Costs." It was felt we
should co-operate in helping the Chest.

Building Committee: Mrs. Craig discussed several aspects of the
