New Business: An invitation was extended the Board from the

Miss Ash has asked to pay $55.00 a month for the room
and board of her sister.

The Merger: Mrs. Craig reported that Mr. Greham and

Mr. Leicester stated that the Ladies Protection and Relief
Society should become a Corporation. It was vnanimously voted
to accept the interim By-Laws.

A discussion about laundry expenses was held.
Mrs. Stephenson reported it would cost about $7500.00 to install
laundry equipment. It was decided to investigate the question
further.

Superintendent's Keport: Miss Ash reported April a quiet
month. There was a dearth of convalescents. Donations of
clothing are needed.

May 17, 1955

The regular mid-month meeting was held at the Home with
Mrs. Warren Perry in the chair.
Those present were MNesdames Perry, Hilbert, Stephenson, Craig,
Landis, Lyon, Towne, Charles Smith, Stuart Smith, Bowes, Bruck,
Krick, Nigh, Byrnes and the Misses Allyne and Rolph.

it was moved by Mrs. Craig, seconded by Mrs. Stephenson
and carried that three season tickets be bought for the
Vallery Lectures from the Bazaar Fund, for the enjoyment of
the Family.

It was announced that on June 10th, at 3 o'clock
Mrs. Frank Adams will give a concert at the Home.

It was moved by Mrs. Craig, seconded by Mrs. Lyon and
carried that the name of the Interim Protection and Relief
Society be given the Home, and that the Secretary sign the
following: "to transfer all property of the San Francisco
Ladies Protection and Relief Society, both real and personal,
and to authorize the secretary to execute the necessary documents
to effect the transfer."

There being no further business the meeting adjourned.
