meeting the age limit was discussed and 65 was again agreed upon.

February 1, 1955

The regular monthly meeting of the Ladies Protection and
Relief Society was held at the Home. Mrs. Warren Perry presiding.
Present were Mesdames Perry, Stephenson, Cambron, Krick, Stuart Smith,
Charles Smith, Miller, Towne, Bowes, Landis, Nigh, Hilbert and the
Misses Rolph and Zane.

A brief devotional period was observed.
The Corresponding Secretary: Mrs. Stephenson reported having
received a letter from the Marina Civic and Property Owners League.
The Recording Secretary: Read the minutes of the January regular
meeting and mid-mon meeting. These were approved as read.

The Treasurer: Mrs. Hilbert gave the Annual Report; a comparison
between Budget figures and actual expenses.

Saving on Income $ 2,973.05
Saving on Expenses 3,771.49
Bank Balance December 1,1954 17,413.14

It was moved, seconded and carried to turn back $10,000 to the
Trustees.

doing beautifully, and is to be moved to Mrs. Kingsbury's room to
facilitate her care. Both Mrs. Cadman and Miss Houchin's rooms will
be done over. Mrs. Hoeft has passed her physical examinations end
