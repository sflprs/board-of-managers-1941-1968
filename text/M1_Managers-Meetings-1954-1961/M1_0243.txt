COMMITTEE REPORTS

Infirmary: Mrs. Morrison, Chairman, reported her committee had a
Special meeting September 5, 1961, to review the pressing situation
of an over-crowded Infirmary. She said of the total 97 residents,
59 are Life Care, and 2 Boarders have applied for Life Care. The
average age of Life Care residents is 84 years. All 15 beds in the
Infirmary are occupied; 8 of these patients need round-the-clock
sare SO more aids have been employed. Life Care persons in the

ether building are becoming concerned about being sent elsewhere if
taken ill.
