May 20, 1958

The regular meeting of the Board of Managers of the Sa:

Ladies Protection and Relief Society was held at 3400 “one
Street on Tuesday, May 20, 1958, at 10;30 A.M., the President
Mrs, William H. Nigh, jr., presiding. :

Present were Mesdames: Nigh, Craig, Hilbert, Charles smith,
Stuart Smith, Mortimer Smith, Lyon, Matignon, Miller, Wilson,
Leavitt, Krick, Byrne, Griffith, Pogson, landis; Misses: Rolph
Zane, Bridge. ,

Minutes - The minutes of the meeting of April 15, 1958, were
accepted as read with two minor corrections,

Treasurer - The Treasurer, Miss Bridge, read the Treasurers'
report. ‘

Operating Balance - April 30, 1958 $7,061.93

It was moved, seconded and carried that this report be placed
on file.

Administrator - Mrs. Pogson reported that there are 106 residents
in the Home. We are deluged with requests for monthly.care, Plans
have been completed for vacations for the staff. A blaze in the
garbage chute was controlled by the automatic fire extinguisher
system before the fire department arrived. The water damage is
covered by insurance. Mr. Van Horn has completed his term as
supervisor of the dining room. It was moved, seconded and carried
that this report be accepted.

Mrs. Nigh stated that she had arrived after the fire and found
everyone serene and activities uninterrupted.

Nurse - Mrs. Miller read the report of Mrs. Josephine Owen, the
Head Nurse. As of May 19, there are 13 patients in the Infirmary.
There are many throughout the building who are receiving nursing
and geriatric care. “The general problems of reorganization and
of establishing a nursing staff sympathetic and adequately trained
to care for the problems faced in geriatrics are gradually being
worked out.™ It was moved, seconded and carried that Mrs. Owen's

report be accepted.

COMMITTEE REPORTS
Finance - Miss Bridge read the following recommendations:

1) The Finance Committee asks that it be empowered by the Board
to make up a revised budget for the last six months of 1958,
based on the experience of the first four months, and that it
shall also send the budget to the Board of Directors for their

approval.

2) The Finance Committee recommends to the Board that Mrs, Pogson
buy 31 full length mirrors, to complete the installation in
the new wing, at a cost of $620, this to be referred to Mr.
Anderson for payment by the Directors as part of the building
cost.

The Committee also recommends the purchase of a tray wagon to
be used for trays in the dumb walter, at a cost of $134.68,
