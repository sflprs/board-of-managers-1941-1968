December 19, 1961

The regular meeting of the Board of Managers of the Sa
Ladies Protection and Relief Society was held on Tuesd
December 19, 1961, at 10:30 A.M. at 3400 Laguna Street
President, Mrs. William H. Nigh, Jr., presiding.

Present _were:Mesdames Nigh, Noble, Scott Smith, Bruck, Cheles
mith, Lyon, Griffith, Gunther, Lloyd, Craig, Perry, Porter
and Misses Bridge and Rolph.

Excused:Mesdames Bowes, Blackie, Krick, Skene, Rettenmayer,
Matignon, Kirkwood, Morrison, Landis, and Hilbert.

The Secretary read the minutes of the November 21, 1961, regular
meeting of the Board of Managers, which were approved after
corrections.

Treasurer: Miss Bridge, Treasurer, read the Financial Report,
noting a deficit for November of $2,531.00.

Operating Balance November 30, 1961 $7,468.77

Miss Bridge reported the Finance Committee was including all
outside medical costs, such as drugs, etc., in "Operating
Expense" to show the cost of care. It was moved, seconded,
and carried that the Treasurer's report be placed on file.

Administrator: Mrs. Pogson reported Life Contracts had been
signed by Rev. Harlan Bailey, and Mr. Frederick Jackson; Life
Membership Trust Agreements had been signed by Miss Fee, Miss
Koepel, Miss Taylor and Miss Drant. On probation for Trust
Agreement is Mrs. R. H. McCreary; for Low Income Agreement,
Mrs. Lillian McCune. The death of Miss Elizabeth Reid - a
Life Member for 16 years - occurred December 1, 1961. A
Christmas gift of $250.00 was received from Mr. John Ham, and
a gift of $25.00 from Mr. Hunt in memory of his mother. Dr.
Buchkolz will try holding chapel services at 4:30 P.M. during
the December Sundays. If this is popular with the majority,
he will make it a permanent time. Many Christmas entertain-
ments by carolers have been arranged for the Holiday Season,
and thanks go to Mrs. Craig for procuring the Cathedral Choir
boys for the annual Christmas party on December 20th. All
Board members are welcome. A vote of thanks was given Miss
Brewer for the beautiful posters, announcing the Christmas
party, and for the charming place-cards for the Christmas
dinner tables. Mrs. Eva Anderson assisted Miss Brewer with
decorating the place-cards.

Infirmary: In Mrs. Morrison's absence, Mrs. Nigh reported
briefly that the Infirmary is full. Plans for a possible new
Infirmary have been delayed because of Capt. Hindes! illness.
Mr. Thornton, of the firm of Gardner A. Dailey, will draw
preliminary plans. Bed jackets made by Mrs. Dunn (one of the
Staff) for the Infirmary patients were shown and admired.

Finance and Budget: Miss Bridge, Chairman,-- no report.
