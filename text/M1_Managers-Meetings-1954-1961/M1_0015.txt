* Superintendents Report: Miss Ash reported that December was a
very busy month. The house was full and several had to be
turned away.

The Christmas Eve party was a wonderful
success - so many came that extra chairs had to be addéd. On
Christmas Day Ben provided a delicious dinner, and tea was served
the day after Christmas. Nuts were sent by Mrs. Stuart Smith.

Mrs. Cambron sent greens for decorations and Mrs. Krusi sent a
poinsetta. A donation of $35.00 was received from Mr. & Mrs.Horst.
Mr. Horst was once a child in the Home and always remembers the
Home at Christmas. The Marina Business & Professional Womens!
Club sent a gift to each member of the Family.

Each member of the Family received a
gift of $47.00 at Christmas.

It was moved by Mrs. Krusi, seconded
by Mrs. Craig and carried that the policy of giving $2.00 to
each member of the Family from the regular treasury be discontinued.

Mrs. Stephenson read notes and letters of
appreciation from the Family.

There being no further business the meet-
ing adjourned.
