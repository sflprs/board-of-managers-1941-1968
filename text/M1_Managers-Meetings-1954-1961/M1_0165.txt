Administrator - Mrs. Pogson reported the death of Mrs, Cordie
Cadman on December 20, 1958. Memorials amounting to $75 have
been received in her name. There are 106 residents, 36 on Life
Care Contracts, 18 on the $71 minimum, and 52 monthly guests,
The elderly men and women on the waiting list are urgently in
need of protective service. An agreement has been signed with
the Paul Debbes Company for a period of 3 months, They have
installed a suitable food control system especially for our needs,

Nurse and Infirmary - Mrs. Miller, Chairman, read the report of
the Head Nurse, Mrs, Owen, which listed the objectives accomplished
in the past year, Looking to the. future, Mrs, Owen noted the
need to enlarge the Infirmary capacity to give better care to the
residents and to better utilize the nursing staff, The total
amount billed to the California Physicians’ service for 1958
amounted to 3608.60 and the amount received from this source for
drugs averaged $50 a month. Mrs. Owen noted that there is a
lightening of the welfure program and certain drugs have been
taken off the list and patients must pay for them, It was moved,
seconded and carried that Mrs. Owen be given a vote of thanks.
