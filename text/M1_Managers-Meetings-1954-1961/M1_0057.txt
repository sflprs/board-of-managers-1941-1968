January.3, 1956

The regular meeting of the Ladies Protection and Relief
Society was held January 3, 1956 at the Home, with the President,
Mrs. Warren Perry presiding. Present were Mesdames Perry, Nigh,
Landis, Craig, Bruck, Bowes, Hilbert, Krick, Miller, S. Smith,
Lyon, Towne and the Misses Rolph, Zane and Ash.

The Treasurer's Report: Nrs, Hilbert's report read as follows:
Income for November, 1955 $7520.12
Expenses 6827.89

Bank Balance November 1, 1955 8720.23
Bank Balance November 30,1955 9305.33

A letter containing $200.00 for the Family Christmas
cheques was received from Mr. Hutchinson.

Family Committee: Mrs. Krick reported that the Family is fatigued
and suffering from minor colds. However all enjoyed a beautiful
Christmas. The entertainment on Christmas Eve was delightful, as
usual, and this year there was the added attraction of Ben's
Children playing the piano. On December ith the ilarina Business
and Professional Womens Club entertained with a programme and
refreshments. Lovely Christmas gifts were left for the Family,
Convalescents and Staff. A letter of appreciation is to be sent
the Club.

Convalescent Committee: Mrs. Bowes reported for November:

600 Bed Days Available
479 Bed Days Occupied
20% Bed Days Un-occupied
78 Free Bed Days given
Income ...$962.50

Follow-Up Fund: Miss Rolph reported sending -

Fifty Dollars to E. Alexander; Twenty-five Dollars to
Agnes Rohrle and Thirty Dollars to Irene Mansfield.

Mrs. Miller reported that Miss Mansfield shows slow but
steady improvement, and has held her job since September.

Garden Committee: Due to the recent storms a pine tree had to
be propped up, and a poplar tree broke a window in some adjacent
flats.

Books: Mrs. Krick reported buying twelve books and receiving
five donations.

Entertainment Committee: The day-after-Christmas Open House was
postponed this Year until the Tuesday after Christmas, in order
to invite the Crocker Board to attend. Five of the Crocker Board
accepted and had a chance not only to visit with the Board members
of the Ladies Protection and Relief, but also to meet many of the
Family.

Superintendent's Report: Miss Ash stated that December was an
extremely busy month with a full house. Numerous parties were
given including one by the Marina Business and Professional Womens
Club; a tea on December 21st was given by Miss Radford. Both
Calvery Church and a group of Girl Scouts sang carols at the Home.

A number of ex Convalescents were here for Christmas dinner
which was perfect, as usual.

Gifts from various merchants and stores were received.

Twenty-five dollars was sent to the Follow-up Fund.
