Mrs, Hilbert read a very comprehensive report on the work done by the Garden
Committee with the help of Podesta and Baldocci. It was moved by Mrs. Porter -
seconded by Mrs. Nigh and carried that the report of the Garden Committee
relative to the use of planters at an estimated cost of $20 be accepted and
referred to the Committee on Furnishings to be included in their over-all
budget. Moved by Miss Bridge - seconded by Mrs. Stephenson and carried that

a copy of the full report of the Garden Committee be sent with the minutes
to the Board of Directors.

Moved by Mrs. Craig, seconded and carried that a letter be sent from the
Board of Directors to Mr. Crothers advising him that according to his contract
with the Home - only one room is allowed one person so that in the event ef the

death of either Mr. or Mrs. Crothers the survivor will be allowed one room
instead of the two now available for both.

Mrs. Stephenson reported for the Corresponding Secretary the receipt of a

letter from Mrs, James Towne acknowledging with appreciation her having been
elected an Honorary Member of the Board of Managers.

It was moved by Miss Bridge - seconded by Mrs, Porter and carried that the
Building Committee give consideration to the providing of a Committee room
in the new building,

There being no further business the meeting adjourned.
