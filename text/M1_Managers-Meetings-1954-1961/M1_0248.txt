Miss Bridge reviewed the budget for the past nine months, and stated
that next year certain items might be changed.

Occupational Activities: Mrs. Nigh thanked Mrs. Rettenmeyer, the
chairman, and Mrs. Skene, the Tea chairman, Miss Liston and all
the ladies who had helped in making the Tea so successful. Mrs.
Rettenmeyer stated there was no November birthday party because of
the Tea. She said $220.30 had come from the sale of Bazaar
articles, and many persons were disappointed there was not more to
buy. There followed a general discussion on the worth of the
Bazaar as therapy.

Entertainment: Mrs. Skene, Chairman, thanked all who had helped with
the Tea and said it had been enjoyed greatly by the residents of

The Heritage and their guests. It was moved, seconded, and carried,
that the Christmas Party be held at 7:30 on the night of Thursday,
December 2lst. Mrs. Porter was appointed to do the decorations; Mrs.
Skene will arrange for a program.

Personnel: Miss Bridge, acting for Mrs. Kirkwood, reported that Miss
Tong had left, and there was no present plan to replace her as
Administrator's Assistant.

Building and Maintenance: Miss Bridge, Chairman, reported there was
some painting going on, and the repairing of a leak. Because of

a complaint from the Smog Control, a device is being installed in
the furnace to control the smoke from burning paper.

Garden: Mrs. Hilbert, Chairman, reported on winter clean-up. She
is getting new roses and replacing a wisteria with a Weeping Cherry,
and replacing a cherry tree. Weeds are being taken from between
flagstones.

Rooms and Furnishings: Mrs. Blackie, Chairman, reported the new
office furniture had been installed, and a new upholsterer was being
tried on some old furniture. She said the next project would be
a screen for the office. Mrs. Bruck offered to give a screen to The

Heritage as a Christmas present. This offer was enthusiastically
received.

Dining Room: Mrs. Porter, Chairman, reported the cost of meals as
-/¢ per person per day. She will suggest to Mr. Debis that more
meat be served.

Visiting: Mrs. Lyon,Chairman, reported a very happy spirit through-
out the house.

Library: Mrs. Matignon, Chairman, reported she had brought books
and puzzles today, asked for more books, and thanked Mrs. Nigh for
her contribution of books,

Nominating Committee: Mrs. Charles Smith, Chairman, reported Mrs
cott Knight Smith had agreed to serve on the Board, but that
Mesdames Monkhouse and Gardner regretted they could not. It was
moved, seconded, and carried, that Mrs. Scott Knight Smith be made

a member of this Board. It was moved, seconded, and carried that
Mrs. Robert Kirkwood be granted a six-month leave of absence.
