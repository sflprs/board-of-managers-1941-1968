Operating Balance - January 31, 1959 $4, 282.63

Administrator - Mrs, Pogson reported that Miss Rose Elliot who is
here on probation for Life Care on Old Age Assistance has been
assigned to room 224, Her probation.period started on February
1, 1959. The State Department of Social Welfare has licensed the
Home for the care of 100 residents and the Department of Public
Health has renewed our license to operate a 16 bed Infirmary.
Miss Mansfiela's suit against the Home has been settled by the
insurance company.and a dismissal of the suit is on file. Under
the direction of the Paul Debbes Company the food preparation and
service has improved. The cost of food per person per day is 88
cents. Hymn books have been purchased for the chapel. A gift

of $25 for the chapel has been received from Mrs. Stuart Smith.

Nurse and Infirmary - Mrs. Miller, Chairman, summarized the report
of the Head Nurse, Mrs. Owen. There are 16 patients in the Infir-
mary.

The 30 out patients during the month required 875 hours of

care and a total of 3633 visits. The California Medical Program

paid §78 for physicians services and $95.03 for drugs. Mrs. Miller

then read the Infirmary Policies approved by the Policy Committee:

1. General Policy for all Residents: The Infirmary is for the

_ use of the Life Care Residents, the Old Age Assistance Resi-
dents, and the Low Income Residents. However, if there isa
vacant bed in the Infirmary and a Paying Guest is in need of
care, the bed is available to him with the understanding that
if the bed is needed for anyone in the above categories the
Paying Guest must make other arrangements; such decision is
made by the Administrator on the recommendation of the Director
of Nursing. One bed is kept unoccupied for an emergency case.
The Home maintains the services of one physician for all resi-
dents except the Paying Guests. Anyone not wishing to use these
services may employ his own physician, provided he pay his own
medical and drug bills. All appointments for special medical,
dental services, physical examinations or other related ser-
vices must be made through the Director of Nursing. It was

moved, seconded and carried that the General Policy for all
Residents be accepted,

Financial Policy Within the Infirmary:

1) A Life Contract Resident is provided for by his contract.
2) An Old Age Assistance Resident is partially cared for by
fees which the California Public Assistance Medical Care
Program pays for him, An Old Age Assistance Resident re-
ceiving permanent care in the Infirmary shall pay one
dollar per day to the Infirmary for his care.

3)

Low Income Residents without financial resources are sub-
sidized by the Home,

4) A Paying Guest shall not pay less than the minimum per
capita cost of maintaining the Infirmary. He shall pay
for his own physicians and medicines. Any deviation from

the above policy shall be made by the Administrator and
the Infirmary Committee.

It was moved, seconded and carried that the Financial Policy
within the Infirmary be accepted.
