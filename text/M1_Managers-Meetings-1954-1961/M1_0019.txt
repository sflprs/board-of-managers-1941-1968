February 15, 1955

The mid-month meeting of the Ladies Protection and Relief
Society was held at the Home, February 15th. Mrs. Warren Perry
presiding. Present were Mesdames Hilbert, Craig, Krick, Byrnes,
Miller, Towne, C. Smith, Bowes, Stephenson, Stuart Smith, Lyon,
Nigh, Miss Rolph and Miss Zane.

The Treasurer: Mrs. Hilbert read the report for January.
Income $ 7,532.14
Expenses 4,167.21
Bank Balance January 1,1955 17,413.14
Bank Balance January 31,1955 18,285.89

Finance Committee: Miss Rolph reported sending $59.00 to the
March of Dimes, which included $50.00 from the Follow-Up Fund
and $9.00 comprising donations from the Home. The sum of $18.00
was spent for a surgical corset for Mrs. Wyckoff.

Miss Rolph also reported that Miss Moore is thinking of
retiring. The question was asked what Miss Moore will have on
retiring. She will receive $150.00 Social Security, towards which
the Board has paid $19.90 per Month. Miss Moore also elected to take
$7000.00 in cash from the Insurance Fund for reinvestment. It was
decided that the question of Miss Moore's retirement be taken up at
a later date.

Miss Rolph reported $2700.00 in the Special Fund. It was felt
that part of this could be drawing interest in a savings account,
the amount to be transferred should be left to the discretion of
Mr. Anderson.

Corresponding Secretary: Mrs. Stephenson read a letter from
Mrs. Bruck , presently in Honolulu thanking the Board for the cable
sent to her.

Family Committee: Mrs. Krick told the Board how happy and deeply
grateful Mrs. Hoeft was at being able to enter the Home. She
arrived February 10th. Mrs. Lennox is now able to go down to one
meal a day and continues to improve. She sent Mrs. Krick one of
her books of poetry for children. Mrs. Powers is also able to go
to the dining-room.

On March ist Mrs. Lennox will have been at the Home for her
six months probationary period. It was moved by Mrs. Krick, second-
ed by Mrs. Hilbert and carried that she be made a permanent member
of the Family.

Convalescent Committee: Mrs. Bowes reported that January was a
very busy month. There were -

Available bed days 620

Occupied bed days 569

Free bed days 59

Income $1385.00

Follow-Up Fund: Mrs. Miller suggested that some help could be
given Irene Mansfield. The Board agreed that it should be left
to Mrs. Miller's discretion.

Miss Rolph reported that according to Dr. Burnham
Miss Scott is improving.
