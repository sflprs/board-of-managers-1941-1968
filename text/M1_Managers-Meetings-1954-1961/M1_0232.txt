COMMITTEE REPORTS 1

Infirmary - Mrs, Smith reported in the absence of the Chairman, Mrs, \
Morrison, "An epidemic of severe nausea, probably viral in origin, .
swept the Home," Careful checks of the kitchen gave no evidence of
food poisoning, It was concluded that the infection was one probably |
brought into the Home. None of the Infirmary patients were affected, |
During the epidemic Mrs, Rathman worked many hours as well as the
evening. The Committee feels that we are fortunate to have such {
a dependable Director of Nursing. A letter was sent to her expressing |
appreciation for the work she did "beyond the call of duty." Two
extra nurses were employed during the peak of the epidemic. There |
are 11 patients in the Infirmary: 8 Crocker and 3 subsidized. In
the new budget the sum allocated-for medical and dental fees was
increased from $175.00 to $300. Children's Hospital gives us a
35% discount on X-rays, lab work, the operating room, etc. The bill

at Children's Hospital from February 15th to March 4th was $215.00,

The Committee recommended that:

1. Bills for ambulance service and hospital bills be kept as
separate items, :

2. Bills for ambulance services, outside hospital care and dental
care be sent to the Finance Committee to be presented to the
Board of Directors as a special item.

3. Salary increases voted for the Infirmary staff begin on March Ist.

Mrs, Smith stated that the salaries of the nurses had been raised
from $175.00 to $200.00 monthly.

Personnel - Mrs, Kirkwood, Chairman, stated that the Committee recom-
mends the increase in salary, commencing on April 1st, for:. |

1, Miss Margaret Long from $350.00 to $375.00 monthly.
2. Mrs, Anna Pogson - an increase of $50.00 monthly.

It was moved, seconded and carried that the increases of salary
recommended by the Personnel Committee be accepted.

Mrs, Kirkwood noted the following changes: Miss Liston will take

full charge on Saturdays and Sundays and 4 evenings a week, She will
assist at the Birthday Parites, Miss Long will take the week-day

noon hours, Miss Liston's salary will remain the same but she will
occupy a large corner room with bath in the new building. The Com-
mittee is interviewing applicants for Secretary for the hours from 9-5.

Finance - Miss Bridge, Chairman, stated that the Finance Committee

recommends to the Board of Managers: :

1. That the amount left in the Handicraft Fund be transferred to
the Special Fund in the Director's Account, to be used for teas
and special recreational activities. ;

2, ‘That any gift given the Heritage shall be deposited in the Special
Fund to be drawn out as designated by the Board of Managers.

It was moved, seconded and carried that the amount in the Handicraft
Fund be transferred to the Special Fund.
