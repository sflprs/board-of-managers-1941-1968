MAY 1, 1956

The regular meeting of the San Francisco Ladies and Protection
and Relief Society was held T uesday, May 1st, at the Home.
Mrs. Warren Perry presiding. Those present were Mesdames Miller,
Charles Smith, Landis, Byrne, Bowes, Nigh, Stephenson, Lyon, Craig,
Towne, Krick, Bruck and Miss Rolph.

A brief devotional period was observed.

The Corresponding Secretary, Mrs. Stephenson, Reported writing
one letter.

The minutes of the previous meeting were read and approved.

The Treasurer, Mrs. Hilbert gave the financial report for March.

Income $6989.50
Expenses 7032.27
Bank Bal. March ist. 7493.70
Bank Bal. March 31st. 7294.69

FAMILY COMMITTEE: Mrs. Krick reported that the Family have
been quite well during April. Mrs. Kingsbury needs some dental and
optometry care which will be charged to the "Doctors and Hospital
Fund". Mrs. Craig brought up the question of care for a Mrs. Taylor,
aged 85, who can go to the Crocker Home now if assured of future care
here. It was decided to have this matter considered by the Family
Committee as well as the Policy Committee, and a recommendation pre-
sented at a later date. It was pointed out that the Convalescent
Department might take Mrs. Taylor.

Mrs. Stuart Smith reported that Miss Bowley's collection of
wild flowers received recognition at a meeting held at the Civic
Auditorium.

Mrs. Krick called the attention of the Board tothe fact that
Miss Pooley and Mrs. Searight have been in the Home for 6 months.
It was moved by Mrs. Nigh and seconded by Mrs. Miller, carried, that
they be asked to consult the Trustees concerning their affairs. A dis-
cussion was held on this matter and it was recommended that the Policy
Committee set a definate policy for the future.

CONVALESCENT COMMITTEE: Mrs. Bowes gave her report for March,
as follows:- ~
Available bed days 558
Occupied bed days 427
Free Bed days 35
Income $1077.00

ENTERTAINMENT COMMITTEE: It was announced that on the evening
of June 23rd, at 7.30 o'clock, Dr. and Mrs. Kroll will show their
latest pictures at the Home.

HOME FURNISHINGS: Mrs. Towne reported that Mme. Olivier's room
has been painted; a chair and footstool done over for Miss Knowlton;
and a request made for two standard lights in the TV room.

GARDEN COMMITTEE: Mrs. Hilbert reported that the garden con-
tinues to look lovely.

HOME ACTIVITIES: Mrs. Byrne reported that work for the Bazaar
is well started; and asked the Board for any old pillows.
