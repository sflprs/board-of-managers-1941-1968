Nurse - The report of the Head Nurse, Mrs. Rood, from September 2h,

1957, to October 1h, 1957, was read by the Secretary. Mrs. Rood
reported on the patients treated in the infirmary by Dr. McKenzie
and noted those dismissed to nursing homes. She stated that an
inventory has been taken and all medicines have been signed and
accounted for. This report was accepted in response to a motion
by Mrs. Blackie, seconded by Mrs. Miller.
