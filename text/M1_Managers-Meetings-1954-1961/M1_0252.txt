Personnel: No report.

Dining Room: Mrs. Porter, Chairman, reported cost of food per
person per day 84.6 cents. More meat has been added to the menus,

as suggested last month. It was moved, seconded, and carried that
Mr. Debes, Food Consultant, be retained for 1962.

Building and Maintenance: Miss Bridge stated the committee was
going to make a survey of need and cost of a kitchenette in the
Hobby Room.

Garden: Mrs. Hilbert, Chairman, absent -- No report.

Library: Mrs. Matignon, Chairman, absent -- no report.

Rooms and Furnishings: In the absence of Mrs. Blackie, Chairman,
Mrs. Nigh acknowledged the gift by Mrs. Bruck of a beautiful
screen now in place in the office.

Visiting: Mrs. Lyon, Chairman, reported not as much visiting as
usual, but that everyone seemed in good spirits.

Corresponding Secretary: In the absence of Mrs. Skene, Mrs. Lloyd
read a letter of resignation from Mrs. Granger. It was moved,
seconded, and carried that the resignation be accepted with regret.

Letters of thanks for salary raises were read from Mrs. Rathman
and Miss Zolezzi.

Qld Business: Mrs. Nigh reported that she and Mrs. Pogson had
attended a meeting of the Association of Northern California

Homes, held at the Danish Home in San Rafael on November 28, 1961,
and at that meeting it was agreed that Dr. Louis Kuplan be employed
as permanent consultant and legislative representative of the
Association at a salary of $500.00 for the year 1962. Also Dr.
Kuplan will advise The Heritage of laws pertinent thereto, and
provide other consultant services. Mrs. Nigh further reported

that she had attended that portion of the White House Regional
Conference dealing with San Francisco housing and medical care.

There being no further business, the meeting was adjourned.
