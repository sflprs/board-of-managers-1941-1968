Mrs. Miller then read the letter to be sent to all doctors of monthly
guests:

Dear Dr. 3

To facilitate the medical and nursing care the merged Boards
of the San Francisco Ladies Protection and Relief Society of 3,00
Laguna Street and the Old People's Home of 2507 Pine Street have
adopted the following policies:

1. All doctors treating private patients are requested to stop at
the Nurse's Station and obtain the patient's chart before calling
on the patient. If it is desired, a nurse will accompany the
doctor during his visit.

2. All medications prescribed are to be given under the supervision
of the nursing staff.

3. If desired by the doctor, a certain amount of geriatric care, i.e.,
shampooing, bathing, skin care, can be given to patients by the
nursing staff.

he The doctor is requested to inform the nursing staff of any patients
who may require emergency treatment so that they may be kept
under special observation.

Very truly yours,

Mrs. Porter suggested that the administrator as well as the nurse
be notified by the doctor of a paying guest when he enters the
building.

Mrs. Landis moved that the Interim Policies as recommended by the
Infirmary Committee be accepted. This was seconded by Irs. Miller
and carried.
