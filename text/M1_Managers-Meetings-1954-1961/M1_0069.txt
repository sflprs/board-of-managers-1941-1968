Building Committee: Mrs. Craig reported that a fourth floor was
being considered by the Committee and the Trustees. Highteen more
guests or patients could be accommodated. A Smoke tower would be
necessary at an approximate cost of $8,000.00. The Henry Cowell
Estate is to be approached for a contribution. lrs. Craig pointed

if investéd at 4% would bring in $5320. If these rooms were rented
at 200.00 a month, they would realize $430,000 in a year and the
total cost could be paid off very rapidly. Mrs. Blackie read a re-
solution as follows;- "That the Board of Managers urge the Board

of Directors to give consideration to adding a fourth floor to the
new wing now being erected at 3400 Laguna Street. This would give

us facilities for caring for approximately 100 persons, the economic
unit for operating this type of institution. Living, dining, and
kitchen units now being constructed will do for this number. If it
is not possible to obtain a donation from the Cowell Estate, we ask
that other methods of financing be looked into. The additional rooms
could be rented to those in great need of our services, and the in-
come from these rooms would pay within four to five. years for the
cost of constructing the fourth floor." it was moved,by Mrs. Porter,
