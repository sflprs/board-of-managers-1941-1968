NOVEMBER 1, 1955

The regular meeting of the Ladies Protection and Relief
Society was held November 1st at the Home. Mrs. Warren Perry
presiding. Present were Mesdames Hilbert, Craig, Landis, Lyon,
Stuart Smith, Bowes, Nigh, Byrne, Charles Smith, Stephenson and
the Misses Rolph and Zane.

A brief Devotiona] period was observed.
There was no correspondence.

The minutes of the previous meeting were read and approved.

Treasurer's Report: Mrs. Hilbert gave the financial report for
September, which was as follows:
Income 7095.00
Expenses 6932.78

Bank Balance September 1st. 8803.19
Bank Balance September 30th. 8891.49

The Family are to be notified that Pensions will be in-
creased by $5.00 per month.

Family Committee: Mrs. Perry asked that the Board forward their
cheques for the Christmas Fund before December ist.

Mrs. Krick, Mrs. Lyon and Mrs. Stuart Smith have called on the
Family and find them quite well and happy.

Convalescent Committee: Mrs. Bowes reported that the Department

is rurming smoothly, although not full.

Available Bed Days 600
Occupied Bed Days 449 or 74.8%
Free Bed Days 76
Income $858.00

Mrs. Bowes told the Board that nowadays the convalescents are

not too enthusiastic about receiving calls from the Board. It
was decided that the future policy would be to call only on those
patients who have asked for callers, or would enjoy callers in the
opinion of Miss Ash and Miss Moore.

Follow-up-Fund: Miss Rolph reported that one of the convalescents
is receiving training at the May Morrison Center to help overcome
spastic disabilities. The Center will help her to finda job. It
was moved by Miss Rolph, seconded by Mrs. Landis and carried to
give the convalescent $25.00 a month for six months.

Entertainment: Miss Zane is planning another bingo game party
between Thanksgiving and Christmas; and with the Entertainment
Committee will start making plans for the Christmas party.

Bazaar Committee: Mrs. Byrne was given a vote of thanks for the
wonderful success of the Bazaar, and for her untiring work in
planning towards that success. Mrs. Byrne then reported that
all expenses are not yet in, but that the Bazaar grossed $1360.72.
Seven of the help in the Home worked all day to make the Bazaar a
success, and it was voted to give each of the seven $5.00 in apprec-
