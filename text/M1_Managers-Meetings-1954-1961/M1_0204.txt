Entertainmenment - Mrs. Lloyd, Chairman, showed the Board a picture
of an Ampex Stereo Record Player and Stereo Radio and described them
in detail. They are encased in a handsome walnut cabinet, mounted on |
large brass casters to enable it to be moved easily. A standing mi-
crophone can be plugged in at any time to convert the set to a pub-

lic address system. It has an excellent tone. Two members of the
Board have approved it.

It was moved, seconded and carried that this instrument with the mi-
crophone be purchased for $1,048.84. A plaque will be placed on the
inside of cabinet door stating that it is a gift from Mr. Stetson G.
Hindes. Mrs. Lloyd was requested to write a letter to Mr. Hindes
stating the appreciation of the Board for his generous gift.
