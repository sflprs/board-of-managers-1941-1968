August 29, 1956

A special meeting of the Finance and Policy Committees was
held et the Home on August 29th. lirs. Warren Perry was in the
chair. Present were the Mesdames Towne, Krick, Hilbert, Craig,
Leavitt, Miller, Nigh, Stuart Smith, Landis, Porter and the
Misses Rolph and Bridge.

Mrs. Towne brought up the matter of building a fourth floor.
It was pointed out that 100 rooms is the nationally known ec-
onomic unit. Also, it was felt that possibly one additional
nurse and one maid could take care of the added eighteen guests.
in order to build as planned, $750,000 will be the cost of build-
ing the present plan of sixty-one rooms. %150,000 to %175,000
additional will be needed for a fourth floor. The Henry Cowell
sstete is to be approached for a donation.

A letter incorporating the following motion was sent to
Capt. Barrett Hindes.

(1) That the fourth floor is definately desired by the
Board of Managers.

(2) That a joint meeting of the Trustees and the Board
of Managers be held before a definate decision is make by
the Trustees regarding the fourth floor.

(3) That the Trustees employ Mr. Anderson to help Mrs. Craig
make an estimated cost of operating the eighteen additional
rooms and future budget.

(4) That no initiation fee be considered for boarders.
(5) That the Board of Managers wants the fourth floor be-

cause it will make a nationally recognized economic operation
and we can therefore provide more facilities to do the work

we wish to do.

(6) That our ultimate objective is to furnish restricted
life care.

There being no further business the meeting adjourned.

Respectfully submitted,
