October 17, 1961

The regular meeting of the Board of Managers of the San Francisco
Ladies Protection and Relief Society was held on Tuesday, October
LT; 1961, at 3400 Laguna Street at 10:30 A.M., the President, Mrs.
William H. Nigh, Jr., presiding.

Present were: Mesdames Craig, Blackie, Porter, Nigh, Bruck, Hilbert,
Krick, unther, Skene, Morrison, Perry, Stephenson, Matignon,
Charles Smith, Noble, Landis, and Misses Rolph and Bridge.

Absent (with excuses) were: Mesdames Bowes, Rettenmeyer, Lloyd, 4
Kirkwood, Granger, and Lyon. &

Mrs. Nigh spoke of the death of Mr. Paul Newell, a member of the
Board of Directors.

The minutes of the regular meeting of the Board of Managers of
September 19, 1961, were approved as read.

Miss Bridge read the minutes of a special meeting of the Board of
Directors of Thursday, September 14, 1961.. These minutes noted
with regret the death of Mr. Bruce Cornwall, a former director; |

The Secretary of the Board of Managers was instructed to write to
Mr. Bradley Brown, regretting his resignation and thanking him for
all that he has done for the Board of Managers, as well as for the
Society.

Mrs. Nigh then introduced Mr. Thomas M. Jenkins, the attorney for
the Society, who presented, a detailed explanation of our Life Care
Contracts and our Trust Agreement Contracts, and answered pertinent
questions. He agreed to draw up a brief outline of the important
points in theTrust Agreement to be given applicants to clarify,
their understanding of the contract.

TREASURER: Miss Bridge, Treasurer, read the Financial Report, noting
a deficit for September of $863.99

Operating Balance ; September 30, 1961 $8,849.97

It was moved, seconded, and carried that the Treasurer's report be
placed on file.

ADMINISTRATOR: Mrs. Pogson reported there are no new residents.
Mrs. Splivalo and Miss Brewer completed their periods of probation.
Miss Helen Moore has left The Heritage to live with a friend. 2
Questionnaires will be sent to 190 applicants for Life Care.

COMMITTEE REPORTS

Infirmary: Mrs. Morrison, Chairman, reported on a special meeting,
September 19, 1961, to discuss the crowded Infirmary. Attending
were Mrs. Nigh, Mrs. Gunther, Miss Bridge, Mr. Charles Bradley and
Mr. Joseph Moore. Mr. Bradley and Mr. Moore agreed on the need

for a new infirmary. Mr. Bradley suggested a study of the situation
be made by a qualified person working with the Board of Managers.

It was agreed we should not resume the admission of Boarders. The
Department of Public Health has licensed us to care for 18 persons
