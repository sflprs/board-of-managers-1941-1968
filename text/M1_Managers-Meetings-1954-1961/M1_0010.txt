8. Ques. Are we not leaning too much on promises of future benefits

offset by the hazards of the stock and real estate markets

and increasing costs of institutional care?
Ans. Acceptance of the contract by Crocker states that they
will give "satisfactory guarantee" of the $800,000. for
stock and real estate assets, and that the Mary A. Crocker
trustees give all possible legal and moral guarantee of
the $150,000. Both the B of T and B of M will have the
judgment as to what is satisfactory. Any bequests would
be additional to the merged endowment funds. Regarding
increased costs of care, it is probable that the old age
pensions will increase somewhat with the cost of living
and more revenue could thus be gotten from the pensioners.

9. Ques. Afraid that the financial problems arising from this merger
Might destroy the serenity of the home's operation and

= perhaps cause financial harassment and unpleasantnesses.

financially able guests at higher rates just as the
Crocker home now does. There is no reason for the seren-
ity of the family or guests or the comfort to be disturbed
under the intelligent supervision of the B of M in co-
ordination with the B of T.
