Judge Johnson said that he wished that a larger income could be obtained, in order to spread our work further, and build on, to have more space for the work which he praised as being excellent.

Mrs Krick told of a few outstanding events in the year, summarizing briefly, and paying a tribute to Miss Ash. The Trustees then withdrew.

The corresponding secretary reported that she had written 17 letters, mostly of thanks for gifts.

The secretary read the annual report.

The election was held.

Mrs Krick appointed.
    Judges: Miss Woods &
            Mrs Lyon
    Tellers: Mrs Hindes &
             Mrs Pierson

The secretary read the ballot.

Mrs Craig moved, Miss Rolph seconded the motion, that the nominations be closed. It was so voted.

It was moved and carried that the secretary cast the ballot.

The meeting adjourned and reconvened as a new Board.

Miss Rolph read the list of investments.

Mrs Krick said a few words.

                        The meeting adjourned.
                        Margaret James Porter, Secretary
