[Book page 22]

(1) Judge Edgar Zook [?] telling us of a gift of $5,000 from Miss Rowena Beans in memory of her sister.

(2) Mr. Hutchinson expressing his regret at being unable to attend the annual meeting, and reporting that the Fay [?] Mausoleum plot is in excellent condition.

(3) Mrs. E. Lawrence Barnes thanking us for the flowers we had sent to the Edgewood celebration of their one hundredth anniversary.

The Secretary read the annual Report. Mrs. Towne moved, Mrs. Byrne seconded, and the Board approved a motion that this report be accepted.

Mrs. Lyon and Mrs. Craig were appointed judges of the Annual Meeting, and Mrs. Pierson and Mrs. Barnes were appointed tellers.

The Secretary read the ballot. Mrs. Craig moved and Mrs. Perry seconded, and the Board approved a motion that the nominations be closed. The Secretary cast the ballot. Mrs. Towne moved and Mrs. Stephenson seconded a motion that the meeting be adjourned. It was a vote and so ordered.

The meeting reconvened as the new Board for the coming year.

Mrs. Krick paid tribute to Miss Ash. She spoke of the way Miss Ash keeps track of the many regulations and of her gracious and kind approa[cut off at edge of photo] to the work at the Home. Mrs. Krick thanked the Committee Chairmen and members of the Board for their work. It was moved by Mrs. Pierson and seconded by Mrs. Craig and voted that messages be sent from the annual meeting to Miss Allyne, Mrs. Hindes, and Mrs. Humphreys.

Miss Woods spoke of the blessings that fall on the Society, and expressed our gratitude at having a leader who can carry on our traditions.

The Vice-Presidents were asked to stay and meet with the President after the meeting to appoint the chairmen of the committees.

                        The meeting adjourned.
                        Respectfully submitted,
                        Barbara Olney Landis,
                             Secretary.
