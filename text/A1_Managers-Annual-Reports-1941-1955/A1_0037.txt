February 7th, 1952

The ninety-eighth annual meeting of the San Francisc[cut off] Ladies Protection and Relief Society was held at the Home at two o'clock, the President of the Board of Managers, Mrs. Krick presiding. Present were: Mrs. Cambron, Mrs. Craig, Mrs. Krick, Mrs. Knight Smith, Mrs. Miller, Mrs. Barnes, Mrs. Lyon, Mrs. Perry, Mrs. Allen, Mrs. Krusi, Mrs. Stuart Smith, Mrs. Landis, Miss Ally[cut off] Miss Woods, Miss Rolph, Miss Zane. Mrs. Pierson and Mrs. B[cut off] were unable to attend and sent proxies.

Present were the Trustees: Mr. Bruce Cornwall, Mr. Joseph A. Moore, Jr., Mr. David White, Mr. Edwin V. Krick, Mr. Barrett Hindes.

Mrs. Krick greeted the Board of Managers and the Trustees and explained to the new members of the Board of Managers that these two Boards form the Society.

Mrs. Krick then introduced the four new members of the Board of Managers: Mrs. Edward Hohfeld [?], Mrs. James H. Allen, Mrs. Ellen Hindes Krusi and Miss Margaret Zane.

Mrs. Krick paid tribute to Mrs. Stetson [?] Hindes, who passed away on August 30, 1951, and expressed her pleasure that Mrs. Hindes' daughter, Mrs. Krusi, was a member of the Board of Managers. Mrs. Krick told the Society that Mrs. Allen was the second generation of the Gibbons family to serve the Society.

The reading of the minutes of the last annual meeting was deferred until after the Trustees with-drew.

After the Financial Report was read, Mrs. Krick explained that there is a net gain for the year of $16,558. Last year when we asked the Trustees for $8,000 and an increase of $1,500 a month for operating expenses we had allowed for all possible "contingencies." Of this amount $4,000 is being used for repairs on the building and $4,000 has been kept in the bank to insure a balance of $5,000. We are not returning the surplus money to the Trustees at this time because we hope to have a celebration in 1953 to commemorate the hundredth anniversary of the Society; also, one member of the "Family" requires expensive care in a convalescent home. The Red Cross has requested us to find a haven for the "Fami[cut off] and the staff in case of an emergency. The garden and furnishings in the Home require additional expenditures.
