The Eighty-eighth Annual meeting of the Directors and Board of Managers of the San Francisco Ladies Protection and Relief Society was held at the Home on February 5th, 1942, at two o'clock. The president, Mrs Craig, presided. Those present were: Mesdames:
    Deering
    Lyon
    Krick
    Humphreys
    Cameron
    Hindes
    Clough &
    Porter
The Misses
    Alleyne
    Beans
    Rolph &
    Woods
Messrs.:
    Cornwall
    Livermore &
    Hutchinson.

The reading of the minutes of last year's meeting were omitted.

Mrs Clough read the treasurer's report.
    December 31st, 1940, Balance: $5,022.02
    Receipts: $25,200.00
    Home: $4,616.22
    Convalescents: $5,508.00
    Donations: $1,604.00
    Total receipts: $42,012.24
    December 31st, 1941, Balance: $6,469.00
In the Special account:
    December 31st, 1940, Balance: $3,927.46
    Disbursements: $1,801.00
    December 31st, 1941, Balance: $2,126.46

The Corresponding Secretary reported that she had written twenty-two letters during the year, chiefly of appreciation for gifts or services to the Home.
