February 5th., 1948

The ninety-fourth annual meeting of the Directors and Board of Managers of the San Francisco Ladies Protection and Relief Society was held at the Home at two o'clock, the President, Mrs Krick, presiding.

Present were, Mesdames:
    Craig
    Cambron (Honorary)
    Knight Smith
    Humphrey
    Towne
    Stephenson
    Pierson
    Fairlie
    Lyon
    Hindes
    Clough
    Perry
    Stuart Smith
    Miller
    Landis &
    Porter.
The Misses:
    Allyne
    Beans
    Brown
    Rolph &
    Woods
The Trustees:
    Mr Caldwell
    Mr Krick
    Mr Moore &
    Mr Leicester.

Mrs Krick greeted the meeting and spoke with regret of the death of Judge Walter Perry Johnson

It was voted that the reading of last year's minutes be omitted.

Miss Rolph read the treasurer's report.
    For 1947 - Receipts: $58,268.36
    Disbursements: $52,882.18
    For the Francisco Street flats. $1,719, and $1,075.
    December 31st, balance: $2,592.70
The treasurer's report was accepted.
