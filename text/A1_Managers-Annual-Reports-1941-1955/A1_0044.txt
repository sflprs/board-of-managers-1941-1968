In conclusion Mrs Perry told of an interesting afternoon with Mr. Hutchinson, who answered questions of the Board concerning the Hawes will, and policies of the Society.

Mrs Perry asked Mr. Hindes for the report of the Board of Trustees.

Mr. Hindes stated that the funds of the Society have improved more than usual because of the selectivity of investments. Treasury bonds have been sold in favor of those with higher yield. All sources of investment and rentals have totalled $77,500, or $3,500 more than the gross income for the previous year.

Mr. Hindes further stated that in looking at the over all picture of the Home - the size of the establishment in relation to the number of people cared for - he wondered if some addition to the building could be made to enable the Society to care for more people without much additional expense. However it would not be possible to build this addition from capital funds without reducing the Society's income beyond a "good and intelligent" balance. Noting that donations for the year, exclusive of those for the 100th anniversary, totalled only $481, Mr. Hindes commented that the Society should have a broader base to build larger interest to bring in donations. Mr. Hindes stated that the new By-Laws limit the number of people connected with the Society, and suggested sustaining memberships to create a new interest in our work in the community. At present there are only 35 people in the Society.

In conclusion Mr. Hindes stated that he would prefer to see the membership broadened rather than narrowed, and felt that in order to increase our work, it will be necessary to have some money from the outside to build a new wing and to add to our Trust fund.

Mr. Hindes asked the Trustees for their opinions.

Mr. Brown stated that it was satisfactory to be connected with a Society which provides a

[Book page 31]

need unmatched by any other organization. Mr. Brown expressed the belief that it would be a mistake to restrict the number of members, and stated that an organization grows and prospers only as it keeps up with the needs of the community, and should reach out to get an active part in the community.

Mr. White said he had no comment to make as Mr Hindes had covered everything, but added that he was glad to have Mr Hindes as President of the Board of Trustees.

Mr. Leicester stated his pleasure at being at the meeting and had nothing to add to Mr. Hindes' report.

Mrs. Perry thanked the Trustees in behalf of the Board of Managers, and the Trustees withdrew.

The Secretary read the minutes of the 99th annual meeting. They were accepted as read.

The Secretary then read the 100th annual report. This was accepted as read.

Mrs Pierson and Mrs Barnes were appointed judges of the annual meeting, and Mrs Craig and Mrs. Allen were appointed tellers.

The Secretary read the ballot. Mrs. Stephenson moved and Mrs Miller seconded a motion that the nominations be closed. The Secretary then cast the ballot. Upon a motion seconded and carried the meeting was adjourned.

The meeting was reconvened as a new Board for the coming year.

Mrs Perry thanked the Committee Chairmen and members of the Board for their work. The Vice Presidents were asked to stay and meet with the President after the meeting to appoint the Committee Chairmen.

The meeting was adjourned with a prayer.

                        Respectfully submitted,
                        Barbara Olney Landis
                             Recording Secretary.
