February 4. 1954

The 100th annual meeting of the San Francisco Ladies Protection and Relief Society was held at the Home at 2:00 PM; the President, Mrs. Warren Perry, presiding. Present were Mesdames: Cambron, Knight-Smith, Stuart Smith, Allen, Krusi, Miller, Craig, Lyon, Pierson, Byrne, Barnes, Hilbert, Charles Smith, Perry, Krick, Nigh [?], Stephenson, Landis; the Misses Allyne and Rolph.

The Trustees present were Messrs: Hindes, Brown, White, Leicester & Graham.

Mrs. Perry opened the meeting with a tribute to the memory of Mr. Norman Livermore, who had served the Society for 35 years.

After greeting the Trustees, Mrs. Perry introduced the new members of the Board of Managers, Mrs. William Henshaw [?] Nigh [?] and Mrs. Charles Edward Smith.

The minutes of the last annual meeting were ordered deferred until after the Trustees withdrew.

The Treasurer read the financial report:
    Balance in bank - Dec. 31, 1952 $16,830.68
    Total receipts 86,115.11
    " disbursements 91,654.22
    Balance in bank - Dec 31, 1953 11,291.57
This report was ordered placed on file.

Mrs. Perry then introduced Mr. Hindes, President of the Board of Trustees. Mr. Hindes stated his pleasure at being a member of the Society and introduced Mr. Chaffers [?] Graham, a new Trustee.

Mrs. Perry gave the report of a very successful year. The "Family" had kept fairly well considering their average age is 80 years. This relieving us of the expense of doctors' bills and extra nurses. Mrs. Hole [?] passed away; and

[Book page 29]

Mrs. de Mari [?] had been placed in the Casa Bella Sanatarium. Mrs. Laura [?] Mercado [?] is a new member of the Family.

During the year there had been many improvements in the Home. The dining room and kitchen had been painted, glass doors put in the showers, new fire alarm boxes, and a new bigger [?] communication [?] installed. The year had seen the purchase of a new automobile, a kitchen stove, and furniture for two sun rooms. Mrs. Pierson [?] had given a lamp, Mrs. Nigh [?] a shade for the lamp, Mrs Krusi an arm chair for the hall.

On April 18th the 100th anniversary of the Society had been commemorated with a reception attended by 800 people. Gifts and good wishes showered on the Home, and gifts of money amounted to $1706. Mrs Towne provided the stationery for the engraved invitations and a handsome leather bound guest book. The reception cost $1000. The history of the Society, "In as Much [?]," compiled from the minutes by Miss Rowena Beans and edited by Carol Green Wilson, had been completed in time for the reception.

In spite of these expenses, we had been able to return $10,000 to the Trustees. The main reason for this surplus had been the added income from the increased rates of the Convalescent Department. However free care is continuing to be given to those who require it.

A luncheon in honor of Miss Ash and Miss Helen Moore had been given to commemorate their 20 years of service to the Home. At that time Miss Ash's salary had been raised to $400 a month, and Miss Moore*, who is on half time duty had her salary increased to $225 per month.

Mrs. Perry spoke of the generous gift of stock from Mrs. Stetson Hindes; and the bequest of three small houses in Mill Valley and some furniture from Miss Pooley [?] and Mrs Seawright [?] upon their death.

During the year Miss Lottie [?] G. Woods had been elected an Honorary Life Member in tribute to her many years of service and devotion to the Society. The resignation of Mrs George Beaver [?] because of ill health had been accepted with regret.
