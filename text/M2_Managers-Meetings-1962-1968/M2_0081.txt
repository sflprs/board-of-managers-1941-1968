Building and Maintenance: Miss Bridge, Chairman, said she had no fur-
ther report than that included under Finance and Personnel.

Garden: Mrs. Porter, Chairman, reported the plans for the new Infir-
mary were out for bids. A plan made by Landscape Architect Casey
Kawamota for the Pocket Garden was shown. Mrs. Porter stressed that
every effort had been made to save existing trees and make the area
usable. These plans are out for bids. Mrs. Porter and Mrs. Nigh had
met with Mr. Dailey to discuss saving as much planting as possible
when work on the Infirmary begins. The moving, or scrapping, of the
Sun House was brought up and, after discussion, it was moved, seconded
and carried that the architect be given a free hand in this matter.

Nominating Report for New Members: Mrs. Charles Smith, Chairman,
absent; no report.

Cor. Secretary: Mrs. Stephenson read a letter from Mrs. Wanda Hoeft
thanking THE HERITAGE for the wonderful care she had received follow-
ing surgery at the Children's Hospital. Mrs. Bruck and Mrs. Hilbert
both requested six months! leave of absence. This was granted.

Rooms and Furnishings: Mrs. Blackie, Chairman, called attention to the
fine new lights in the Theatre Room. She said no further decoration
will be planned for this room until the work on the parking lot direct-

ly outside its windows is finished. The Fourth Floor Sitting Room has
been successfully redecorated with materials on hand.

Visiting: Mrs. Craig reported she.went to call on Mr. Crothers at
Children's Hospital. Mrs. Perry has a list of residents who really
enjoy callers.

Mrs. Skene was thanked again for "Heritage Happenings", which has
grown to two sheets.

New Business: Mrs. Nigh reported she attended the opening of a Day
Treatment Center at the Presbyterial Hospital. On June 27 Mrs. Pog-
son and Mrs. Nigh will go to the meeting of the Association of North-
ern California Homes at The Sequoias.

It was moved, seconded and carried to omit the Board of Managers!
meeting in July.

As there was no further business, the meeting was adjourned.
