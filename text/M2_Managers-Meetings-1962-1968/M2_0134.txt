August 22, 1967

The regular monthly meeting of the Board of Managers of the
San Francisco Ladies' Protection and Relief Society was held
Tuesday, August 22, 1967, at 10:30 A.M. at 3400 Laguna Street,
San Francisco, Mrs. Jack S. Logan presiding.

PRESENT: Miss ‘Bridge and Mesdames Bailey, Bowes, Bruck, Cox,
Craig, Gunther, Howard, Logan, Perry, Pinney, Porter, Rettenmayer,
Schilling, Charles Smith, Knight Smith, and Stephenson.

EXCUSED: Mesdames Bean, Houston, Kent, Krick, Marshall, Metcalf,
Nigh, Nishkian, Noble, and Staples.

The minutes of the June 27, 1967 meeting were read and approved
as corrected.

TREASURER'S REPORT: Miss Bridge reported that costs are going
up, particularly in salaries, provisions, meat, drugs, medical
costs, and maintenance.

Per capita cost for June of 105 residents........$ 400.00
Balance in Operating Bank Account, June 30.......$9,911.00

Per capita cost for July of 106 residents........$ 363.00
Balance in Operating Bank Account, July 31.......$6,398.00

It was moved, seconded and carried that the Treasurer's Report
be accepted and placed on file.

CORRESPONDENCE: In the absence of Mrs. Nishkian, Mrs. Pinney
read a letter from Mrs. Fesca, who thanked the Board for pay-
ment for the slip covers she had made.

COMMITTEE REPORTS:

FINANCE AND PERSONNEL: Miss Bridge reported that the cost of
the Intercom System repair and reconstruction was $2,100.00.
Upon recommendatinn of the Committee, and following discussion,
it was moved, seconded and carried that:

1. $300.00 a year be added to the Social and Recreation Budget
for expenditures necessary for the work of the Social Director,
such expenses to be approved by the Administrator.

2. $150.00 a year be added for tours for residents.

3. A chair be repaired at a cost of $195.00, $105.00 of
which has been donated by Miss Brewer.

4. New book shelves be put in the library at a cost of
$175.00; to be refinished by our painting contractor.

5. Doors in halls be fixed so that they automatically close
in case of fire. This was recommended by the fire inspector
and ADT Service. Swinerton and Walberg Co. have bid $2,540.00.

6. Twenty-two windows and the front door be put in working
order by Mr. Sommers at a cost of $1,144.00;(104 working hours
estimated).

7. An additional clothes dryer be purchased at a cost of
$495.00 plus installation. More laundry is now being done
at The Heritage, which constitutes a saving.

Mrs. Schilling reported that the following matters are under
consideration by the Committee: the retirement and replacement
of our Head Nurse, Mrs. Hallum; increasing salaries in the upper
nursing brackets; the eventual retirement and replacement of

our Medical Director, Dr. McKenzie. Specific recommendations
will be forthcoming.
