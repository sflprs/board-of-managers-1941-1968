Visiting and Library: Mrs. Craig reported all is well with those

She visits. Mrs. Lloyd reported the Library in good order, several
new books on hand.

Occupational Activities and Entertainment: Mrs. Rettenmayer,
Chairman, said there had been 15 entertainment programs in
February. Six symphony seats are provided for every Thursday's
performance. Tickets had been donated for the Gaslight Gaities,
62 had enjoyed the St. Patrick's Day Birthday Party. A fine
assortment of Bingo prizes had been given by the Livingston
store. Mrs. Rettenmayer showed what her working Group had

been doing, crocheting repairs on articles for the Home, and
she demonstrated the construction of the exquisite “angels"
being made for next Christmas.

Correspondence and Heritage Happenings: Mrs. Charles Smith said
she had written Mrs. Lawry the Board would welcome her back
whenever she felt her health would allow it. The most recent
issue of Heritage Happenings came out early in March.

New Business: Mrs. Scott Knight Smith appointed a Nominating
Committee for new officers: Mrs. Craig, Chairman; Mrs. Bowes,
Mrs. Lyon, Mrs. Noble and Mrs. Perry.

It was moved, seconded and carried these members serve on
the Committee.

There being no further business the meeting was adjourned.

Respectfully submitted,

Frances Lloyd
Acting Secretary
