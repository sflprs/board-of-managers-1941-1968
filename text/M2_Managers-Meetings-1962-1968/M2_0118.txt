RECREATION: Mrs. Rettenmayer reported that, in addition
to the regularly scheduled programs of movies, lectures and
concerts, residents have attended the San Francisco Symphony
with tickets arranged for by Mrs. Metcalf. Forty residents
attended a meeting of the tempoary committee for The Heritage
Council, a group being formed by the residents. Mrs. Casper,
our social director, took a group of residents to a concert.
Other activities included a birthday luncheon which included
all residents who came to the dining room, a bingo game, and

a variety show.

LIBRARY: Mrs. Kent reported the following new books:
four fiction; one non-fiction, three detective; one history,
Copies of Pacific Discovery and National Wildlife would be
appreciated.

"HERITAGE HAPPENINGS": Mrs. Charles Smith reported that
the January issue is published, and that there will be another
issue when more material is received.

VISITING: Mrs. Perry reported that she made several visits
including one to the Infirmary. Mrs. Logan urged Board members
to visit residents whenever possible.
