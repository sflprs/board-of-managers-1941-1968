January 23, 1968

The regular monthly meeting of the Board of Managers of the

San Francisco Ladies' Protection and Relief Society was held
at 3400 Laguna Street, San Francisco, on Tuesday, January 23
1968, at 10:30 A.M. The President of the Board of Managers ;
Mrs. Jack S. Logan, presided at the meeting. ,

PRESENT: Miss Bridge and Mesdames Bailey, Bowes, Bruck, Craig
Gunther, Houston, Howard, Kent, Logan, Nigh, Noble, Perry, ,
Porter, Rettenmayer, Schilling, Charles Smith, Knight Smith,
and Staples.

EXCUSED: Mesdames Bean, Cox, Krick, Marshall, Metcalf,
Nishkian, Pinney and Stephenson.

The minutes of the November 28, 1967, meeting of the Board

of Managers were read and approved as corrected. The
minutes of the two special meetings of the Board of Directors
held on October 31, 1967, and December 6, 1967, were read.

TREASURER'S REPORT: Miss Bridge reported:

Per Capita Cost for December, 1967, of 114 residents..$ 437.00
Balance in Operating Bank Account,December 31, 1967..$15,305.00.

Budget appropriation--$33,983.00. Overexpended by $11,302.00.
It was moved, seconded and carried that the Treasurer's Report
be accepted and placed on file.

CORRESPONDENCE: In the absence of Mrs. Nishkian, Mrs. Logan
reported that all correspondence is up to date. Mrs. Gunther
volunteered to act as corresponding secretary in Mrs.Nishkian's
absence. Many letters of appreciation for the Christmas Party
have been received.

COMMITTEE REPORTS:

FINANCE AND PERSONNEL: Miss Bridge reported that the
Board of Directors approved the proposed payroll budget
of $299,627.00, to start as of January 1, 1968, at their
special meeting held on December 6, 1967. Upon recom-
mendation of the Committee, it was moved, seconded and
carried that a proposed budget for 1968 for 12 months
of $529,807.00 and a separate capital outlay budget of
$3,987.50 be recommended to the Board of Directors. It
was emphasized that the proposed 1968 budget should cover
everything, so that an overexpenditure in one place must
be balanced by an underexpenditure somewhere else. It
was suggested that residents be encouraged to join Medi-
care Plan B to pay for drugs and other costs covered by
this Plan and reimburse The Heritage for such expenses.

ADMISSIONS: Mrs. Nigh reported that Miss Berthe D'Aubigny,

8, has moved in on probation. Mrs. Eva Alves, 81, and
Mrs. Frances Stacy, 72, are on the waiting list. Mrs. Ruth
Hall, 72, and Mrs. Alice Case, 77, will be interviewed to-
day. The files are cleared through 1965, and the Committee
is now working on 1966 applications. Upon recommendation
of the Committee, it was moved, seconded and carried to
accept as permanent residents upon completion of probation:
Mrs. Alice Bronson, 83, low income; Mrs. Blanche Clear, 81,
low income, Mrs. Harriet Young, 75, medium income. After
discussion, it was moved, seconded andarried that the
monthly payments of permanent patients in the Infirmary
who are on Old Age Assistance or on low income be raised
$1.00 a day in accordance with the State provisions. The
possibility of raising monthly payments of all permanent
Infirmary patients, or of raising monthly payments in
general, is also under discussion.

INFIRMARY: Mrs. Schilling reported that there are 25
patients in the Infirmary, 23 of whom are permanent.
