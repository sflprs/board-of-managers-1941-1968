March 26, 1963

The regular monthly meeting of the Board of Managers of the

San Francisco Ladies' Protection and Relief Society was held

Tuesday, March 26, 1963 at 10:30 a.m. at 3400 Laguna Street,

Mrs. Wm. H. Nigh, Jr., President, presiding,

Present were: Miss Bridge and Mesdames Rettenmayer, Bruck, Porter,

Metcalf, Lawry, Griffith, Lyon, Lloyd, Logan, Krick, Noble,

Schilling, Matignon, Perry, Bowes, Landis, Blackie, Stephenson,

and Gunther.

The Secretary read the minutes of the February 26, 1963 meeting

which were approved as read.

Treasurer: Miss Bridge, Treasurer, read the financial report.
Operating Balance February 28, 1963 .... $5,446.19

It was moved, seconded and carried the Treasurer's report be

placed on file.

Administrator: Mrs. Pogson said a normal routine was evident in life

at "The Heritage". Two new residents had begun probationary periods--

Mrs, Wintringham and Mrs. Grebe. Several gifts had been received--

from Mrs. Krick a donation of two cases of Girl Scout Cookies; from

Mr. Crothers new plants for the dining room planters; a loud speaker

for the theatre room from Mrs. Glover; a box of beautiful camillias

from Mr, Evans; St. Patrick and Springtime table favors from

Mrs. Byrne. Mrs. Pogson had purchased five new books for the

Library and asked the Board for books and magazines.

Finance and Personnel: Miss Bridge, Chairman, reported a meeting with

the Board of Trustees on February 26th at which time she presented the

monthly Budeet of $23,962.00 for the coming year. She said there was

some discussion of the rise in salary of the Food Consultant and on

the sum of $500.00 for outside medical and dental care. After

discussion the Trustees approved a monthly Budget of $24,000.00,

Miss Bridge said at the examination of accounts the auditors

reported a profit of $2,469.00. She announced the estate of

Ann Duffy as approximately $5,000.00 in stocks.
