Library: Mrs. Charles E. Smith reported that consideration was
being given to the placing of bood-cases for paper backs in some
of the lounge areas.

Visiting: No report.

Nominating Committee: In the absence of Mrs. Kirkwood there a
was no report. was moved, seconded and carried that Mrs.

Kirkwood be empowered to fill the quota of 31 members for the
Board of Managers, subject to Board approval.

Corresponding secretary: A note from Mrs. John T. Stephenson,
corresponding secretary, was read requesting permission to resign
from the office because of the pressure of duties as the Chair-
man of the Admission Committee. Mrs. Charles E. Smith was
appointed to fill the office.

New Business: The re-activation of a fund for the sending of
flowers to those who are ill was discussed and approved. Mrs.
Rettenmayer was thanked for the gift of Christmas tree ornaments.
Mrs. Pogson discussed the meeting in Los Angles on April 25th

of the Association of Homes for the Aging. Members of the Board
were asked to write state legislators urging them to vote against
A B 641 which would curtail the work of the Heritage by establish-
ing taxation based on an organization's resources rather than

on number of people cared for and type of care given. A B 978

is also going to be considered by the legislature and its possible
effect on homes for the aged was discussed. Mrs. Gunther thanked

the Board for its warm hearted support as she begins her term
of office as president. .

There being no further business the meeting was adjourned.

Respectfully submitted,

Elizabeth Smith
