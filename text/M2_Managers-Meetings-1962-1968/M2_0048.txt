JUNE 25, 1963

The regular monthly meeting of the Board of Managers of the San
Francisco Ladies' Protection and Relief Society was held at 10:00
a.m, on Tuesday, June 25, 1963 at 3400 Laguna Street, Mrs. Ernest
L, Gunther, President, presiding,

Present were: Miss Bridge and Mesdames Bowes, Bruck, Craig, Griffith,
Kirkwood, Lawry, Lloyd, Logan, Lyon, Nigh, Porter, Rettenmayer,
Knight Smith, Charles Smith, Stephenson and Schilling.

Excused were: Mesdames Blackie, Krick, Landis, Matignon and Metcalf.

The Secretary read the minutes of the May 28th meeting which were
approved as read.

Treasurer: Miss Bridge, Treasurer read the financial report.
Operating Balance May 31, 1963---$20,369.16

Miss Bridge noted that five thousand dollars (85,000) of this sum

was reserved for transfer to the Replacement Fund.

It was moved, seconded and carried the Treasurer's report be placed

on file.

Administrator: Mrs. Pogson reported that due to the alterations

of the building no new residents were being accepted. One vacancy
had occurred because of Mrs. Wintringham's decision not to remain

at "The Heritage" and Mrs. True Durbrow has taken her place, with
probation period commencing June 15th. Mrs. Pogson reported that
while Staff were having vacations, college students were replacing
them. The current events talks given by Mr. Terzian of the School
Department, have terminated for this season and will probably be
discontinued. Mr. Pape will continue to show his travel movies
during the summer. Mrs. Logan has planned a variety of entertainment

presented the name of Mrs. Walter Moore; it was moved, seconded

and carried that Mrs. Moore be asked to become a member of the Board.
The Nominating Committee will present other names at the next meeting.
