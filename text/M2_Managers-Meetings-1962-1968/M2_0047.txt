i intenance and Furnishings: Mrs. Bruck, Chairman, report-
Building, Maintenance renovating of the 3rd floor sitting room. Fabric
has been chosen to recover the chairs, as well as paint for the walls,
Very attractive framed flower prints donated by Mrs. Bruck will be
hung and a card table and four chairs added to the furnishings.

Mrs. Nigh reported meeting with Mr. Thornton on changes on
converting the old Infirmary into bedrooms. Changes on the 3rd
floor of the old building are also being considered but as yet no
decision has been reached.

Garden: Mrs. Porter, Chairman, said garden construction in the
Court should be finished by September lst. A most attractive sketch

of this future garden is on view at the entrance to the dining
room.

Library: Mrs. Charles Smith, Chairman, was absent. Mrs. Pogson

reported several donations of books, and plans to add bookcases in
one of the sitting rooms. :

Visiting: Mrs. Lyon continues to visit, spending most of her time
with those who do not leave their rooms often.

Nominating Committee: Mrs. Kirkwood, Chairman, said she expected to
present names to be voted on at the September meeting. Mrs. Skene

read a letter from Mrs. Walter Moore regretting that because of her
health she would not be able to become a Board Member.

Corresponding Secretary: Mrs. Skene read a letter from Captain

indes thanking the Board for a plant sent during his convalescence.
She also brought to the Board's attention the notification of the
death of Blythe Safely, a longtime and faithful employee of "The
Heritage" for many years on the pension roll.

New Business: Mrs. Gunther read a letter from Mr. Jenkins, saying
he was studying the State Medical Aid program in its relation to
"The Heritage". On his suggestion, Mrs. Pogson, Mrs. Gunther and
Mrs. Nigh will meet July 23rd with Mr. Friedman of the Hebrew Home
for the Aged. Mr. Friedman is conversant with all aspects of the
Medical Aid to the Aged program. Mrs. Gunther read the draft of a
letter raising the cost of care of seven Heritage Residents, based
on their ability to meet our per capita cost. She also reported
that Miss Keopel fell and broke her hip. As no bed was immediately
available at Children's Hospital.and our physician considered this

an emergency, Miss Keopel was sent to Presbyterian Hospital and
operated on there.

There being no further business, the meeting was adjourned.
