NEW BUSINESS: Mrs. Logan announced that Mrs. Gunther has been ap-
pointed to the Finance and Personnel Committee.

Thursday, December 21, 1967, was selected as the date for the
Christmas Party, which will be at 7:30 P.M.

The problem of tipping was discussed. Mrs. Gunther suggested
that at the time salaries are raised both maids and residents be
advised that there will be no tipping, but that residents may con-
tribute to a Christmas fund for employees instead. Charges for trays
should go to The Heritage; there is no charge for trays ordered by
Dr. McKenzie, but all other trays are 25¢ each to discourage residents
from asking for them unnecessarily.

There being no further business, the meeting was adjourned at
12:00 Noon. The meeting was followed by a luncheon in honor of
Miss Lila Liston.

Respectfully submitted,

Om

Joan Bailey
Recording Secretary
