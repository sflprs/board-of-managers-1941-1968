ADMISSIONS: Mrs. Nigh reported that five rooms are empty,
or are about to be empty, because of moves to the Infirmary.
Mrs. Blanche Clear and Mrs. Harriet Young are moving in on
probation. Miss Berthe D'Aubigny is being cleared financial-
ly and will take her physical examination. Being interviewed
today are: Julia Smith, Trust Agreement; Eva D. Alves, Trust
Agreement; Alice D. Bronson, low income. The Committee is
clearing the files and is on the year 1965.

INFIRMARY: In the absence of Mrs. Schilling, Mrs. Bell
reported that there are 25 patients in the Infirmary. Dr. Wilson,
who sees several patiénts here, told her that our Infirmary has
the best facilities and is the best run of any he has seen.

HOUSE:

GARDEN: Mrs. Porter reported that the garden is looking
well-cared for. The gardener has worked an extra half day to
get things cleared up. Mrs. Metcalf requested that a letter
of appreciation be sent to Mrs. Evans for her work on the garden,
Mrs. Evans is pleased with her fee of $50.00 a month.

FURNISHINGS: Mrs. Marshall absent--no report.

RECREATION: Mrs. Rettenmayer reported the following social
activities; two concerts, followed by social hours; slides by
Mr. and Mrs. Hill and Mr. Deal; armchair exercises and short
excursions, both conducted by Mrs. Casper, Social Director;
hobby classes conducted by Mrs. Staples; a Birthday party.
Future events will include groups of residents attending the
Burke School Festival with Mrs. Cox, and the San Francisco
Symphony with tickets given by Mrs. Metcalf. Mrs. Perry ree
ported on the Fall Tea, which was a great success. She thanked
Mr. Debes, Duchess Catering, and the maids for the delicious
food and excellent service at the Tea. Most ladies who poured
were former Board members, with the exception of Mrs. Craig and
Mrs. Charles Smith, who are active Board members. Mrs. Perry
requested that letters of appreciation be written to Duchess
Catering and to Mrs. Barton, who arranged the flowers on the
tea tables and on the piano.

LIBRARY: Mrs. Kent reported that she had met with Miss Fox
and will be requesting specific books from Board members. Detec-
tive stories and current magazines are particulary in demand.
Mrs. Rettenmayer suggested a subscription to the Detective Book
Club. Mrs. Metcalf suggested that a list of requested books be
circulated. The amount of $10.00 a month is budgeted for buying
books.

“HERITAGE HAPPENINGS": Mrs. Charles Smith reported that
there is an issue at press.

VISITING: Mrs. Perry reported that she had made several
visits including one to Miss Minser, who was reading an
article about The Heritage written by another resident, Mrs.
Jennings. Mrs. Craig reported that she will visit Mrs.
Hyman in the hospital.

ADMINISTRATOR'S REPORT: Mrs. Bell reported that the recent
bus tour of the Berkeley Campus, Tilden Park and the Golden
Gateway was greatly enjoyed by residents. A bus tour of

the city during the Christmas season is in the planning stage.
