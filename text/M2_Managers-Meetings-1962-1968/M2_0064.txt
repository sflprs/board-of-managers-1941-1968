January 22, 1963

The regular meeting of the Board of Managers of the San Francisco
Ladies’ Protection and Relief Society was held on Tuesday, January
22, 1963, at 10:30 a.m, at 3400 Laguna Street; Mrs. James Skene,
lst vice-president, presiding. Those present were: Miss Bridge
and Mesdames Scott Smith, Gunther, Logan, Schilling, Bowes,
Craig, Krick, Blackie, Lyon, Lawry, Griffith, Bruck, Rettenmayer,
Kirkwood, Porter, Stephenson, Perry, Charles Smith, Noble, Landis
and Lloyd. Excused: Mesdames Nigh,- Metcalf and Matignon.

Mrs. Skene welcomed the new Board Member Mrs. James Lawry.

The secretary read the minutes of the December 18, 1962 meeting
and which were approved as read. The secretary then read the
minutes of two meetings held by the Board of Directors, - one

on December 6th and one on December 20, 1962. These pertained

to the lease of the Jack Tar property and are appended hereto.
Treasurer: Miss Bridge, treasurer, read the financial report. -
Balance in Operating Bank Account as of December 31, 1962 -
$406.88 overdrawn.

It was moved, seconded and carried that the financial report be
placed on file.

Miss Bridge reported that one thousand dollars from the estate

of Mrs. Merle Smith, a former Board Member, had been given to the
Heritage.

Administrator: Mrs. Pogson said that-there were 99 residents,
with no vacancies; Miss Patricia Davidson and Mrs. Charles Pratt
had completed their period of probation and were now ready to

sign Life Care Contracts. Mrs. Pogson noted that the Infirmary
survey by Mrs. Maxine Bishop had been completed; that all drugs
will now be pruchased through the St. Francis Pharmacy at a
twenty percent charge over "Red Book" prices. This was approved
by Dr. McKenzie. Mrs. Pogson reported an unfortunate incident
between a white and a colored nurse; the nursing staff has been

reduced and now numbers nine white and seven colored. - A
