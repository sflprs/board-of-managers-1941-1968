The regular monthly meeting of the Board of Managers of the San
Francisco Ladies" Protection and Relief Society was held January
28, 1964 at 10:30 a.m. at 3400 Laguna Street - Mrs, Ernest Gunther,
President, presiding.

Present: Miss Bridge and Mesdames Bowes, Bruck, Griffith,
Hinman, Houston, Lawry, Lloyd, Matignon, Nigh, Noble, Perry,
Porter, Rettenmayer, Skene, Chas. Smith, Knight Smith,
Stephenson and Schilling.

Excused: Mesdames Craig, Kirkwood, Logan, Marshall and Metcalf.

The Secretary read the minutes of the November 26, 1963 meeting
which were approved as read. It was noted there was no December
meeting.

Administrator: Mrs, Pogson said the Heritage had 90 residents,
ranging in age from 72 to 98, making the average age 85. Fifteen
of the residents were removed from The Morgan Building so recon-
struction work could begin on January 5. The work is expected to

be completed April 30. Miss Marie Hinard, a life contract member

of The Heritage for 13 years died at French Hospital January 5th.
The Dept. of Public Health issued a license for our 20 bed Infirmary,
and Mrs. Hinz of the Dept. of Social Welfare approved the application
for the renewal of The Heritage license which expires February 3.

On completion of the reconstruction, 22 new members will be accepted
as fast as possible. Most of these have been on the waiting list
for some time. Mrs. Pogson thanked the Board for the Christmas
Bonus for herself and the staff.

Infirmary: Mrs. Schilling, Chairman, reported 16 persons in the
Infirmary. Three, who are fracture cases, are improving with
great rapidity. The new Bath Lift has proved extraordinarily
useful.

Finance and Personnel: Miss Bridge, Chairman, reported a
recapitulation of the Budget for the whole year 1963 showed a
saving of $23,621.02. The Committee checked the building for

Major repairs needed, these repairs to be made from the Budget

sum saving after approval of the Board of Directors. These items
were: sprung window frames, the convector heaters, and the heating
System of the whole 4th floor. It was moved, seconded and carried
that the Finance Committee investigate prices and bring in a report.
Miss Bridge said the Finance & Personnel Committee recommended the
following payroll raises: Chef C. Wong from $400 to $425 per month,
@ new second cook $375 per month, all dining-room help raised $10
Per month, 4 house-maids raised $10 per month. Mrs. Hallum, the
Infirmary head nurse, from $400 to $425 per month. It was moved,
Seconded and carried that the recommendation of the Finance &
Personnel Committee be accepted, and the raises retroactive from

January 1, 1964

Admissions: Mrs. Stephenson, Chairman, said that there was a long
waiting list, but that all papers were in order for 4 persons to
take up residence as soon as the rooms are ready.

Occupational Activities: Mrs. Rettenmayer, Chairman, said some of

the residents are already working on Christmas presents. Mice
