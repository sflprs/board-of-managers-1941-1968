October 24, 1967

The regular monthly meeting of the San Francisco Ladies' Protec-
tion and Relief Society was held Tuesday, October 24, 1967 at
10:30 A.M. at 3400 Laguna Street, San Francisco. Mrs. Lawrence
V. Metcalf, First Vice-President, presided in the absence of
Mrs. Jack S. Logan, President, who was absent due to jury duty.

PRESENT: Miss Bridge and Mesdames Bailey, Bowes, Craig, Gunther,
Houston, Howard, Kent, Metcalf, Nigh, Nishkian, Noble, Perry,
Porter, Rettenmayer, Charles Smith, Knight Smith, Staples and
Stephenson.

EXCUSED: Mesdames Bean, Bruck, Cox, Krick, Logan, Marshall,
Pinney and Schilling. Mrs. Metcalf reminded Board members to
notify the office if they are unable to attend Board meetings.

The minutes of the September 26, 1967 meeting were read and
approved.

TREASURER'S REPORT: Miss Bridge reported:

Per Capita Cost for September of 110 Residents... $ 358.00
Balance in Operating Bank Account September 30... $30,427.00.

Budget appropriation $33,983.00. Overexpended by $1,012.00.

The $30,000.00 transferred by the Board of Directors to the
Board of Managers' Operating Bank Account is not in the budget,
so that overexpenditures can still be determined. It was moved,
seconded and carried that the Treasurer's Report be accepted
and placed on file.

CORRESPONDENCE: Mrs. Nishkian reported that she had written
thank-you letters to: Miss Halcyon Brewer for money donated

for recovering the chair in the pocket garden lounge; Mrs.Maude
Milnes for a hi-fiset; Mrs. Charles W. Fay for clothes from her
mother; Mrs. Margaret W. Wright for a picture which had belonged
to her mother, Mrs. Peabody, a recently deceased resident. Mrs.
Nishkian also read letters of appreciation from: Miss Liston
for the luncheon in her honor, and for the gift presented to her};
Mrs. Hilbert and Mrs. Landis, former Board members, for Miss
Liston's luncheon; Mrs. Wellington, the daughter of a resident,
Mrs. Sherwood, for the Fall Tea; Mrs. Rosemary Van Sweden, a
nurse at The Heritage, for her insurance coverage; Mrs. Rochfort,
a resident, for a hearing aid purchased from her funds; Mrs.
Marsh, a new resident, for being at The Heritage, and particular-
ly for the well-balanced and well-served meals; Mrs. Lennox, a
resident, for the Birthday party; Mr. Hartnack, a resident, for
the renovation of his room. Mrs. Metcalf read a letter from
Mrs. Marshall requesting a leave of absence from the Board for
seven months to complete the Docent Course in Oriental Art at

the De Young Museum. Mrs. Marshall will be able to help with the
decorating, and with cleaning out the storage room, but she will
be unable to attend Board meetings because the course meets on
Tuesday mornings. It was moved, seconded and carried that Mrs.
Marshall be granted a seven-month leave of absence.

COMMITTEE REPORTS:

bus trip cost $51.50, plus tip, for 25 hours; she suggested that
more trips be planned since residents enjoy them. Uniforms for
the waitresses are now being purchased at a total cost of $186.00
per year, a substantial savings over former uniform rental at a
total cost of $300.00 per year. A charge to residents of 25-cents

concerned; each waitress has her own tables and takes care of her
