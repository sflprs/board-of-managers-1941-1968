Visiting: Mrs. Lyon, Chairman, said a new visiting policy should be
considered as it had come to her attention some of the residents
preferred not to have visits from the Board. Discussion followed,
and it was determined members of the Visiting Committee should con-
sult Mrs. Pogson before seeing residents.

Cor. Secretary: Mrs. Stephenson had no report.

Nominating Committee: Mrs. Charles Smith proposed the name of Mrs,
Walter Schitline. Mrs. Schilling was unanimously elected to the
Board of Managers.

New Business: Miss Bridge suggested the Board consider approaching
persons who were specialists in matters in which our Board has only
an amatuer understanding, and that these specialists be asked to be-
come advisors to our committee. Discussion followed with no concrete
conclusion, but that the idea should be given further thought.

Mrs. Nigh announced the next meeting will be the 4th Tuesday in June,
commencing a three months! trial of the changed meeting date from the
3rd to the 4th Tuesday.

Mrs. Nigh said the building of the Infirmary will mean the Sun House
must be moved. After discussion, it was determined the architect be
consulted as to the best place for a Solarium.

Mrs. Nigh called attention to the second edition of "Heritage Hap-
penings", and congratulated Mrs. Skene, the editor.

Papers announcing the new committees for 1962-63 were distributed.

Mrs. Metcalf asked for a leave of absence during the three summer
months.

Mrs. Nigh reported she and Mrs. Pogson had attended a session of the

California Conference of Health and Welfare. Mrs. Gunther had gone

to a second session and said the policy of the new care for Cali-

fornians was to be more flexible.

There being no further business, the meeting was adjourned.
