April 23, 1968

The regular monthly meeting of the Board of Managers of
The San Francisco Ladies' Protection and Relief Society
was held at 3400 Laguna Street, San Francisco, California
on Tuesday, April 23, 1968 at 10:30 A.M. The President of
the Board of Managers, Mrs. Jack S. Logan, presided at the
meeting.

PRESENT: Miss Bridge and Mesdames Bailey, Bowes, Craig,
Gunther, Houston, Howard, Logan, Metcalf, Nigh, Nishkian,
Noble, Perry, Porter, Schilling, Charles Smith, Knight Smith,
Staples and Stephenson.

EXCUSED: Mesdames Bean, Bruck, Cox, Kent, Krick, Marshall,
Pinney and Rettenmayer.

Mrs. Logan announced that Mr. Logan would speak on the year-
ly audit at 11:15 a.m. In the interest of time, it was
moved, seconded and carried that the reading of the minutes
of the March 26, 1968 meeting be waived.

CORRESPONDENCE: Mrs. Nishkian read a letter from Mrs. Bruck
thanking the Board for the letter of condolence sent to her
upon the death of her father, Mr. Stetson Hindes. She read

a letter from Mrs. Bean tendering her resignation from the
Board of Managers because of the press of other duties. It

was moved, seconded and carried that Mrs. Bean be thanked

for making the tray favors, and that her resignation be ac-
cepted with regret. Mrs. Logan announced that Mrs. Rettenmayer,
after reconsidering her resignation from the Board of Managers,
still feels that she must resign because of ill health. It was
moved,seconded and carried to accept, with regret, Mrs. Retten-
mayer's resignation.

COMMITTEE REPORTS:

FINANCE AND PERSONNEL: Upon recommendation of Miss Bridge
and the Committee, it was moved, seconded and carried that:

1. salaries for aides in the Infirmary be brought up to
minimum union standards beginning July 1, 1968. This will
amount to $1,950.00 for six months, which we hope can be
recovered from the estimated overtime and vacation substi-
tution allocations.

2. 1,000 new contracts be ordered at a total cost of
$68.50.
