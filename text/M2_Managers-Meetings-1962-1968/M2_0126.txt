HOUSE:

GARDEN: Mrs. Porter reported that the pool in the
pocket garden needs repair. Following discussion, it was
moved, seconded and carried that a new pump, galvanized
bowl, dome spray and light be installed at an estimated cost
of $385.00. It was moved and seconded to lower the center
jet in the pools in the main courtyard and install a water
level control at a cost not to exceed $150.00. The Board
voted six for and six against this motion. The President
voted for this motion, which was then carried. It was moved,
seconded and carried to employ the gardener for 3 days a
week instead of 2, at a total salary of $450.00, instead of
$300.00 per month, to be effective January 1, 1968.

FURNISHINGS: No report

RECREATION: Mrs. Logan thanked Mrs. Perry for her chair-
manship of the Fall Tea. She announced that there will be
a Birthday Party on November 29, 1967. Because of Mrs.Pinney's
illness, Mrs. Nishkian has taken over as chairman of the
Christmas Party. It was decided to ask Mr. and Mrs. Marsh to
sing at next year's Christmas Party, since they are booked up
for this year. Mrs. Rettenmayer reported that,in addition to
the regular programs of lectures, movies and slides, there was
a concert, a Bingo game, a trip to the Burke's School Festival
conducted by Mrs. Cox and a Thanksgiving Party. Since Mrs.
Staples has asked to be excused over the holidays, Mrs.
Rettenmayer is conducting the hobby classes. She displayed
articles made by residents. She also thanked Mrs. Nishkian
for bringing alder and hemlock cones. Mr. Dewey Roberts is
conducting a Tuesday evening lecture series on current events.
Tene Dominic Schlafani said Mass in the chapel on October 25,
1967.

LIBRARY: In the absence of Mrs. Kent, Mrs. Schilling report-
ed that Mrs. Kent has obtained several books for the library.

"HERITAGE HAPPENINGS": Mrs. Charles Smith reported that the
October issue is out. There will be another issue after the
first of the year when more material is received.

VISITING: Mrs. Perry reported that she visited Mrs. Smith
and Mrs. Marsh.

ADMINISTRATOR'S REPORT: Mrs. Bell reported that there are 115
residents; 25 of whom are in the Infirmary. Mrs. Durbrow is at
the St. Francis Memorial Hospital Psychiatric Clinic for observa-
tion. Thanksgiving dinner was served to 97 residents and guests.
The cost of food per person per day was $1.12. Mrs. Bell had a
fireside meeting, attended by 60 residents, to report on her
trip to the American Association for the Aging Administrators!
Conference in Atlanta, Georgia. There were excellent speakers
at the conference representing government, private industry

and church groups. There were lectures and discussion groups

on such subjects as food, community participation, and rotating
boards. Those who attended were invited to homes in small

groups and also toured neighboring retirement residences. Mrs.
Bell visited Wesley Woods which is on the campus of Emory Uni-
versity. She found that The Heritage is unique and that no-
where else do residents live so graciously so reasonably. Next
year San Francisco will be host to this conference.
