Visiting: Mrs. Craig visited a number of members and found every or
contented and happy.

Entertainment: Much activity this last month. A goodly number of
musical programs, lectures, slides, Bingo, etc., and of course,

the usual Birthday Party.

Twelve puppets were delivered to Children's Hospital, and the
angel project has been completed.

Corresponding Secretary:

Mrs. Charles Smith reported that
Mrs. Lyon could not reconsider her resignation from the Board

of Managers, so it was moved and seconded that Mrs. Lyon be
made an Honorary Member.
Respectfully submitted,

Mildred N. Schilling
