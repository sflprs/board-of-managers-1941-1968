GARDEN: Mrs. Porter reported that a Monterey Pine
($40.00) has been planted, and that a Pipe Veronica was
moved from the back garden to the front. Five small Pipe
Veronicas have been planted in the back garden. Mrs. Evans
continues to donate here services as garden consultant.
FURNISHINGS: Mrs. Marshall reported that the new office,
stair-landing and fire-door curtains have been installed.

Miss Bridge requested the estimated furnishings' budget for
next year.

LIBRARY COMMITTEE: In the absence of Mrs. Knight Smith,
Mrs. Pogson reported that six new books had been purchased.

"HERITAGE HAPPENINGS": Mrs. Charles Smith reported that
the Committee is working on a new issue at present.

RECREATION COMMITTEE: Mrs. Rettenmayer announced that there
will be an Antique and Hobby Show in March. Residents have
been requested to leave a list of what they wish to exhibit

at the front desk. Miss Davidson will check items in and out.
Twenty-six Residents celebrated their birthdays at the combined
October and November birthday party. Members of the Hobby Class
have made beautiful paper flowers to decorate gift boxes, which
were displayed. Mrs. Logan, Mrs. Metcalf, Mrs. Nishkian, Mrs.
Rettenmayer, and Mrs. Stephenson took groups of Residents to
the Rembrandt Exhibit and the Brundage Collection. Mrs. Retten-
mayer took a group of Residents to her home for lunch after the
visit to the Brundage Collection. The Christmas Party has been
changed from the 2lst to the 20th of December; Board members
are requested to attend, and reminder cards will be sent. Mrs.
Bean continues to make delightful holiday favors. Mrs. Retten-
mayer read a letter of appreciation from a Resident.

VISITING COMMITTEE : Mrs. Craig reported that she has made
several visits to Residents and urged all Board members to
visit when they can. Mrs. Perry also made several visits.

NEW BOARD MEMBERS' COMMITTEE: In the absence ofMrs. Nishkian,
Mrs. Metcalf reported that the Committee recommends that Mrs.
Joseph Staples be invited to join the Board of Managers. Dis-
cussion followed. It was moved, seconded, and carried that

Mrs. Stqales be asked to join the Board, and that she be invited
to the Christmas Party and the January meeting.

QLD BUSINESS: None.

NEW BUSINESS: Mrs. Bruck's offer to arrange for a prestidigi-
tator to give a show for Residents was accepted with pleasure.
Mrs. Logan asked Board members to bring magazines, and thanked
Mrs. iligh for her gift of a plant. She announced that Mrs.
Marian F. Bell, who will be the new Administrator, and Mr. White,
President of the Board of Directors, will be present at lunch
after the meeting. After discussion, it was moved, seconded,

and carried that the December, 1965 Board meeting be cancelled.

There being no further business, the meeting was adjourned at
12:15 p.m., November 22, 1966.

Respectfully submitted,

Joan Bailey,
Recording Secretary
