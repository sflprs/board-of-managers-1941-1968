Street, Mrs. William Nigh, President, presiding.

Present were: Miss Bridge and Mesdames Nigh, Craig, Porter,

Excused were: Mesdames Blackie, Matignon, Metcalf, Schilling,

The Secretary read the minutes of the November 27, 1962, meeting,
which were approved after correction.

Treasurer: Miss Bridge, Treasurer, read the financial report.
Operating balance November 30, 1962....$1150.70

It was moved, seconded and carried the Treasurer's report be
placed on file.

Administrator: Mrs. Pogson reported Mrs. Frances Airth had
entered The Heritage on probation. Due to the building of the
new Infirmary, and shifting of some of the residents, no rooms
are available at present. Mrs. Pogson said Dr. Edgar Snell
had been committed to Napa State Hospital because of mental
illness. She also reported the death of Mrs. Marion Fraser
at the age of 97, after 32 years as a resident.

Plans are being made for Christmas decorations, and Christmas
dinner favors will be sprays of holly donated by Mrs. Retten-
mayer. Mrs. Pogson spoke of the accomplishments and progress
made by THE HERITAGE in the past five years, and expressed her
gratitude to those who had brought this about.

Infirmary: Mrs. Kirkwood, Chairman, reported 15 patients in the
Infirmary, one on the critical list, and 55 out-patients, most
of whom required medication three times daily.

Building Committee: Mrs. Porter, Chairman, pointed out the
Parking lot had been carefully designed to save the existing
trees. Work has been slower than anticipated.

Finance and Personnel: Miss Bridge, Chairman, said her committee
ad voted to’ buy amsonite folding arm chairs, and 40 plain
chairs - total price $341.00 (at factory) for the Hobby Room,

and moved this amount to be put in as a replacement item in

1963. This was seconded and carried. She said three lights

at $14.00 each ahd been installed in the Hobby Room. Miss
Bridge reported on Miss Sutherland's various suggestions for
redecorating the Theatre and Hobby Rooms. It was moved, seconded,
and carried that the $187.50 be spent to paper the west wall

of the Hobby Room. Miss Bridge said it was necessary to replace
the worn carpet in front of the elevator in the East Wing,

and plastic tile would be put down - the cost $147.00. She

said expenses for the Tea Party were $735.00, which was more
than had been expected, but there was about $600.00 for Christmas.
A complete payroll analysis has been compiled by Mr. Anderson.
