June 27, 1967

The regular monthly meeting of the Board of Managers of the
San Francisco Ladies' Protection and Relief Society was held
Tuesday, June 27, 1967, at 10:30 A.M. at 3400 Laguna Street,
San Francisco, Mrs. Jack S. Logan presiding.

PRESENT: Miss Bridge and Mesdames Bailey, Bowes, Bruck, Craig,
Gunther, Houston, Logan, Marshall, Noble, Pinney, Porter,
Schilling, Staples and Stephenson.

EXCUSED: Mesdames Bean, Cox, Howard, Kent, Krick, Metcalf ,Nigh,
Nishkian, Perry, Rettenmayer, Charles Smith and Knight Smith.

The minutes of the May 23, 1967 meeting wererread and approved.
TREASURER'S REPORT: Miss Bridge reported:

Per capita cost for May of 104 residents.........$ 409.00
Balance in Operating Bank Account May 31l.....-++.$15,455.00

It was moved, seconded and carried that the Treasurer's Report
be accepted and placed on file.

CORRESPONDENCE: Mrs. Nishkian absent -- no report.

COMMITTEE REPORTS:

FINANCE AND PERSONNEL: Miss Bridge reported that the repair
and reconstruction of the Intercom System will probably cost
$1,600.00. The new curtains for all upstairs rooms will cost
$1,048.80 for 121 pair; half will be purchased this year and
half next year.

Upon recommendation of the Committee, it was moved, seconded
and carried that the Board of Managers:

1. offer the position of Social Director to Mrs. Ursula H.
Casper for 5 hours a day, 5 days per week, at $3.50 per hour,
which averages $393.75 per month.

2. give a payment of $100.00 to Mrs. Fesca, who made 112 chair

backs now in use in the dining room, and has now made 112 more.

3. adopt as a Personnel policy that 70 years shall be the
mandatory retirement age for all employees of The Heritage.
A compromise may be worked out, if desirable, by a Board

member and the Administrator with the employee in question.

ADMISSIONS: In the absence of Mrs. Nigh, Mrs. Stephenson
reported that Mrs. Nora Barker has moved in on probation.
Mr. Rochfort and Mrs. Heffron have moved to the Infirmary
permanently. Mrs. Rochfort has moved into Mrs. Heffron's
room, thereby releasing an apartment. Three applicants were
interviewed.

INFIRMARY: Mrs. Schilling reported that there are 18 patients
in the Infirmary. Dr. McKenzie is ill. Dr. Henderson has
taken over in his absence. He has suggested that once a year
all ambulatory patiens should have a physical at the doctor's
office. The prior laboratory work could be done at The
Heritage by the Medical Arts Laboratory. Discussion followed
and definite arrangements will be made later after further
consultation with the doctors.

HOUSE:
Maintenance: Mrs. Houston <= no report.
Garden: Mrs. Porter reported that $796.00 of the garden
budget has been spent. The possibility of giving a

Christmas payment to Mrs. Evans, our volunteer garden
consultant, was discussed.
