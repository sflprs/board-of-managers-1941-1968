Admissions: Mrs. Stephenson, Chairman, reported that the

Building & Maintenance: Mrs. Nigh, Chairman, announced that the
carpet will be in the Julia Morgan Building today, the fire
alarm system will be operable today and completed by Friday,

after which the residents may be moved in.
Work is progressing slowly on the heating changes in the
Perry Building. An inspection tour of the entire Heritage
has been made by Mrs. Nigh, Mrs. Pogson and Miss Bridge. They
found everything in good condisiton with only a few minor repairs

to be made.
