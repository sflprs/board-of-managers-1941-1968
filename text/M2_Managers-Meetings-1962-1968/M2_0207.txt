MAY 6, 1965

The Annual meeting of the Board of Directors and the Board of
Managers of the San Francisco Ladies' Protection and Relief
Society was held at 2 p.m. at 3400 Laguna Street on May 6, 1965,
Mr, Joseph A. Moore, Jr., President, presiding.

The purpose of this meeting was the election of members to the
Board of Directors, and members to the Board of Managers.

Minutes of this meeting were taken by the Board of Directors!
Secretary.

Immediately following the adjournment of this meeting, an
organization meeting was held by the Board of Managers, Mrs.
Walter Schilling, lst Vice-President, presiding.

Present: Miss Bridge and Mesdames Craig, Griffith, Perry,
Logan, Skene, Lloyd, Charles Smith and Rettenmayer.

Mrs. Schilling called on Mrs. Craig, nominating chairman, to
present the list of candidates for officers of the Board of
Managers for 1965-66. There being no further nominations, the
Secretary was instructed to cast the ballot and the following
officers were elected -

President Mrs. William H. Nigh, Jr.
lst Vice-President Mrs. Jack Logan

2nd Vice-President Mrs. Lawrence Metcalf
Treasurer Miss Helen Bridge
Secretary Mrs. Francis Lloyd

Cor. Secretary Mrs. Charles E. Smith

The following resolution was passed unanimously:
That the commercial and savings accounts with
Wells Fargo Bank and Crocker-Citizens National

Bank be continued in accordance with resolution
attached hereto and made a part of these minutes.

There being no further business, the meeting was adjourned.
