Dining Room _& Kitchen: Mrs. Porter, Chairman, announced that the
August 19 cost of food per person per day was 92¢,
The deep freeze is to be installed in the next few days. Arrange-

ments have been made to have the old deep freeze taken away at
no cost to The Heritage.

Mrs. Pogson has ordered the replacement of dining room mats
at a cost of $84.24,

Garden: Mrs. Hinman, Chairman, reported that necessary pruning
is underway. The new gardener is very satisfactory. Two large
planters on castors are being purchased to provide spots of
color in the court garden. They will be planted with seasonal
plants and wheeled out when the plants need replacing.

Entertainment & Occupational Activities: Mrs. Rettenmayer,

Chairman, announced that two piano recitals were given during
September, one by Miss Liston's sister-in-law and one by Miss
Janet Graham. The travel lectures were well attended and the
last Bingo Party had the greatest turnout so far. There were

72 at the September Birthday Party. The October Birthday Party
will take place on October 20th and the Fall Tea on October 30th.

Corresponding Secretary: In the absence of Mrs. Charles Smith,

Mrs. Knight Smith said that the next issue of Heritage Happenings
will be out in about a week and thanked Mrs. Pogson for her help
in writing some of the articles.

Old Business: Mrs. Knight Smith read a letter from Mrs. Kirkwood.
It was moved, seconded and carried to accept her resignation
with deep regret.

It was moved, seconded and carried that Mrs. Gunther be empowered
to purchase a gift for Charlie Wong without further approval of
the Board.

Mrs. Perry thanked Mr. Anderson for his work on the "Memory Book
of Gifts to The Heritage" and Miss Liston for typing the entries.
She then showed it to the Board. It contains a record of all
gifts to The Heritage since the merger in 1957, and will be

kept up to date.

Mrs. William Nigh announced that there will be a meeting of
the California Association of Homes for the Aged this ae .
and a meeting of the National Association of Homes For The Age
in November in Washington, D. C.

There being no further business, it was moved, seconded and
carried that the meeting be adjourned.

Respectfully submitted,

Elizabeth C. Lawry
Recording Secretary
