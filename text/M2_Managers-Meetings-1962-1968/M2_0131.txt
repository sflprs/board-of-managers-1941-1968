September 26, 1967

The regular monthly meeting of the Board of Managers of the San
Francisco Ladies' Protection and Relief Society was held Tuesday, '!
September 26, 1967 at 10:30 A.M. at 3400 Laguna Street, Mrs. Jack
S. Logan presiding. >

PRESENT: Miss Bridge and Mesdames Bailey, Bowes, Bruck, Cox,
Craig, Gunther, Howard, Logan, Metcalf, Nigh, Nishkian, Noble,
Perry, Pinney, Schilling, Charles Smith, Knight Smith and Staples.

EXCUSED: Mesdames Bean, Houston, Kent, Krick, Marshall, Porter,

Rettenmayer and Stephenson. Mesdames Porter and Stephenson attend-
ed the luncheon for Miss Liston which followed the meeting.

The minutes of the August 22, 1967 meeting were read and approved.
TREASURER'S REPORT: Miss Bridge reported:

Per Capita cost for August of 108 residents.........$ 401.00
Balance in Operating Bank Account August 31,0000 00 094,839.00.

She reminded Board members that cost of care is a relative term.

At The Heritage it means what it costs to run the entire organiza-
tion, including depreciation, legal audit, ete. In comparing our
figures with those of other homes one must be certain that identical
items are included. Few residents at The Heritage pay the full cost
of care, an average of about $350.00 a month, since we charge only
what residents are able to pay. Miss Bridge also read a letter dated
September 6, 1967 from the Board of Managers to the Board of Direc-
tors requesting “additional funds due to unavoidable salary increases,
particularly in the Infirmary, and to extraordinary expenses not
anticipated at budget time." She reported that the Board of Directors
has transferred $30,000.00 to the Board of Managers' operating bank
account. It was moved, seconded and carried that the Treasurer's
Report be accepted and placed on file.

CORRESPONDENCE: Mrs. Nishkian read Miss Liston's letter of accept-

ance for the luncheon in her honor after the September 26, 1967
Board meeting.

COMMITTEE REPORTS:

FINANCE AND PERSONNEL: Miss Bridge reported the recommendation
of the Committee that Mrs. Bell attend a Conference on Housing for
the Aged, in Georgia in late October and early November, at a cost
of $380.00. It was moved, seconded and carried that Mrs. Bell do
50. Mrs. Logan read a letter from AAHA (American Association of
Homes for the Aging) requesting a voluntary assessment of $100.00
to support its work. It was moved,seconded and carried that a
voluntary assessment of $100.00 be sent to AAHA. (See also,
INFIRMARY.).

ADMISSIONS: Mrs. Nigh reported with regret the death of Mrs.
Peabody. There is one empty room. Two people are ready to enter,
and one person is on the immediate waiting list. Upon recommenda-
tion of the Committee, it was moved, seconded and carried that
Mrs. Nora Barker be accepted as a permanent resident.

INFIRMARY: Mrs. Schilling reported that there are 19 patients
in the Infirmary. Upon recommendation of the Infirmry Committee
and the Finance and Personnel Committee, it was moved, seconded
and carried that Miss Patricia Hughes, R.N., be hired as Head Nurse
beginning as of November 15, 1967, at a salary of $780.00 a month. ‘
Miss Hughes, 32, received her nurse's training at St. Francis Memori-
al Hospital in San Francisco, and is presently Nursing Supervisor at

Mt. Zion Hospital.
HOUSE:
MAINTENANCE: Mrs. Houston absent--no report.

GARDEN: In the absence of Mrs. Porter, Mrs. Charles Smith
reported that the back fence has been completed.
