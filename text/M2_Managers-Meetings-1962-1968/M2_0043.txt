Garden: Mrs. Lawry reported that as there is no place for the dis-
posal of cuttings, etc., a fenced-off area is being prepared for easy
access for the scavengers. The new fountains should be in proper
working order this week. Mrs. Hughes, a neighbor on Octavia Street,
has complained that the Pocket Garden trees interfere with the
clotheslines, light, etc. Estimates have been secured on the trim-
ming of these trees. Mr. Shooter will trim them and the pine over
Mrs. Pogson's cottage for $275. Discussion followed as to the advis-
ability of removing some of the trees completely, and it was resolved
that the Garden Committee and Mr. Shooter consult further and solve
this problem without bringing it back to the Board.

Library: No report.

Rooms and Furnishings: Mrs. Pogson informed members the several
rooms presently vacant would be held for residents to move into when
work began on the 3rd floor reconstruction.
