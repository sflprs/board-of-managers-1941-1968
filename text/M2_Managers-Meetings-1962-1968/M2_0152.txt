January 24, 1967

The regular monthly meeting of the Board of Managers of
the San Francisco Ladies' Protection and Relief Society
was held on Tuesday, January 24, 1967, at 10:30 a.m. at
3400 Laguna Street, Mrs. David Houston presiding.

PRESENT: Miss Bridge and Mesdames Porter, Staples, Nigh
Schilling, Rettenmayer, Pinney, Noble, Nishkian, Perry, ,
Bowes, Metcalf, McKnight-Smith, Houston and Gunther.

THOSE ABSENT OR EXCUSED: Mesdames Bailey, Craig, Kent,
Marshall, Charles Smith, Stephenson, Bruck, Logan and Bean.

Mrs. Ernest Gunther read the minutes of the November 22,
1966 meeting.

TREASURER'S REPORT: Miss Bridge reported:
Per Capita cost for November

of 107 Residents......+.++-- $ 304.00
Balance in operating bank
account November 30 17,973.00

It was moved, seconded and carried that the Treasurer's
Report be placed on file.

CORRESPONDENCE: Mrs. Gunther reported that she wrote on
December 8 to Mrs. Ritter acknowledging the gift of a teak
table and a beautiful Japanese bowl and plate. On December
18, to Mrs. Hans Barkan for her kindness in having assumed
all funeral expenses in connection with the death of her
sister, Miss Bunker, and also for her generosity in donat-
ing the last Equitable Life Insurance check of Miss Bunker's
to The Heritage. On January 21, 1967, a note of thanks to
Mrs. Ebba Burrows, acknowledging her check in the amount of
one thousand dollars to be used for repainting of the
Infirmary.

ADMINISTRATOR: Mrs. Pogson reported much activity during

the past two months, entirely aside from the Holiday festivi-
ties. A license has been issued by the Department of Public
Health to cover twenty beds in the Infirmary Unit. Mrs.

Joan Vaughn of the Department of Social Welfare made her
yearly inspection in December. A license has been applied
for to cover all residents not under infirmary care. The
present license expires February 23. The Deputy State Fire
Marshall inspected the main building and the Infirmary Unit.
Fire clearance is renewed for a capacity of 97 well Residents
and 20 patients in the Infirmary Unit. Two new members were
admitted. There is a vacancy for a couple due to the death
of Mrs. Angell on January 3.
Mrs. Teal has been emotionally upset for some months past and
on January 8 left the Home to visit her daughter. Her family

placed her in the Belmait Sanitarium.

INFIRMARY COMMITTEE: Mrs. Schilling reports there are now
20 patients in the Infirmary and also that over the Christmas
Holidays that there were no critically ill people. The

Infirmary is running smoothly.

FINANCE AND PERSONNEL COMMITTEE: Miss Bridge requested that
the Board of Managers accept the revised payroll for 1967,
before presentation of the total budget, so that the salaries
could be made retroactive to January lst, 1967. This request
was accepted by the Board. It was moved and seconded that

the estimate for repainting in the Infirmary by Russell Hinton
cx Company be accepted. Mrs. Burrows' check of one thousand

dollars was given for this specific purpose. A brochure for
the Heritage, as submitted by Mr. Paul, was examined and
discussed at length and it was decided to refer this matter
to Mrs. Logan and a committee for further discussion.

Mr. Angell is moving to Room 306.
