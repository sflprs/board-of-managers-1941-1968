CORRESPONDENCE: Mrs. Gunther reported that all correspond-
ence is up to date.

ADMINISTRATOR: Mrs. Bell reported that there are three new
residents -- Mr. and Mrs. Percy Lord, and Miss Lua Minser.

Miss Irene Kelly will move in on April 14th. Mrs. Bell thanked
Board members for bringing books and magazines and Mrs. Houston

for bringing

daffodils. She reported that six new books have

been purchased for the library, and Miss Davidson has donated
14 mystery stories. On Easter there were many visitors and
guests. Dr. Buchholz conducted morning services, and Father
McLaughlin afternoon services. Easter ilies were given by
Dr. Evans and by Mr. Schimmel of Fillmore Florists. Miss
Davidson and Mrs. Fry arranged flowers, and Mrs. Bean made
Easter tray favors for the Infirmary patients. lirs. Bean also
made St. Patrick's Day tray favors. Mr. Korp of the Music
Foundation arranged for several residents to attend a concert,
and Mrs. Fesca handled the tickets. Mrs. Schilling and irs.
Bell attended a Social Welfare meeting on "Manual Regulations
on Ambulatory Aged Persons". The cost of food per person per
day was $1.015, with generally improved menus. Miss Liston is
much better, and Miss Estep, 2 boarder, is in our Infirmary
with a private nurse.

INFIRMARY COMMITTEE: Mrs. Schilling reported that there are
21 patients in the Infirmary.

FINANCE AND PERSONNEL COMMITTEE: Miss Bridge reported that

the Directors approved our budget of $407,789.00 for opera-
tingcexpenses and $2,232.30 for capital outlay at their

March 1, 1967, meeting. Upon recommendation of the Com-
mittee, it was moved, seconded and carried that we buy Mr.
Angell's furniture for $85.00 and that we install three showers
at $40.00 a piece. On the basis of the Auditor's new inter-
pretation of costs in the Infirmary, Mr. Pedroni is trying to
recover the difference from the Blue Cross, which would be due
us from the State. Mrs. Bell is working with Mr. Debes to
improve and vary our menus. Since we did not raise our budget-
ed amounts for meat or provisions from last year, we may face

higher costs.

Mrs. Bell has asked Mr. Perry to make recommenda-

tions on our roof. Mrs. Rettenmayer reported two leaks in the

hobby room.
