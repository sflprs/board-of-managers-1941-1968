The regular monthly meeting of the Board of Managers of the

San Francisco Ladies' Protection and Relief Society was held Aug-
ust 26, 1963 at 10:30 AM at 3400 Laguna Street - Mrs, Ernest Gunther,
President, presiding.

Members Present: Miss Bridge and Mesdames Bowes, Craig, Griffith,
Tandis, Lawry, Lloyd, Logan, Lyon, Metcalf, Nigh, Porter, Skene,
Charles Smith, Scott Smith, and Schilling.

Absent_with Excuses: Mesdames Stephenson, Rettenmayer, Bruck, Krick
and Perry.

Before calling the meeting to order, Mrs. Gunther asked the members
to join her in observing a minute of silence in memory of our late
Board member Helen (Mrs. E. E. ) Blackie, who died July 26, 1963.

The Secretary read the minutes of the July 23 meeting, which were
approved as read.

Treasurer: Miss Bridge, Treasurer, read the Financial report.
Operating Balance July 31, 1963......$18,132.34.

It was moved, seconded and carried that the Treasurer's report be

placed on file.

Administrator: Mrs. Pogson reported that Mrs. Jane B. Steele had
completed her probationary period. The Board voted to accept her as
a permanent resident. The death of Mrs. Edna Steward at Agnew on
August 26 was announced with regret.

Mrs. Bruck's home in Ross, which was tremendously enjoyed by forty-
