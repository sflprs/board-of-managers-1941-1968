Finance & Personnel: Miss Bridge, Chairman. It was movea,

Seconded and carried to adopt the recommendation of the Finance
and Personnel Committee that $1,172.00 be allocated to furnish
a two-unit room in the Infirmary. Miss Bridge pointed out

that this would in no way interfere with Mrs. Bruck's plan
to do all or part of the room.

It was moved, seconded and carried to adopt the recommendation
of the Finance & Personnel Committee to allow Charlie Wong

to stay in his room until Mrs. Pogson can find him a permanent
home.

Admissions: Mrs. Stephenson, Chairman, announced that on Jyne
Qth letters had been sent to 15 people admitting them to the
Home and letters had been sent to three people regretting that
they would not be able to enter the Home. On June 10, four
people were interviewed for possible places on the waiting list.

It was moved, seconded and carried to admit Miss Elma MacDougal
to The Heritage as a permanent resident on June 28, at which
time she will have successfully passed her probationary period,

Mrs. Porter asked the Board to consider increasing the maximum
monthly rate to something above $250. Since Mr. Anderson is
on vacation and has the figures and since the addition of 25
new residents will lower the per capita cost, it was suggested
that this be postponed until the next meeting. Miss Bridge
mentioned that Barrett Hindes used to keep a chart on this

and suggested that Fentress Hill be asked to continue the chart.

Building & Maintenance: Mrs. Nigh, Chairman, informed the
Board that the painting of three rooms, a shower, the elevator

and spots around the heaters has been completed.

Mr. Thornton has asked Mr. Rodoni for an estimate on plumbing
and heating maintenance.

Rooms & Furnishings: In the absence of Mrs. Marshall, Chairman,
whi is in Europe, there was no report.

Dining Room & Kitchen: Mrs. Porter, Chairman, announced that the
cost of food per person per day in May amounted to 78 & 1/2c and
that, according to Mr. Debes, the life of the present deep freeze
is limited. A replacement, which should be put in the kitchen
instead of the basement for the convenience of the cooks, will
cost in the neighborhood of $2,600. Mrs. Porter will meet with
lr. Debes again and report back to the Board at its next meeting.
Garden: In the absence of Mrs. Hinman, Chairman, Mrs. Pogson
reported that the services of Anton, the gardener, will be
terminated. He will be given three weeks vacation with pay.

Mrs. Pogson and Mrs, Hinman have been interviewing possible
replacements,
