Mrs. Gunther proposed a vote of thanks to Mrs. Rettenmayer for the
generous gifts of her time and abilities.

Entertainment: Mrs. Skene absent.

Mrs. Craig said the Colonial
ames

ociety will show a movie of Williamsburg to THE HERITAGE.

Slides of Europe have been shown by Mr. Letrodec's son and daughter,
and were much enjoyed.

Personnel: No report.

Dining Room: Mrs. Porter, Chr., reported meal costs of 83.1 cents
per person per day.

Building and Maintenance: Miss Bridge reported the painting of
three rooms and the elevator.

Garden: Mrs. Hilbert, Chr., reported all going well.
Library: Mrs. Matignon, Chr., - no report.

Rooms and Eurnishings: Mrs. Blackie, Chr., reported estimate of the
coming year was included in the Treasurer's report. Draperies in the

living-room have to be replaced due to sunburn, and some re-uphol-
stry must be done.

Visiting: Mrs. Lyon, Chr., reported all new comers happy and most
enthusiastic.

Cor. Secretary: In the absence of Mrs. Skene, Mrs. Gunther read a
note of appreciation from Mrs. Wm. Leckey for the tender care of her

father, Rev. Harlan Bailey, during his final illness.

Mrs. Scott Smith asked for and received a leave of absence for two
months.

Mrs. Gunther reported the generous donation of $100.00 from
Mr. Stetson G. Hindes. It was agreed this should be spent on phono-
graph records.

There being no further business, the meeting was adjourned.
