VISITING COMMITTEE: Mrs. Craig reported several visits

to Residents, including Miss Kelly and Mr. and Mrs. Rochfort.
Mrs. Logan thanked Mrs. Craig for visiting, and urged other
Board members to visit when time permits.

OLD BUSINESS: Mrs, Logan announced that, in accordance
with a suggestion at the Annual Meeting, a joint committee
has been selected from the Board of Directors and the
Board of Managers to consider a new admissions system.

Mr. Logan, Mr. West, Miss Bridge and Mrs. Nigh are members
of this Committee.

NEW BUSINESS: Mrs. Nigh thanked Mrs, Stephenson for taking
over the Admissions Committee in her absence. Mrs. Nigh
also said that she and Mrs. Bell would attend a meeting of
the Northern California Homes for the Aged on May 25th.

Mrs. Logan announced that members of the Committe to
study the possibility of building a recreation center met
recently with Mr. Thornton, the Architect. The possibility
of buying the property adjoining the Francisco Street flats
and of getting permission to build from the Planning Come
mission was discussed. Possibilities of enlarging The
Heritage instead of building a recreation center, or of
using surplus income in some sort of Community work are
also under discussion. Releasing the present recreation
area would make it available for rest areas for the help,
in turn making the present rest areas available for more
rooms for residents. Tentative plans are being formulated
for a guest room with private bath, which the present guest
room does not have.

Mrs. Logan announced that the new list of Committees
would be out soon, and requested that the present Committees
function until that time.

There being no further busines, the Meeting was adjourned
at 12:00.
