Infirmary:

Mrs. Schilling, Chairman, said the epidemic of a
mild type of influenza was on the wane. During its height,
temporary extra nurses were employed. All the nursing staff
deserve special praise and thanks for their untiring care of all
our patients during this period. Mrs. Schilling showed the clothes
hangers being made by Mrs. Vickery for each Infirmary room, and
exhibited the Easter tray favors sent by Mrs. Byrne. She
demonstrated the use of handtowels as Infirmary bib-napkins and
any additional linen of this kind would be gratefully received.
The new gurney has arrived and the nurses find it very useful.
Finance and Personnel: Mrs. Nigh, reporting in Miss Bridge's
absence, said the Committee had met March 17. The cost of the
planters in the Garden House, now installed and planted, would
be taken from Surplus Funds.

It was thought the cost of the new
heaters in the building could be absorbed in the Building Contract,

and the cost of painting on the lower floors absorbed in the
Painting Contract. The Finance Committee moved, seconded and
carried that the decision for the estimate for planting the

Laguna Street garden be referred to the Original Garden committee;
i.e., the Building Committee, the Architect and Landscape
Architect. The Committee moved, seconded and carried that an

estimate for a new directory Board for residents’ names be
obtained from the Tablet and Ticket Company.

The Board of Directors has approved the $24,900 monthly Budget.
The auditor reports a credit of $4,012 for the past year.

He
also reports the cost of care, excluding administrative costs,
average $280 per month per capita. Mr. Anderson's new secretary,
Miss Fahy, is proving very capable. Mrs. Gunther said that with
the Board's approval Miss McDougal would receive financial help
from the Tittle Fund during her probationary period. The Board
approved this.

Mrs. Gunther announced many donations had been
received in memory of Barrett Hindes.
Admissions: Mrs. Stephenson, Chairman, said Miss McDougal had
entered the Heritage March 28. Four more single applicants,
and two couples, have met with the Admissions Committee. The
Committee has approved three applications and sent them to the
Board of Directors for confirmation.
