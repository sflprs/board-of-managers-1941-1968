The regular meeting of the San Francisco Ladies! Protection and
Relief Society Board of Managers was held on Tuesday, February 26th
at 10:30 a.m. at 3400 Laguna Street; Mrs. William H, Nigh, Jr.,
President, presiding,

Present were: Miss Bridge and Mesdames Scott Smith, Charles Smith,
Bruck, Krick, Rettenmayer, Gunther, Blackie, Logan, Perry,
Griffith, Porter, Bowes, Skene, Landis, Lloyd and Lawry.

Excused: Mesdames Matignon, Stephenson, Noble, Lyon, Schilling,
Kirkwood, Metcalf and Craig.

The secretary réad the minutes of the January 22, 1963 meeting
which were approved as read.

Operating Bank Balance January 31, 1963--$1551.17 overdrawn.

It was moved, seconded and carried that the treasurer's report

be placed on file.
