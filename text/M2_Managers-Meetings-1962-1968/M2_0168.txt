be occupied. She reported with regret the death of Mrs. Gertrude Charruau.
She reported the following gifts: a Keith painting; a portable television
set for the Infirmary; two cases of wine bottled for "The Heritage", fresh
cherries; books for the library; end $25.00 to be used for the Infirmary.

making Infirmary favors for all holidays. The cost of food per person

per day was 93 cents in May.
