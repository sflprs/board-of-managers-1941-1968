New Business: Mrs. Gunther said she would ask Mr. Jenkins

to speak on the Taxation problem at the Annual Meeting. She
suggested Dr. McKenzie be asked to luncheon before the Annual
Meeting. She announced the Tea at the Protestant Episcopal
Home on April 29 to which all Board members are invited. Mrs.
Gunther asked the Corresponding Secretary to write letters

of thanks to Mr. Thornton and to the foreman in charge of

the renovations for their untiring efforts which have resulted
in the remodeling being carried out with minimum disturbance
to The Heritage.

There being no further business, the meeting was adjourned.
