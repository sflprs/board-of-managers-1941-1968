Administrator: Mrs. Pogson reported that Mrs. Antoinette Helm
and Miss Margaret Stuart are new residents. All rooms, with the
exception of one suite, are occupied.

The following gifts have been presented to "The Heritage” -

1. A handsome silver Lazy Susan now on the dining room
buffet from Mrs. Scott Knight Smith in memory of
Mrs. Reginald Knight Smith.

2. A check from Mrs. Skene in memory of Barrett Hindes.

3. An unusually fine antique clock for the library and 4
large mirror for the front stair case from Mrs. John
Procter, a recent resident.

4, Mrs. Bruck has added an ottoman and two pictures to
the Infirmary room furnished in memory of Barrett Hindes.

5. Miss Brewer, a resident, has given money to purchase
two ottomans for the Infirmary.

6. Mrs. Page, a relative of Mr. Letroadec, has given a
large quantity of beautiful table linen.

The Department of Social Welfare has scheduled a meeting of
Board members and Administrators for September 23. Mrs. Knight
Smith and Mrs. Pogson will represent The Heritage.

Mrs. Irene Hinz, social worker for the Department of Social
Welfare, resigned because of ill-health. Her place has been
taken by Mrs. Joan Vaughn, the new representative for this area-

Dr. McKenzie will give flu shots to all employees tomorrow and
to any residents who desire this service.

Infirmary: Mrs. Schilling, Chairman, reported that there are 18
patients in the Infirmary at the present time, An extra nurse
was hired for two weeks because one patient was very ill. Dr.
McKenzie will be on vacation during October. Dr.Henderson will
take his place.
