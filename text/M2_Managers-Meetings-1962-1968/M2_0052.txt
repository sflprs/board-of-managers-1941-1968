ANNUAL MEETING MAY 9, 1963
EE, 1963

The Annual Meeting of the Board of Directors and the Board of
Managers of the San Francisco Ladies! Protection and Relief Society
was held on Thursday, May 9, 1963 at 2:00 p.m. at 3400 Laguna street.
Mr, Charles Bradley, President of the Directors Board, presided.
Minutes of the meeting were recorded by Mr. Anderson. Following

the adjournment of the meeting an Organization Meeting of the Board
of Managers was held, Mrs. William Nigh, President, presiding.
Present were: Miss Bridge and Mesdames Bruck, Craig, Griffith,
Gunther, Landis, Lawry, Lloyd, Logan, Lyon, Noble, Porter, Rettenmayer,
Skene, Charles Smith, Scott Smith, Stephenson and Schilling.

Mrs, Nigh called for the report of the Nominating Committee for
officers of the Board of Managers for the ensuing year. This was
read by the Secretary. There being no further nominations from the
floor, the Secretary was instructed to cast the ballot and the

following were elected for 1963-1964:

President .......... Mrs. Ernest L, Gunther
lst Vice President ... .. Mrs. William H. Nigh, Jr.
2nd Vice President ... . Mrs. James Skene
Treasurer... ..... . Miss Helen Bridge
Recording Secretary ... . Mrs. Francis Lloyd

Corresponding Secretary . . Mrs. John T. Stephenson
Mrs, Gunther was installed as President and presided for the remainder
of the meeting. *
A vote of thanks to Mrs. Nigh for her five years of devoted service was
proposed by Mrs. Noble. Mrs. Porter spoke briefly of the appreciation
of both Boards for Mrs. Nigh's devoted work.
After discussion it was decided that the fourth Tuesday of the month
be continued as the regular meeting day.

There being no further business the meeting was adjourned.
