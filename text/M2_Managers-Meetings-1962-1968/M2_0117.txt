TREASURER'S REPORT: Miss Bridge reported that, according to
the authority delegated to it at the Special Meeting of the
Board of Directors on December 6, 1967, the Executive Committee
of the Board of Directors has approved the 1968 budget of
$529,807.00 and the separate capital outlay budget of $4,053.30
recommended by the Board of Managers. Budget appropriation for
for January, 1968--$44,152.00; expenditures--$41,928.00; under-
expended by $2,224.00. it was moved, seconded and carried that
the Treasurer's Report be accepted and placed on file.

CORRESPONDENCE: In the absence of Mrs. Nishkian, Mrs. Bell re-
prted that all correspondence is up to date.

COMMITTEE REPORTS:

FINANCE AND PERSONNEL: Miss Bridge--no report.

ADMISSIONS: Mrs. Nigh reported that there are three vacant
rooms. Mrs. Frances Stacy, 72, and Mrs. Eva Alves, 81, are on
the immediate waiting list. Three people have been interviewed
recently, and three others will be interviewed today. Upon
recommendation of the Committee, it was moved, seconded and
carried to accept Mrs. Julia Smith, 88, medium income, as a
permanent resident when she completes probation. Mrs. Nigh
also reported the Proposed changes in the Life-Care Agreement,
which have been approved by Mr. Jenkins, our lawyer, who is now
discussing the changes with the Department of Welfare. Upon
recommendation of the Committee, and following discussion, it
was moved, séconded and carried to accept the proposed changes as
detailed. (See attached).

INFIRMARY: Mrs. Schilling reported that there are 26 patients
in the Infirmary, 23 of whom are permanent. New equipment has
been purchased for the Infirmary with money donated by Mrs. Bruck,

Mr. Hindes, Mrs. Burrows and Mrs. Roemer. Mrs. Bruck will also
donate an air purifier.

HOUSE:

GARDEN: Mrs. Porter reported that work on the pools in
the inner courtyard has been completed. A dome spray has been
installed in the pool in the pocket garden, for which a light
will be purchased with money from the garden budget. Mrs. Evans,
our garden consultant, will return on Thursday, after a month's
absence because of her son's death.

FURNISHINGS: Mrs. Marshall is on a leave of absence.
