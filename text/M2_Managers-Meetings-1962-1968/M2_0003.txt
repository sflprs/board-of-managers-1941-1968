Garden: Mrs. Skene reported that the mound in the Parking
Lot is being replanted.

Library and Visiting: Mrs. Lloyd reported several current books
had been donated to the Library. Mrs. Craig and Mrs. Lyon

report everyone they visit seems content, and grateful to be
at The Heritage.

Entertainment and Occupational Activities: Mrs. Rettenmayer
reported a full and varied program of entertainment for January
including travel slides, lectures and music, in addition to the
regular events. A lecturer's desk with a good reading light
has been purchased for the Theater Room. A Valentine Birthday
Party was held for 72, with a special party in the Infirmary

to celebrate Mrs. McCreary's 99th birthday. Miss Liston is
asking for prizes for the Bingo Games.

The Sewing Group
completed 25 puppets for Children's Hospital.

Correspondence and Heritage Happenings:

Mrs. Charles Smith
reported all correspondence was up-to-date, and Heritage

Happenings current issue was at the press.

Mrs. Nigh gave a detailed and interesting report of the

Annual Meeting of California Homes for the Aged held in
Sacramento February 16th and 17th. Reports were given on
by-laws, health and accident insurance and Workmen's Compen-
sation, but the main topic was taxation of non-profit homes.
Speakers included Nicholas Petris, Jesse Unruh, Canon Lazenby,
past President of AAHA, and Thomas Jenkins. It was moved,

seconded and carried that the Association go on record that
"increased services necessitate non-taxation."

There being no further business the meeting was adjourned.

Respectfully submitted,

Vepuuctee L/ A.

Frances Lloyd
