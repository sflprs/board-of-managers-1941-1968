Building and Maintenance: Miss Bridge, Chairman, said the Building
and Maintenance as well as the Building Construction Committees had
met with Mr. Moore (Board of Directors President) and Architect
Thornton. It was suggested an allowance be made in the monthly
Budget for $15 to pay for Heating Maintenance. Later, the Rodoni
Heating Company will submit an exact bid. They will also try a
new element in Room 423; if this is successful, all the 4th floor,
and 10 rooms over the dining-room on the 2nd floor, will have
heating units corrected in a similar manner. There will be new
heating units throughout the main floor. Miss Bridge said the
Norman Copeland Co. had inspected the windows in the Perry Building
and said the frames could not be repaired but were useable. There
was no charge for this service. Mr. Moore said the repair of

small leaks, etc. should come out of the Board of Managers' regular
maintenance funds. Mr. Moore said he felt the matter of furniture
for the Garden House was not the responsibility of the Board of

Directors, but he felt comfortable furniture suitable for our aged
residents should be considered.
