NEW BOARD MEMBERS COMMITTEE: Mrs. Nishkian reported that

the names of Mrs. John Parr Cox and Mrs. Henry Howard have
been submitted for consideration as new Board members. After
discussion, it was moved, seconded and carried that the Com-
mittee meet these people and report to the Board. Qualifica-
tions for Board members were discussed, and it was suggested
that a profile be drawn up as a guide in selecting new members.

OLD BUSINESS: Mrs. Logan urged Board members to spend more time
in getting to know the residents. She said that she would have
cards sent out announcing the birthday parties, so that Board
members could attend on a rotating basis.

Mrs. Staples reported on the brochure for The Heritage. An
informational pamphlet from Neil, Stratford and Kerr could be
printed for $117.00 per 1,000 or $156.00 for 2,500. The essay-
type brochure from Mr. Paul could be printed for $2,865.00 for
2,000 plus a $250.00 fee for Mr. Paul. After discussion, it
was moved, seconded and carried that the President and the
Committee be empowered to use their discretion in ordering a
brochure that is informational in type, rather than essay.

It was moved, seconded and carried that Mr. Paul be sent a
check for $100.00 with a letter thanking him for his time and
services.

i BUSINESS: The question of revision of the by-laws was
discussed, and Mrs. Logan said that she would appoint a com-
mittee to make the revisions.

There being no further business, the meeting was adjourned
in memory of Mr. Chalmers Graham at 12:15 P.M., March 28, 1967.

Respectfully submitted,

, Joan Bailey
Secretary
