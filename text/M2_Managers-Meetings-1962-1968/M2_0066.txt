Building and Maintenance: Miss Bridge, chairman, no report.
Garden: Mrs. Porter, chairman, - Mrs, Skene reported that the

center court garden had been dug up earlier than planned because

it was expedient to have the work done when the bull-dozer was

on hand. The Board of Directors accepted the cost of this.

Mrs. Porter showed plans for the Interior Court made by Mr.

Kawamoto. She said plans for the planting of the Parking Lot

had been sent to the landscaping contractor, Alex Catani.

Library: Mrs. Matignon, chairman, absent. Mrs. Pogson acknow-
_ ledged the gift of many books from Mrs. Nigh.

Rooms and Furnishings: Mrs. Blackie, chairman, commented on

the improvements in the Hobby Room and acknowledged, with thanks,

the gifts of Mrs. Nigh of stands and flower containers for the

theatre room and for several yards of upholstry material; she

noted that when the changes are made in the first floor hall

due to the new Infirmary, all usable articles such as rugs,

mirrors, etc., will be saved for future use.

Visiting: Mrs. Lyon, chairman, reported all those who she visits

are happy and grateful.

Chapel Committee: Mrs. Craig, chairman, reported she had visit-

ed twenty eight Episcopalians in The Heritage. A Communion
Service to be held on the fourth Sunday of each month at 3 p.M.,
administered by a Canon of Grace Cathedral and at which all
sects would be welcome was proposed. It was moved, seconded

and carried such a service be instituted.

Nominating Committee: Mrs. Charles Smith, chairman, welcomed
Mrs. Lawry to the Board and reported Mrs. Bean and Mrs. Shurtliffe
were unable to accept the Board's invitation at this time.
Corresponding Secretary: Mrs. Stephenson said she had written
letters of thanks to all who had been so generous at Christmas,
She read a letter from Mrs. Morrison regretting she could not
return to the Board at present.

New Business: Mrs. Skene read an invitation to all the Board

Members to a tea on Feb. 14 at the Protestant Episcopal Home,

There being no further business the meeting was adjourned
