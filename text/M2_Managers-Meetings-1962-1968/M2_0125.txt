The minutes of the October 24, 1967 meeting were read and approved.
TREASURER'S REPORT: Miss Bridge reported:

Per capita cost for October 1967 of 115 residents $ 379.00
Balance in operating bank account October 31, 1967...$28,271.00.

Budget appropriation--$33,983.00. Overexpended by $3,361.00,
principally due to increased medical, dental, hospital and drug
expenditures. Mr. Pedroni, the Accountant, works out an itemized
list of expenditures each month so that all costs are known.
Infirmary costs per capita for October 1967 were $20.25 per diem.
It was moved, seconded and carried that the Treasurer's Report be
accepted and placed on file.

CORRESPONDENCE: Mrs. Nishkian reported that all correspondence is
up to date. She reported the following gifts: a television set
from Mrs. Morgan; a book on design from Mrs. Rettenmayer. She
read letters of appreciation from Mrs. Fesca, a resident, and

Mrs. Fry, the Executive Secretary. It was decided that thank you
letters for gifts need not be written to Board members or staff.
Notices acknowledging gifts for tax purposes are sent from The
Heritage.

COMMITTEE REPORTS:

FINANCE AND PERSONNEL: Miss Bridge reported that the Fall Tea
cost $832.00; $850.00 had been budgeted for it. She read an itemi-
zed list of the individual salary raises recommended by the Com-
mittee, After discussion, it was moved, seconded and carried that
the individual salary increases be accepted and begin as of January
1, 1968. The total payroll will be worked out by Mr. Pedroni and
presented to the Board of Directors at their meeting on December 6,
1967. A policy of minimum and maximum wages is being worked on
by the Committee. The Committee will meet on December 12, 1967
and requested estimated budgets for next year before that date.
