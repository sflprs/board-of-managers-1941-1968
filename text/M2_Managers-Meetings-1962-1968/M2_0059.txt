Infirmary: Mrs. Kirkwood, Chairman, absent. Mrs. Pogson reported

eleven residents in the Infirmary, and Mrs. Young in Presbyterian

Hospital with a broken hip. On March 12th Mr. Zem of St. Luke's

Hospital came to "The Heritage" to advise on furniture for the
Infirmary. Mr. Thornton (architect) and Miss Sutherland are meeting
the Infirmary Committee Thursday to discuss the Infirmary color scheme,

Occupational Activity: Mrs. Rettenmayer, Chairman, reported her
sewing group continue to make articles for the Infirmary--three

dozen feeding bibs have been finished; seven baby sacques for the

Needlework Guild are in the making. There were 72 persons at the

March Birthday Party. Mrs. Rettenmayer said she could not be

present at the April Party, and hoped many Board Members would come
to help Mrs. Logan. The Board's Antique and Hobby Show on March 2ith

was of much interest and delight to the residents.
