The Recording Secretary read the Minutes of the April 25,
1967, Meeting, which were approved as read.

TREASURER'S REPORT: Miss Bridge reported:

Per capita cost for April of 104 Residents ...$ 386.00
Balance in operating bank account April 30 ...$17,417.00.

She also noted that there was a $1,500.00 per month raise
in Maintenance Salaries, and that salaries in general are
going up. It was moved, seconded and carried that the
Treasurer's Report be accepted and placed on file.

CORRESPONDENCE: Mrs. Gunther -- No report.

ADMINISTRATOR: Mrs. Bell reported with regret the deaths

of Mrs. Isabel Ferguson, Mrs. Lillian McCune, and Mrs.
Estelle Walker. She also reported that the windows have
been cleaned. Major water damage from a clogged sewer line
was prevented by Mr. Vogel, a resident, who discovered
water in the furnace room. Mr. Perry, the architect,
located the blueprints so that the source of the water
could be traced, and a plumber made the necessary repairs.
Mr. Perry also reviewed the blueprints to pring placement
of pipes, etc., up to date. The intercom system is being
overhauled. Mrs. Peabody has loaned a colored crayon drawe-
ing to be hung in the sitting room near Bay Street. Mrs.
O'Connell is no longer on the desk in the evenings; Mrs.
Lillian Thompson has been hired to replace her in accord-
ance with the new policy under which residents may not be
paid employees. Mrs. Bell also reported that Mr. W. D.
Orloff of Marsh and McLennan met with her and Mr. Pedroni
to lay the foundation of a retirement plan for the employees.
Such a plan would help stabilize employment, particularly
in the Infirmary, where the turnover is greatest. Mr. Orloff
suggested that the matter be placed in the hands of four
persons, two from the Board of Managers and two from the
Board of Directors. He also suggested that management carry
the whole expense of the retirement plan, rather than hav-
ing employees contribute to it. Mrs. Jenny Charters has
taken Residents on rides through Golden Gate Park. Miss
Davidson has been watering the new lawn in the pocket garden.
Mrs. Bell reported that she visited the Carmel Valley Manor,
The Hacienda, and The Canterbury on a recent trip to Carmel,
and that as she saw each new place she realized how unique
The Heritage is.

INFIRMARY COMMITTEE: Mrs. Schilling reported that there
are 18 patients in the Infirmary, none of whom is critical-
ly ill. Mrs. Hallum, the Head Nurse, has returned from

her vacation.
