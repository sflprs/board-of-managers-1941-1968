Revisions of the By-Laws

1. The Board of Directors is now called the Board of Trustees; the Board of
Managers is now called the Board of Directors.

2. The Board of Directors (formerly called Managers) will be a rotating
board because of the requirements of the Department of Social Welfare.

3. The name of the Infirmary Committee has been changed to the Medical
Committee. |

4. ‘The Finance and Personnel Committee has been separated into two committees.

5. The question as to whether or not the Recording Secretary should be on
Executive Committee has been referred to Mr. Jenkins.

6. an Assistant Recording Secretary and an Assistant Treasurer have been
added to the list of officers of the Board of Directors (formerly called
Managers).

7. The question as to whether the specific duties of the Administrator as |
to hiring and firing belong in the By-Laws or in the Standing Rules has }
been referred to Mr. Jenkins.

8. A Joint Committee consisting of two members appointed by the President
of the Board of Trustees (formerly called Directors) and two members
appointed by the President of the Board of Directors (formerly called
Managers) has been created.

9. article X, Special Need Cases, is new, and mst be in to demonstrate
that we are a charitable organization. The question of the possible
addition of "or homes" in line 3, the possible change of"but" to "and"
in line 2, and the possible delition of "specially" in line 8 has been
referred to Mr. Jenkins.
