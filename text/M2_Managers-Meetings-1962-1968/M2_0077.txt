Kitchen snd Dining Room: Mrs. Pogson reported the cost of food per
person per day for August was 88¢.

Maintenance and Repair: Miss Bridge, Chairuwan, stated that there was
no report.

Garden: Mrs. Porter, Chairman, stated that the new "Pocket Garden"
is being used. She thanked Mrs. Nigh for carrying through the project
in her absence.

Rooms and Furnishings: Mrs. Blackie, Chairman, reported that work
in the Hobby Room will start when the kitchen installations are com-
pleted.

New Members: Mrs. Smith, Chairman, reported that Mrs. Dobson and
Yrs. Gilette feel that they cannot at this time become members of
the Board of Managers. Mrs. Bowes stated that Mrs. Walter Schil-
ling will become a member, and will serve on the Infirmary Com-
mittee. Mrs. Porter requested Mrs. Jackson Bean's name be added
to the list.

Corresponding Secretary: Mrs. Nigh reported writing letters of
thanks to Mr. Evans for his gift of $400.00 for the "Pocket Garden",
and to Mrs. Jane Thatcher for a clock for the Linen Room. ©

Qld Business: Mrs. Nigh stated that high bids have delayed break-
ing ground for the Infirmary. Members of the Board of Managers will
meet with members of the Board of Directors to discuss this matter.

New Business: It was moved, seconded and carried that the payroll
be placed in a separate account, and the checks to be signed by one

of the following persons: The President; The Treasurer; The Adminis-
trator.

There being no further business, it was moved, seconded and carried
that the meeting be adjourned.

Respectfully submitted

Barbara Olney Landis
Assistant Secretary
