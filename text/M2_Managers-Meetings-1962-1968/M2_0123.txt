On December 1, L90/, Dr. McKenize retired as Medical Direc-
tor, and Dr. Henderson became the new Medical Director. The
Board of Managers sent Dr. McKenzie a portable color tele-
vision set in appreciation of his service to The Heritage
as Medical Director since 1942, Mrs. Schilling read a
letter of thanks from Dr. McKenzie. The Committee met
recently with Dr. Henderson and Miss Hughes, Head Nurse,

to discuss policies and preventative medicine. Infirmary
costs based on 5 months in 1967 were $22.09 per person per
day, or 662.00 per person per month.

HOUSE:

GARDEN: Mrs. Porter reported that the repair work on
the pool in the pocket garden will begin soon. Mrs.
Gunther was asked to write a letter of condolence to Mrs.
Evans, our garden consultant, on the death of her son.

FURNISHINGS: Mrs. Marshall's report was read by Mrs.
Howard. Under consideration by the Committee and Miss
Sutherland, our decorating consultant, are: extensive
redecoration of the downstairs; disposal of unusable
furniture in the storage room; restricting the temporary
use of the storage room for residents' personal furniture
to items approved by the Committee; redoing the fourth
floor sitting room, for which $833.00 was set aside from
last year's budget; redoing the downstairs sun room. The
loom has been given to the Senior Citizens' Center at
Aquatic Park.

RECREATION: Mrs. Rettenmayer reported several special acti-
vities in addition to the regular concerts, lectures and
films: the Christmas Party on December 21, 1967, under
the chairmanship of Mrs. Nishkian, who also donated wine
for Christmas and New Year's dinners; a bingo party; a

bus trip for 38 residents to see the Christmas lights,
courtesy of an anonymous donor; attendance at the San
Francisco Symphony for 21 residents, with tickets arranged
by Mrs. Metcalf. Details of these and other events may

be found in the January, 1968, edition of "Heritage
Happenings". Mrs. Rettenmayer also displayed a metal
decoration done by Miss Frye, a resident.

LIBRARY: Mrs. Kent reported that 15 books have been donated
to the library, including several specific books requested
by residents.

"HERITAGE HAPPENINGS": Mrs. Charles Smith reported that the
January issue of "Heritage Happenings" will go to press
shortly.

VISITING: Mrs. Perry reported that she had made several
visits, particularly in the Infirmary. Mrs. Craig also
visited residents.

ADMINISTRATOR'S REPORT: Mrs. Bell reported, with regret, the
death of Mrs. Lulu Osborn on January 7, 1968. Mrs. Osborn
left her estate to The Heritage, a gift of approximately
$20,000.00. Other gifts included: $1,000 from Mrs. Burrows
for use in the Infirmary; an antique lamp, valued at about
$300.00, from Mrs. Ritter. Mrs. Bean's Infirmary tray
favors continue to be appreciated. Prizes are needed for
Bingo games. Mrs. Casper, Social Director, has been very
helpful, both to individual residents, and to the group in
general. She has held several fireside gatherings, and is
enlisting more and more resident participation in various
activities. There was a vote of thanks for Mrs. Bell and
Mrs. Casper for their excellent work, particularly during
the holidays. The Christmas pool for tips for employees

did not work out in practice, so the matter will be given
further consideration. The cost of food per person per day
in December, 1967, was $1.13. The Infirmary cost per person
er day for December, 1967, was 5.19.
