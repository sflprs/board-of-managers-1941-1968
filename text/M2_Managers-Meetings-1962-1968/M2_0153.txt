ADMISSIONS COMMITTEE: Mrs. Nigh reported that a suite fora
married couple is now available and that applicants are being
considered for occupancy of this unit.

HOUSE COMMITTEE MAINTENANCE: Miss Bridge reported that re-
pairs have been made to the roof and the painting previously
agreed upon is in progress.

FURNISHINGS: No report.

LIBRARY COMMITTEE: In the absence of Mrs. Knight Smith,
Mrs. Pogson reported that several new books have been
purchased.

RECREATION COMMITTEE: Mrs. Rettenmayer reported that December
had been an especially enjoyable and busy month. There had
been either a lecture or a musicale each Tuesday. The grand-
son of one of our residents, Mrs. Downing, gave a most inter-
esting talk on "Air Force Camps". Both the Christian Endeavour
Group and the Presbyterian Church Choir rendered Christmas
Carols. Travel movies were shown oreevening and the Christmas
party, decorated by Mrs. Barbiere, under the able chairman-
ship of Mrs.Nishkian, was an outstanding success.

VISITING COMMITTEE: Mrs. Perry reported that she had enjoyed
several happy visits with Residents and urged members of the
Board to please pay calls as time permitted.

NEW BOARD MEMBERS COMMITTEE: Mrs. Joseph Staples, a new
member of our Board, was present at the January meeting.

OLD BUSINESS: None

NEW BUSINESS: The Board of Managers discussed at length
Miss Bridge's report on the implications of medical certi-
fication. After due consideration, it was the consensus of
opinion of the Board that we were not in favour of partici-
pating, owing to the state and federal regulations under
which we would then have to operate the Infirmary. This

whole subject of medical certification is to be brought up
again at the next meeting.

Mrs. Nigh presented a gold watch from the Board to
Mrs. Pogson.

The Meeting was adjourned in memory of Dr. Edwin Bruck and
Mr. Philip Landis at twelve o'clock noon, January 24, 1967.

Respectfully submitted,

HELEN ST. GOAR GUNTHER
for

Joan Bailey, Secretary
