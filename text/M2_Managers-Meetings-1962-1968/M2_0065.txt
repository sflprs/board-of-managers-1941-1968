Infirmary: Mrs. Kirkwood, chairman, reports that all is going
well in the Infirmary and that her committee met on January
eighth to go over the Infirmary survey with Mrs. Maxine Bishop.
The survey was accepted and the suggestions are being implemented;;
as a result Mrs. Rathman is able to reduce her staff to 16-3/5.
All Mrs. Bishop's suggestions are applicable to the new Infirmary
as well as to the present one.

Finance and Personnel: Miss Bridge, chairman, had no report

but said her committee would meet this saionth to work’ out the
year's budget with Mr. Anderson.

Admissions: Mrs. Gunther, chairman, said there were no vacan-

cies; Mrs. Charleston had returned from the hospital and on

Dr. McKenzie's advice was recommencing her probationary period;
Miss Patricia Davidson and Mrs. Charles Pratt have completed
their probationary periods and it was moved, seconded and carried
that they become Life Contract Members of The Heritage. Mrs.
Gunther sajd new application forms, which has been approved

by the Board of Directors, were ready for the printer.
Occupational Activities: Mrs. Rettenmayer, chairman, reported
the installation of a dishwasher in the Hobby Room and the
papering of the west wall of that room; Hobby activities had been
at a minimun last month due to Christmas festivities, but arti-
cles are still being made for the Infirmary; she showed the
beautiful afghans knitted by Miss Roustand, a resident. Mrs.

Bruck immediately made a gift of twenty dollars for more

wool. The birthday party for January will be held on January
29th.

Entertainnent: Mrs. Logan, chairman, said everything was

very quiet after all the Christmas parties.

Dining Room and Kitchen: Mrs. Rettenmayer, chairman, reported

food costs up due to time of year and Christmas entertaining.
