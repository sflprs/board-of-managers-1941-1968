The regular monthly meeting of the Board of Managers of the San
Francisco Ladies’ Protection and Relief Society sas hela at
10:30 a.m, on Tuesday, February 23, 1965 at 3400 laguna Street;
Mrs. William H. Nigh presiding in the absence of the President,
Mrs. Scott Knight Smith.

Present: Mesdames Craig, Bowes, Griffith, Lyon Logan, Lloyd
Metcalf, Nigh, Porter, Rettenmayer, Skene, Charles Smith.

Excused: Miss Bridge and Mesdames Bruck, Gunther, Houston, Noble,
~ Stephenson, Schilling and Mrs. Scott Knight Smith.

Mrs. Lloyd, acting for the Secretary, read the report of the
January 26th meeting which was accepted as read,

Treasurer: Mrs. Metcalf read the Treasurer's report in the
absence of Miss Bridge:

January deficit $1,008.59 Transfer from Directors' Account $24,900.00
Balance in Operating Account Jan. 31, 1965... . . $1,086.15

Cost per capita 91 Residents ....... . $202,497
" " " 16 Infirmary patients ... 467.75
" " " Total Residents average 242.14

It was moved, seconded and carried the Treasurer's report be placed
on file.

Administrator: Mrs. Pogson announced Mrs. Downing had become a
Resident of The Heritage February 2nd, and noted the death of
Mrs. Laura Howard February 19th. She said licenses both for The
Heritage and The Infirmary, the latter for 20 patients, had been
renewed for the ensuing year by the Board of Social Welfare and
Department of Public Health. A gift from Mrs. A, C. Griffith of
a "walker" for the Infirmary was reported.

Mrs, Pogson accompanied Mrs. Nigh to a meeting of The Northern

California Homes for the Aging at the Lake Park Home in Oakland,
and to the 4th Annual State Conference of CAHFA at Sacramento on
February 17th. .

Infirmary: In the absence of Mrs. Schilling, Mrs. Bowes reported
17 residents in the Infirmary, and 2 at Children's Hospital, one,
Mrs. McDermott has undergone a successful eye operation; the
second, Mrs. Harris, was operated on for the removal of a tumor.
Mrs. Bowes read a notice from the Public Welfare Department
announcing changes in its policy of providing drugs, dentures, etc.

Finance and Personnel: Miss Bridge absent, no report

Building and Maintenance: Mrs. Nigh said two of the refrigerators
ordered last month had arrived and two more were on order.
painting contract to refresh paint in various parts of the building
has been signed.

Admissions: Mrs. Logan reported that Mrs. Hewitt, whose probation
period would be up March 5, was hospitalized and her return to
The Heritage doubtful. After six weeks of probation, Mr. and Mrs.
Burke had decided to live in Southern California, another couple
on the active waiting list is ready to take their places. It

was moved, seconded and carried that Miss Kohn and Mr. Kelso be

accepted as Life Care Residents in March.

Rooms _and Furnishings: No report.

Dining Room and Kitchen: Mrs. Porter reported the cost of meals
per person per day in January as 95.6 cents. It has been necessary

to put new shelves in the store-room at a cost of $340.00.
