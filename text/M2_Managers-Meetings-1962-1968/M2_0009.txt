Building & Maintenance: Mrs. Nigh, Chairman, explained that a
contract had been signed with Rodoni & Becker. For the sum of

$55.00 per month, this firm will service and maintain all major
installations.

Dining Room & Kitchen: Mrs. Porter, Chairman, announced that the
September 1964

cost of food per person per day was 90%.
The new deep freeze has been installed for the sum of $2,396.81,

13,500 meals per month are served at The Heritage.

Garden:

Mrs. Hinman, Chairman, reported that two planters have
been purchased and that two more have been ordered.

Perennials
have been planted and seeding is to be done shortly. Two Mughho
pines have been purchased.

Library & Visiting:

Mrs, Craig, Chairman, reported that Mrs.
Lyon is visiting and that more bookshelves are needed in the
Library.

Entertainment:

Mrs. Rettenmeyer, Chairman, reported that the
participation of residents in the planned activities is
excellent.

Because of the background of some of the residents
who have recently come to The Heritage, such as Miss Ladd with
her library experience and Miss Jones with her connections

with the San Francisco Conservatory of Music, a new and

exciting type of entertainment is being made available to
our residents.

There being no further business, it was moved, seconded and
carried that the meeting be adjourned.

Respectfully submitted,

Helen St. G. Gunther
Acting Secretary
