ADMINISTRATOR: Mrs. Bell reported that Miss Liston is ill and is
in Childrens' Hospital. The February birthday Party and the
Antique and Hobby Show were cancelled because of illness, but
lectures and movies continued. The Hills showed films on Mexico.
Mrs. Metcalf arranged for 6 season tickets to the Symphony which
Mrs. Fesca is handling in Miss Liston's absence. Mrs. Staples
offered Chamber Music tickets, which could not be usedbbecause

of transportation problems. Mr. and Mrs. Haas have moved in.

Mr. Day, Hospital Field Representative of the State, inspected

and approved the Infirmary. Mrs. Bell and Mrs. Pogson attended
the January meeting of the Department of Social Welfare. In
February Mrs. Nigh and Mrs. Bell attended the CAHA Conference in
Sacramento. Mrs. Marjorie Fry has been added to the staff asa
full-time Secretary and Girl Friday. Mrs. Bell expressed her
pleasure at being here, and said that she would have a tour of her
cottage for residents. Mrs. Logan expressed the Board's pleasure
at having Mrs. Bell here, and thanked Mrs. Metcalf and Mrs. Staples
for their tickets. Both Mrs. Logan and Mrs. Bell requested maga-
zines and paperback books for residents.

INFIRMARY COMMITTEE: In the absence of Mrs. Schilling, Mrs. Bowes
reported that the Infirmary is filled to capacity (20 beds), and
that, as a result of a survey of the out-patient load conducted

by Mrs. Hallum and Mrs.Fry (319 calls in 6 days, or 34 hours of
work), Miss Crowley has been hired as a part-time nurse on a trial
basis. Two boarders have gone to nursing homes; of the six board-
ers who remain,two are in the Infirmary and are paying full cost
for care.
