Building and Maintenance: Miss Bridge, Chairman, said the work on
the iron railings had been completed, and repairs to loose bricks,
the garden gate latches, and kitchen tiles had been made. Work on

the new sink and cupboards for the Hobby Room will be started next
week, this work financed by Miss Rolph's check.

Rooms and Furnishings: Mrs. Blackie, Chairman, said the sitting
room on the thir oor had been reconditioned. The Japanese flower
prints given by the Vickerys were to be framed, and chairs in the
Lobby were to be upholstered.

Library: Mrs. Porter, Chairman, absent. Mrs. Pogson said about six
ooks had been purchased and several donated by Mrs. Nigh.

Visiting: Mrs. Lyons, Chairman, no report.

Cor. Secretary: Mrs. Stephenson, Chairman, absent. Mrs. Lloyd read
a letter from Miss Rolph thanking the Board for making her an honorary
member, and assuring us of her continued interest.

Mrs. Skene said the final blue-prints for the Infirmary had been re-
ceived. Blue prints are now out for bids, and it is hoped work will
start in October. Mrs. Skene reported a gift of Standard Oil stock
from Mrs. Bruck to be used for the Pocket Garden, and a gift of $400
from Mr. Evans, a boarder, to be used for gardening.

Mrs. Blackie reported on a meeting at the Pilgrim Haven in Los Altos

an ial for aged persons on July 26. She attended with Mrs.
Nigh.

New Business: It was moved, seconded and carried that Mrs. Nigh be
given a vote of thanks for all the time and energy she has given to
the Infirmary plans. A second motion was made, seconded and carried

to thank her for the innumerable hours spent at THE HERITAGE in Mrs.
Pogson's absence on vacation.

The monthly meeting date was discussed and it was voted to continue
meeting on the 4th Tuesday.

Date for the Hobby Show was discussed and set for Tuesday, October 9.
Mrs. Logan suggested the hours be limited - 1:30 to 4 PM was agreed

upon as the best.
The date for the Fall Tea was set for Friday, November 9.

There being no further business, the meeting was adjourned.
