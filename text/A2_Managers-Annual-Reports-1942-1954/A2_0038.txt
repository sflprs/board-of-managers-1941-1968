done for the Home.

A birthday tea was given for Mrs Rose on her ninety-fourth birthday, which she so enjoyed that it really did her a great deal of good.

The usual lovely parties were held at Easter, at Halloween, this especially for the children, at [struck through: Easter] Thanksgiving, and at Christmas, with a puppet show and a singer, as well as the supper, Christmas dinner, and Miss Ash's tea, the day after.

Mrs Craig was the chairman of the very successful Christmas entertainment.

Mrs Shucking, president of the Guild for Crippled children sent quantities of superb fruit and vegetables, from a grower's exhibition. Later, Mrs Shucking sent a box for the children.

Miss Rolph gave some curtains that were perfect.

Mrs Beaver sent donations in memory of Mr Horace Van Sicklen and Mr Peter Beaver, and some clothing. Mrs Perry also gave clothing.

Mrs Landis gave yarn, for the knitting. There were Easter baskets from Mrs Vaughan, Mrs Kluck [?] sent an Easter lily, Mrs [struck through: Easter] Stuart Smith, a hydrangea and candy, and they sent plants again at Christmas. Mrs Hindes gave camellias to the staff and the "Family" at Easter. Mrs Hindes gave earphones for Miss Barlow's radio, and her regular contribution to the "Follow-up Fund"

Mrs Cameron sent flowers at Thanksgiving.

Various small cheques were received from patients and friends, and at Christmas, a cheque from Mr Hutchinson of $200.00 so that each of the "Family" received a gift of $25.00

Mrs Clough's niece sent lights and ornaments for the Christmas tree.

An electric orange juicer came from Miss Lucy Stebbins and in June, a cake from Mrs Moya del Pino on the anniversary of Mrs Halsey Smith's birthday.

A convalescent, Mrs Furst, a German refugee, gave a beautiful embroidered banquet cloth, to the Home, in gratitude. A very ill patient, Mrs Isobel McKenzie who had been cured, here, also in gratitude, gave the Home her treasured set of flat silver.

In February, Miss Beans [?] announced that she had completed her History of the Home, up to 1925. She had had it typed. A Committee was formed
