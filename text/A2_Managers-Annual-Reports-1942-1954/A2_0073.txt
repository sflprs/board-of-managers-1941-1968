The 102nd. Annual Report of the San Francisco Ladies Protection and Relief Society. February 1955 to May 1956

This year will always be remembered as a year of great activity and progress because of the merger of the Ladies Protection and Relief Society and 2700 [?] Pine Street. All committees have worked very hard during the year, not only to carry on their own work, but to help plan the new wing and the resulting enlarged program. Mrs. Craig has been indefatigable in her work on blue prints, by-laws, home furnishing, and liason between the two Boards.

Changes on the Board were the death of our beloved Mrs. Carroll Cambron [?]; and the resignation of Mrs. Reginald Knight-Smith and her subsequent election as an Honorary member of the Board. A leave of absence was granted Miss Zane.

The Family as a whole kept surprisingly well during the year thereby resulting in considerable saving in doctor and hospital bills. During the year death came to Dr. Taylor. Mrs. Lerox [?] joined the Family, as did Miss Hoyt [?], Miss Pasley [?], and Mrs. Seargent [?]. Mrs. de Mari's guardianship was taken over by Santa Clara Co. with Miss Ash as her official guardian. Mrs. Krick and the Family Committee called continually during the year thereby maintaining that close relationship between the Family and the Board.

The Convalescent Committee under Mrs. Bowes found during the year that its work was changing from the former quick turn over of convalescent care to longer care of chronic cases. However the following figures demonstrate the need for the Department.
