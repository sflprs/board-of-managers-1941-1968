All of us wished that our European travelers, Mrs. Craig, Mrs. Lyon and Mrs. Towne could have seen the Home on the day of the reception. During the year they had purchased a charming Victorian sofa and a Pembroke table for the reception room on the left of the entrance hall, also a chair upholstered in needlepoint. This was placed in the front hall and was the gift of Mrs. Krusi. A check from Mrs. Joseph Moore was used for the Victorian sofa. This sofa and a chair in the Convalescent Depart-ment had required upholstering. Miss Ralph sent a check for the Pembroke table. Mrs. Beaver and her sister presented the Home with a lamp. Mrs. Nye purchased a shade for it as a gift. New furniture and a couch for the "Family's" second floor sun room arrived in time for the reception. The smoking room has been painted and Mrs. Nye and Mrs. Krusi have selected attractive new furniture for it.

The building is in excellent repair. In the spring Mrs. Towne and the Construction and Repair Committee supervised the painting of the diningroom, livingroom, kitchen, pantry, back halls and several upstairs bedrooms. Light aluminium buckets have replaced the heavy ones in the elevators, and five glass shower doors have been installed. The boiler in the flats has been repaired and a drain pipe near the front door of the Home has been replaced. At the request of the fire department, new fire alarm boxes have been placed in the halls and innovations made whereby bells ring in case of fire. The installation of a buzzer system has facilitated calling convalescent patients to the telephone and is appreciated by the Staff. Because of a minor explosion in the kitchen stove, it recently became necessary to purchase a new one.

Mrs. Hilbert and the Garden Committee by careful planning have kept the garden in bloom most of the year, with a plentiful supply of flowers for cutting. Henry, our gardiner, and those willing volunteers, Mrs. Mercado and Miss Walter, are untiring in their efforts to keep the grounds in good order. The gift of primroses and other plants from Miss Ralph, perenial phlox from Mrs. Allen, two large philodendrons in Chinese jars from Mrs. Krusi, and many plants sent to the Home on the day of the reception have added color and interest to the garden. A substantial saving was effected this year with the purchase of bulbs from the de Jaeger Company.
