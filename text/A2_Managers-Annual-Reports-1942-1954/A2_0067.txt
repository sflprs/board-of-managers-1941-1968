The House Activities Committee was chaired by Mrs. Byrne, who met with the Family and Convalescents for sewing and chatting during the first half of the year. Mrs. Krusi was appointed chairman of the Bazaar, held on November sixth, and with the invaluable help of Mrs. Hilbert and others the Bazaar was a great success netting $840.00. Five dollars from the Bazaar Fund was given each member of the Family at Easter, and $47.00 [?] given each one at Christmas. $50.00 was also donated to the Book Fund.

On May 6th [?] the Family accompanied by the Board and members of the Staff enjoyed a beautiful tea at the Home of Mrs. Krusi. The usual lovely parties were held at the Home on Thanksgiving and Christmas with delicious dinners being provided by Ben, and all sorts of special delicacies sent in by the Board members.

The Convalescent Committee, under the able leadership of Mrs. Bowes [?], continued to evaluate the work done in this department, and came to the conclusion that the type of need is changing from the quick turnover of two weeks care formerly given, to care of chronic cases with a stay of from one to four months. During the year 136 patients were admitted, 140 discharged. 726 free bed days given. There were 4,633 convalescent days, and 10,735 patient days which includes both convalescent as well as Family care.

The Allyne bed was used 47 days this year. (Seven days for Mrs. Flashman [?]; 21 days for Mrs. de Mari [?]; and 19 days in 1954 for Mrs. Lerox [?], or 36 days total for Mrs. Lerox [?].)
