[Transcribed from handwriting by Claude, 2026-10. Spelling and punctuation as written; [?] = uncertain reading, [illegible] = could not read.]

Mrs Krick. Mrs Clough and Miss Allyne, selected and presented to her a lovely jade ring from the Board, which gave everyone great pleasure.

Mrs Krick obtained the renewal of our license to operate for another year.

Since "Ben", the Chinese cook, was taken by the army, a procession of cooks, as also other servants, has been incessant. All the salaries were substantially increased.

Many gifts were received. Several cheques came from Mr Ronald Rolph, in memory of friends. Mrs Hindes's cheques came for the "Follow-up Fund". Mrs Hicks, a grateful ex-convalescent, sent $100.00, saved from her ship-yard salary for the "Follow-up Fund".

Mrs Saffron's son sent a cheque for $50.00, Mrs Arata and Miss Wiggins, both patients, sent $20.00, each and Mr Haile, a patient's husband, sent a cheque for $25.00, and a load of bronze crysanthemums at Thanksgiving.

Mrs Kirkwood and Mr Robert Kirkwood gave 400 pounds of prunes which Mr and Mrs Krick brought to the Home from Saratoga.

Miss Maude Wellendorff gave a six piece plated silver tea-set.

Mrs Prosser gave a quarter of a ton of coal, for which Miss Allyne lent a grate. Candy was sent by Mrs Stuart Smith and Mrs Krick at Thanksgiving and Christmas. Miss Woods sent a subscription to the National Geographic magazine. Mrs Hindes sent plants for the garden, and a large striped bass was given by a friend of Miss Liston's.

                        Respectfully submitted,
                        Margaret James Porter, Secretary.
