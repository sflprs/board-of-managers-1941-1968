The One hundred and first annual report of The San Francisco Ladies Protection and Relief Society from February 1954 to February 1955.

Nineteen fifty four sees another successful year closed for the Ladies Protection and Relief Society.

Our Treasurer, Miss Zane, was granted a leave of absence in June, and was succeeded by Mrs. Hilbert. The books for the year closed showing a saving on income of $2,973.00, and a saving on expenses of $3,771.49. As of December 1954 the bank balance was $17,443.14 due to the good management of the Board of Managers and the Staff, and under the budget chairmanship of Mrs. Krusi and Miss Ralph.

Ten dollar raises of salary were granted the two Chinese kitchen boys and Ben; and Miss Hamilton, of the nursing staff, was raised fifteen dollars as of January 1955.

Miss Ralph reported that the Follow up Fund continued to grow, now having $1644.99 in savings, and $463.64 in the commercial account.

The Family Committee, under the untiring leader-ship of Mrs. Krick called on the ladies throughout the year, and for the most part the Family remained well.

A happy note was struck in August when Mrs. Cobban [?] was married to Mr. Follansbee and left the Home after three years' residence here.

In November Mrs. Biggs passed away quietly after four years of loving devotion from our nursing staff.

In December Miss Hutchins died and her lovely presence will be missed by both the Family and the Board.

In June Mrs. Ragna [?] Hall was elected to membership in the Home having fitted in so well with the Family. And in October Mrs. Mary Lerox [?] moved to the Home to fill the vacancy left by Mrs. Cobban [?]. Thus the Family continues to be a happy and well integrated group.
