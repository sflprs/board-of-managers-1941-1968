The Convalescent Department. Mrs. Miller, Chairman

The number of patients admitted for the year was one hundred and forty-four, discharged one hundred and forty-three, and cared for one hundred and fifty-five, with five hundred and eight free days.

This year the majority of cases in the depart-ment have been adults recovering from strokes, cases of acute exhaustion, and "polio". There have been some cast cases. The "polio" cases have gone daily to the May T. Morrison Rehabilitation Center. The Allyne bed at Children's Hospital has been used for twenty-three days for a Convalescent patient, Miss Viola Estrem [?].

Construction and Repair Department. Mrs. Perry, Chairman

The building is in excellent condition. During the year the following rooms have been painted: the kitchen, the dining room, the lounge adjoin-ing, the large bathroom, six small bathrooms, telephone booths on the third floor, the Anna Beaver room, and such rooms in the "Family" and Convalescent departments as have needed it. The beds in the Anna Beaver room have been surfaced with lacquer for a permanent finish.

A double stainless steel sink has replaced the old sink in the first floor pantry, and new linoleum has been laid there and in the kitchen.

The diet kitchen has been greatly improved.
