Ninety-third annual report of the San Francisco Ladies' Protection and Relief Society, from February 1946 to February 194[last digit cut off at the right edge of the photo]

The Society held eleven regular meetings, and ten mid-month meetings, omitting both the August meeting, and the mid-July meeting. The President, Mrs Krick, presided. The same officers were elected to serve for 1946.

The "Family" Committee, Mrs Stuart Smith, chairman. The "Family" numbered 17, at the beginning, and 15, at the end of the year. The household had kept fairly well, except for colds, and troubles like Miss Barlow's eyes, and Mrs Kelly's depressed condition. In May Mrs Hoyt became a permanent member of the Home. In September, she slipped and fell and fractured her shoulder. She made a good recovery and was very grateful for the care given her. Only two ladies went on vacations, Mrs Hale, to visit her daughter, and Miss Hanchins [?].

Mrs Fox celebrated her 99th birthday with a small party. She has been well enough to take short automobile rides, which she enjoys. Mrs Pierson had often taken Mrs James for drives.

Mrs Rose had her 93rd birthday, and Miss Barlow her 90th, when she entertained members of the "Family" at tea.

In October, Miss Beans became a member of the "Family" Committee, as she visits the ladies devotedly, whenever she is in town.

In November, Mrs Richardson had a stroke, and was under the care of day and night nurses. She became physically better, but mentally weaker, so that she had to be removed from the Home, and was placed in a Cubicle in the Psychiatric ward of the City and County Hospital, as no private Hospital would take the case. She had good care, and, in January was taken to Napa. Mrs Stuart Smith, Miss Ash, and Miss Mathews installed her, visited her, later, and have followed [c]losely to see that conditions were as favourable, as [p]ossible. They are satisfied, and will continue to [v]isit her. Mrs Richardson is happy, and thinks that [s]he is away on a visit. Her friend, Mrs Jones, also, [w]ill go to see her every week.
