Our Board of Trustees finally presented a plan for the sale of the Pine Street property and with the available funds to build a wing on to 3400 Laguna to house 85 to 100 guests and the Crocker life care members. Mr. Charles Warren Perry was retained as architect and Mrs. Craig was appointed chairman of the Building Committee. Many reports and discussions have since been held, and it is hoped that a satisfactory plan can be devised so that by expanding we may meet the challange of the times.

During the year the Board lost by resignation Mrs. Pierson [?] and Mrs. Allen; the loss of their invaluable work and interest will be keenly felt.

This report would be incomplete without a word of appreciation for the leadership and devotion to the work of the organization of our President, Mrs. Warren Perry; as well as the cooperation and unswerving interest of Miss Ash and the Staff who make 3400 Laguna a Home in the true sense of the word.

        Respectfully submitted
                        Kate Boardman Nye - recording secretary.
