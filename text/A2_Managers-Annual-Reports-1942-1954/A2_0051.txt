A double sink with a tiled counter has been installed and shelves have been built below the stove and sink area. The work counters have been lengthened and they and the shelves have been covered with linoleum and edged in stainless steel.

The flag pole has been repaired and painted.

All of these improvements have been made with-in the amount allocated for this work.

At present engineers are working to improve the heating system.

The Home Furnishing Committee.

A beautiful Victorian chair was purchased for the living room and re-upholstered. A chaise longue and several chairs stored in the basement have been recovered. A book case has been purchased for Miss Betke's room. Chinese peel furniture has replaced the old furniture in the first floor sun room.

The Garden Committee. Mrs. Landis, Chairman.

The two rose gardens, planted in January, have bloomed profusely. There have been cut flowers or blossoming shrubs available for decoration most of the year. Henry has kept the garden in good order and has required help on only one occasion: when several trees blew down and had to be cut up. The garden sun-house has been in use most of the year and its gay furniture lends color to the garden.

During the year the following have been elect-ed to the Board of Managers: Mrs. James H. Allen, Mrs. Edward Hohfeld, Mrs. Ellen Krusi, Miss Margaret Lane [?].

The Trustees elected the following officers in March: Mr. Joseph A. Moore, Jr. was re-elected President, Mr. John E. Cushing was re-elected Vice-President, Mr. James H. Hutchinson was elected Secretary, and Mr. Edwin V. Krick was elected Treasurer.

It has been decided to defer publishing the History of the Society, written by Miss Ramona Beans, until the hundreth anniversary.
