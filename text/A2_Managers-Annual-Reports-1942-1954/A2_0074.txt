evening of pictures was held in August; and the Marina Professional Womens Club gave a party before Christmas. Mrs. Nye gave a tea at her home honoring the Family, and Mrs. Bruck [?] entertained at a delightful day in Ross. Of course Thanksgiving, Christmas, and Easter were celebrated with marvelous dinners, decorations, and entertainment as usual, and always so thoroughly enjoyed by the Family, Convalescents, and friends. And last but not least was the important day of the Bazaar culminating hours of loving planning and preparation by Mrs. Byrnes and her committee, plus the amazing spirit and interest of Family, Convalescents and Staff who literally start working for next year the week after the Bazaar.

During the year thanks to Mrs. Towne and her committee, the Home and apartments have been well kept up: painting done, boiler repainted [?], linoleum laid, side walk and fence fixed, chairs recovered; rugs, TV, and some new furniture bought. And the garden was always a joy to behold due to Mrs. Hilbert's plans.

Future policy meetings were held by Mrs. Miller [?] and her committee comprised of members of both Boards; and the By-Laws were rewritten with the help again of the two Boards.

Gifts and donations were received during the year too numerous to list here, but all of which have been graciously acknowledged by Mrs. Stephenson.

The Follow up Fund and Special Fund have been judiciously used as Miss Ralph and her committee deemed best varying from buying shoes, medicine, teeth, glasses and permanents, to paying hospital bills, and outright cash gifts.

As always the minutes were full of reports from Miss Ash, and references to the myriad of extras done by Miss Ash to really make this a Home and not an institution.

And no annual report should close without tribute being paid to the wonderful leadership of our President, Mrs. Perry.

        Respectfully submitted
                        Kate Boardman Nye
