Mrs. Craig as Chairman of Home Furnishings and Mrs. Towne, Chairman of Construction and Repairs, have kept the Home in beautiful condition. New linoleum has been put down in two rooms; one room was completely refurnished; several chairs recovered; a new chest of [?] drawers [?] bought; three lamps added; the buzzer system completed. Tiles for the roof were replaced; a cement sidewalk repaired as well as part of the fence; and shingles added on the tool [?] house. A new type writer was bought for the office; a refrigerator for the diet kitchen; a washer and drier for the third floor. In the Convalescent Department a lift-tilt [?] machine was added; a modern up + down hospital bed; two foam rubber mattresses and back rests. In July the Plymouth was sold and a new Chevrolet bought which has been thoroughly enjoyed by many members of the Family on drives and picnics as organized by Miss Ash. To further insure safety five life guard inner tubes were bought for the car.

Mrs. Krick continues to enlarge and bring up to date the Library by careful purchases and numerous gifts of books from Board members.
