Miss Ash was given a leave of absence of three months to take care of her brother who was seriously ill at his home at Palo Alto.

Miss Ash's brother died, in June.

It was voted, in July, to give Miss Ash a month away, for a complete rest. Miss Moore was to have an extra week's vacation.

The police commission for parking permitted two metal signs, marking the white zone, and two other removable signs for the green zone, with cards permitting parking, there.

Five hundred correct lists of the members of the Board, and the Trustees, were printed, to paste into the little green brochures.

As of January 1st, 1949, the salaries of the nurses were raised. Miss Lister [?] was to work in the office, full time, instead of part time, at a higher salary.

A bequest of $5,298 [?] was received from the estate of Mrs Halsey Smith.

The Trustees had made a remarkable investment of $300,000, waiting in government bonds for such an opportunity. They bought a building in Reno and leased it to Lerner's dress shop, for fifty years, at 5½% interest, nett.

The budget estimate for 1949 proposed a $350.00 monthly increase, over last year, making the allowance $5,063.75.

There were eight recommendations from the Policy Committee. [struck through: All] of them were ratified and carried out.
