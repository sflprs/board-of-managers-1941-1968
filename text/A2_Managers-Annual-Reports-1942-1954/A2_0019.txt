The House Building Committee, Miss Allyne, chairman. The main event was the painting of the entire inside of the house. This took 266 days of labor, at a cost of $5,901.88. All the mattresses were renovated, and the rugs cleaned. There were minor repairs, such as repairing the refrigerator, and the stove. A new washing-machine was purchased. Mr Ferguson made screens for Miss Ash's and Miss Liston's rooms also repaired the arbor, opposite the kitchen court, and cut down some dead trees.

In August, Miss Allyne cleared, sorted and arranged all the materials, in the garden house, also had the garbage area cleared, and a new hedge planted. We are most grateful to her. Miss Allyne was given a vote of thanks for all the time she gave in overseeing the painting.

The House Furnishing Committee. Chairman, Mrs Towne, and later, Mrs Fairlie. Mrs Towne purchased, from the wholesale house, 18 dozen sheets, 18 dozen pillow-cases, 18 dozen bath towels, 18 dozen face towels, and 8 pairs of blankets.
